## Novelty result

The dated primary-source audit is **UNRESOLVED / PARTIAL_COLLISION**. The
elementary T1–T4 forms are not new mathematics merely because they are now
formalized. Active risk comparison and selective-label pretrained-model choice
have especially close precedents: [Sawade et al., 2012](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2012/hash/92fb0c6d1758261f10d052e6e2c1123c-Abstract.html)
and [Karimi et al., 2021](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v130/reza-karimi21a.html).
The audit also verifies the requested ICLR 2026 and UAI 2026 publication records
and distinguishes those records from the exact arXiv versions used for detailed
theorem comparisons. Final-version theorem parity remains an open review item.

This is neither a favorable novelty certificate nor a whole-program kill. A
substantive remaining target would need an actual attainable result for the
shared categorical-label experiment under justified side information, beyond
renaming a robust LP, rank calculation, or established active-risk comparison.

## Remaining gates

1. Formally prove the properly scoped identification/supplement results, while
   distinguishing established supporting mathematics from any new contribution.
2. Replace T5 with a genuine feasible sign-crossing/commitment statement and
   prove the corresponding probabilistic lower bound.
3. Lock a nontrivial commitment task and feedback contract before implementing
   T6–T9. Include delays, requested/returned/pending labels, arrivals, stopping,
   candidate comparisons, and simultaneous confidence control.
4. Establish and compare actual passive/active upper and lower bounds under the
   same family, margin, cost scaling, and success criterion. Rare-stratum label
   savings cannot be relabeled as waiting-time savings.
5. Complete the remaining statistical/resource mutations and synthetic protocol;
   justify observable restrictions and application costs scientifically.
6. Pass fresh-checkout verification and a favorable claim-level novelty decision.
   The design's standalone-paper and natural-experiment gates are not open.
7. Independently close the existing K-Bound source-freeze/release workflow before
   any proposed integration. This track does not resolve its Git, cloud-file,
   clean-commit, or full-release verification boundaries.

None of the design's three program success levels is established yet. The
appropriate description is **a verified initial algebra/guard slice plus exact
negative findings and an unresolved research direction**.
