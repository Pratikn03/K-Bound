# K-Bound Manuscript Strategy

## Authoritative draft

`kbound_short.tex` and `kbound_short_appendix.tex` are the claim-controlled
manuscript. They compile to a 20-page IEEE draft; the synchronized Word version
is 37 pages because it uses a single-column editing layout.

The 59-page `kbound.tex` is historical. Its abstract and body still contain
superseded sign-identifiability wording, a universal seven-source gate claim,
zero-budget mappings of prior work, and numerical claims that are not in the
canonical result manifest. It must not be submitted or copied wholesale.

## Balanced conference core

Keep these elements in the main paper:

1. Adapt/freeze/abstain problem and validity boundary.
2. Interior impossibility, boundary ambiguity, strict-commitment frontier, and
   marginal interval certificate.
3. The explicit population-versus-empirical bridge.
4. KGA architecture, evidence schema, calibration, and rollback semantics.
5. Controlled CIFAR-10-C and ImageNet-C beats-both evidence.
6. Natural no-harm, weak-evidence, and negative results.
7. Concise limitations, formalization scope, and artifact lineage.

For an 8-10 page venue, move detailed natural per-dataset narratives, full
ablation tables, formalization inventory, and camera templates to the
supplement. Keep one consolidated empirical table and three figures in the
core: decision flow, population/empirical certificate distinction, and regime
geometry.

## Supplement only

The supplement may contain complete core proofs, multiclass/regression
derivations, evidence formulas, adapter configurations, additional benchmark
tables, protocol reconciliation, runtime details, Lean inventory, camera
protocol, and the claim-to-artifact map.

Do not restore one-bit, minimax, martingale, weakest-class, historical ELARA, or
large diagnostic ladders until each item has a complete proof, current claim
wording, and a direct reproducibility role.

## Submission gate

The current short draft is a complete faculty/reviewer draft, not a final
camera-ready submission. Venue-specific trimming, author/anonymity settings,
bibliographic style, and final experiment locks remain. Fresh physical-camera
evidence is optional strengthening; if incomplete, retain it only as an
outcome-free preregistration in the supplement.
