\subsection{What the constructions add}
The binary result fixes a predictor pair and a label-free evidence law, then
varies the conditional label kernel within an explicit residual budget. Its
additional content is the attainable clipped benefit set, the strict-sign
boundary including zero-benefit boundary worlds, and the corresponding
fibre-wise audit obstruction. Generic action identification and
indistinguishable-world testing are established ideas; adaptation identification is
formulated directly on evidence fibres in \cite{jhawar2026identifiable}.
Our necessity statement concerns the full declared rich
class. Restricting that class can remove counterworlds, so necessity need not
survive; sufficiency remains valid. Zero disagreement instead gives zero
benefit, without defining disagreement-conditional moments.

The paired-transport construction addresses a different estimand and
assumption class. Relative to the matrix-constraint confidence regions in
\cite{li2026matrixconstraints}, it retains a shared joint table
for two predictors and optimizes their accuracy contrast under a supplied
conditional-transport budget. Tighter bounds than separate subtraction follow
on that same feasible set; no dominance over another method's feasible set is
implied. The probability-box theorem, numerical binomial endpoints and
floating-point objective bounds have the distinct scopes described in
\KBSuppRef{app:nextphase-paired-proof}{Appendix}{the paired-transport supplement}.
The numerical endpoints are not certified inverse-CDF bounds.

KGA uses historical labeled cell outcomes and is a separate empirical
construction. Its interval-to-action implication applies inherited calibration
ideas; it does not establish a new conformal validity theorem or estimate the
population residual budget. Coverage transfer requires its own assumptions.
The analysis in \cite{barber2023beyond} quantifies coverage loss beyond
exchangeability, including for original conformal procedures, and studies
modifications that can improve robustness. Learned adaptation selection also
predates this work. MORPHEUS~\cite{danilowski2026morpheus} selects before candidate
execution, whereas KGA's shadow-candidate features incur candidate computation.
A comparison must match both information access and cost.

The theoretical guarantees concern prespecified decisions and sampling
contracts. Marginal wrong-direction control does not imply an error bound
conditional on ADAPT. Candidates selected using evaluation evidence, repeated
uses and adaptive searches require additional control. The maintained
formalization's scope and exclusions are given in the formal supplement; its
check count is not a measure of originality.

\subsection{Units, utility and sensitivity}
The principal CIFAR panel and the shared-predictor study answer different
questions. Table~\ref{tab:journal-principal-means} reconstructs equally weighted
mean accuracies and ordinary regret for the principal policy. The KGA minus
always-adapt differences are $+0.6183$, $+0.1673$ and $-0.1326$ percentage points
for Tent, EATA and SAR, respectively. The five stream seeds reuse an archived
source checkpoint and are not five independent training replications.

In the shared-predictor comparison, $100L_5$ assigns weight five to harmful
accepted updates. That declared benchmark loss is separate from accuracy,
ordinary regret and interval inclusion. Scaled calibration's observed Tent
advantage is smaller after matching ADAPT exposure, and depends on the
corruption mix (Table~\ref{tab:journal-tent-sensitivity}). Paired resampling of
six corruption types gives wide descriptive ranges for the unmatched point
and margin comparisons. Removing JPEG reduces the advantage over point
prediction to $0.004889$ percentage points and reverses the margin comparison.
This deletion is an influence diagnostic, not a revised primary analysis.
Within a fixed scoring fold, a constant finite radius preserves the shared point
predictor's ordering. The equal-exposure check matches selected cells within that
fold using the same tie rule; it does not establish a common ranking across
separately fitted folds or studies. Changing exposure alone is not evidence of
improved ranking. All candidate,
cohort and rule rows, including harmful acceptance, missed helpful
opportunities, abstention and harm-weight sensitivity, are available in
the accompanying versioned synthesis.

Table~\ref{tab:journal-inclusion-units} separates cell inclusion from the event
that every cell in a complete semantic group is covered. There are 2,160 cells
and 216 complete groups per CIFAR candidate, built from six corruption types;
neither count is an independent-deployment sample size. A group-max radius
does not make its observed cell inclusion a simultaneous-group coverage rate.
All four observed rates remain descriptive evidence of transfer failure.
Unavailable finite-rank and zero-exposure quantities remain undefined rather
than being reported as successful conditional-risk control.

CCT-20's reconstructed equal-cell mean accuracy is $52.2936\%$ for both KGA
and freeze, versus $34.1034\%$ for adaptation. Its zero ADAPT exposure supports
retention, not useful routing. Secondary CCT macro-F1 uses the preserved
16-indicator class universe and a zero-denominator value of zero, computed per
cell from class counts. It is not interchangeable with official single-label
macro-F1 or with F1 recomputed after pooling cells. The invalid historical
iWildCam macro-F1 comparison remains withheld.

\subsection{Scope of independent confirmation}
No eligible fresh natural-shift study was established by the bounded metadata
review. Previously opened environments cannot become fresh by changing a
seed. DomainNet's six domains are insufficient even for the minimum finite
90\% environment-level split-conformal rank before reserving development and
target units; nine calibration units would only satisfy the rank condition,
not establish exchangeability or adequate precision. So2Sat's failed locked
screen remains binding and its target remains unscored. These limitations
leave useful independent natural-shift routing unresolved. The accompanying
eligibility report specifies the population, access-history, sample-size,
protocol and cost requirements for a future study.
