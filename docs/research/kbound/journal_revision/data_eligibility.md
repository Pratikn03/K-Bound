## Why launch remains blocked

Its target remains closed. The old preparation log’s zero calibration reads predates execution; it must not be used to claim that calibration is still unopened. DomainNet has six named domains. Clipart/painting are opened, and real/sketch/infograph/quickdraw retain UNKNOWN access status. The exact finite split-conformal rank at 90% needs at least nine calibration units: at six, the requested rank is seven and the ordinary radius is infinite. Reserving development and target domains reduces the available count further. Repeated streams do not increase the number of domains. An episode-population claim would require a separate justified sampling design.

Camelyon17, iWildCam, RxRx1, FMoW, Office-Home and PACS have opened-target provenance records. CCT-20 is completed and already evaluated. PovertyMap preserves its failed development screen and the historical statement that held-out scoring did not run; that statement alone is not a fresh current custody seal. A new seed or a different image split within an opened environment does not create an untouched environment.

CLEAR and Dollar Street remain candidates for a future metadata/design stage, not launch-ready studies. No local population/access/checkpoint seal was established in the named roots. CLEAR’s ordered buckets need a temporal dependence argument. Dollar Street’s country count does not establish the number of eligible independent household/country units.
