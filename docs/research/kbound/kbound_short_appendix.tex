\section{Calibration and held-out evaluation}\label{app:calib-eval}
Algorithm~\ref{alg:calib-eval} gives the shared evaluation protocol behind the uniform
nine-track panel (Table~\ref{tab:uniform-panel}) and the primary numeric evidence
(Table~\ref{tab:primary-numeric}).

\begin{algorithm}[t]
\caption{Calibration and held-out evaluation for K-Bound}
\label{alg:calib-eval}
\begin{algorithmic}[1]
\Require Conditions $\mathcal{C}$; frozen $f_0$; adapters $\mathcal{A}_1,\ldots,\mathcal{A}_K$;
  calibration split $\mathcal{C}_{\mathrm{cal}}$; test split $\mathcal{C}_{\mathrm{test}}$; level $\alpha$
\Ensure Regret-to-oracle, false-adapt rate, coverage, beats-both verdict
\ForAll{$c \in \mathcal{C}_{\mathrm{cal}}$}
  \ForAll{adapters $\mathcal{A}_k$}
    \State $f_a^{(k,c)} \leftarrow \mathcal{A}_k(f_0, X_c)$
    \State $Z_{k,c} \leftarrow \phi(X_c, f_0, f_a^{(k,c)})$
    \State $\Delta_{k,c} \leftarrow R_c(f_0) - R_c(f_a^{(k,c)})$
  \EndFor
\EndFor
\State \textbf{Calibrate without test leakage:} on stress grids, form leave-one-cell-out
  residuals $\rho_i=|\widehat\Delta_{-i}(Z_i)-\Delta_i|$; on semantic shifts, reserve a
  development calibration split disjoint from the declared test split
\State $\varepsilon \leftarrow (1-\alpha)$ empirical quantile of the calibration residuals
\State Fit deployment estimator $\widehat\Delta(Z)$ on \emph{all} calibration pairs \Comment{scores the held-out test split}
\ForAll{$c \in \mathcal{C}_{\mathrm{test}}$}
  \ForAll{adapters $\mathcal{A}_k$}
    \State Run Algorithm~\ref{alg:kga} with $(f_0,\mathcal{A}_k,X_c,\widehat\Delta,\varepsilon)$
    \State Record decision, regret-to-oracle, coverage, false-adapt indicator
  \EndFor
\EndFor
\State Aggregate metrics vs.\ always-adapt, always-freeze, per-condition oracle
\end{algorithmic}
\end{algorithm}

\section{Evidence feature schema and adapter hyperparameters}\label{app:evidence-schema}
Two label-free evidence panels are used, matched to the two calibration regimes of
\S\ref{sec:method}. Notation: $p_0,p_a$ are the frozen and adapted softmax rows on the adaptation
batch; $\bar m_0=\mathrm{mean}_n\,p_{0,n}$ and $\bar m_a$ are the predicted-class marginals;
$H(p)=-\sum_c p_c\log p_c$; $C$ is the number of classes. The stress-grid tracks (CIFAR-10-C,
ImageNet-C, PACS) use the $11$-dimensional base panel of Table~\ref{tab:schema-base}
(\texttt{kbound\_pkg/kbound/evidence.py}). The natural-shift tracks (Camelyon17, iWildCam,
Office-Home, RxRx1) use the $16$-dimensional panel of Table~\ref{tab:schema-rich}: a $10$-statistic
frozen-logit summary plus a $6$-feature rich panel (\texttt{scripts/run\_wilds\_camelyon17.py}). All
features are target-label-free; the ATC feature calibrates its threshold on \emph{source} labels
only.

\subsection{Excluded wins and regime boundaries}\label{sec:excluded-wins}
Several benchmarks flag beats-both under alternate routes or adapters but do \emph{not}
qualify as headline natural-shift wins under the Protocol~G/H/M v2 bar (canonical
\textsc{KGA}, dev-locked adapter from pre-registered panel, in-domain calibration,
held-out test, false-adapt $\le\alpha$).

\paragraph{Superseded protocols.}
iWildCam Protocol~H v1 (fixed \texttt{sar\_online}) and OfficeHome Protocol~M v1
($14$-candidate win-finder) are audit-only; headline claims use H/M v2.

\paragraph{Office-Home (deployed adapter).}
The source-selected deployed adapter (mild online EATA) is \emph{helpful-dominated} on target
test ($0\%$ harmful cells; always-adapt is oracle). \textsc{KGA} has $0\%$ false-adapts but
does not beat both (regret $0.015$ vs always-adapt $0$). An aggressive online SAR arm can beat
both on the same test split, but that arm was not the deployed adapter; reporting it as a win
would be adapter shopping. The multicandidate deploy route abstains on $100\%$ of target-test
cells (certificate transfer-AUC $\approx 0.49$). Protocol~M v2 (main text) uses a separate
dev-lock panel and held-out target-test scorer.

\paragraph{ImageNet-R.}
The diverse-backbone panel is helpful-leaning (mean $\bar B{=}{+}0.017$, harmful rate
$13.9\%$, best harm-AUC $0.662$). The multicandidate route abstains on all $48$ conditions;
single-candidate \textsc{KGA} often ties always-adapt (oracle) on helpful backbones. This is
the weak-detectability / unknowable regime predicted by Corollary~\ref{cor:forced-abstain}.

\paragraph{iWildCam intermediate domains.}
On id\_val, a multicandidate router can flag beats-both on subsets, but pooled false-adapt
on committed adapts is $\approx 78\%\gg\alpha$---disqualified. Headline claim: Protocol~H v2 on
held-out test only (Table~\ref{tab:iwildcam-H}).

\paragraph{Camelyon17 four-seed hospital slice.}
The original four held-out hospital seeds are helpful-dominated ($0\%$ harmful); \textsc{KGA}
beats always-freeze but not always-adapt (Table~\ref{tab:camelyon}). Protocol~G uses a
composition-stress grid with rich evidence where harmful cells are mixed ($30\%$) and
detectable---a different regime on the same benchmark family.

\paragraph{CIFAR-10.1.}
Within each seed, Tent/EATA abstain heavily with $0\%$ false-adapts and tie always-freeze.
Cross-seed calibration (fit on seeds $0$--$2$, test on $3$--$4$) does \emph{not} clear the
headline bar (false-adapt $44\%$); low-margin benefits do not transfer across independent stream
seeds. Report CIFAR-10.1 as a within-seed safety probe, not a fourth natural headline win.

\subsection{Controlled multimodal corruption (Protocol D33)}
Pre-registered MNIST two-view fusion ($130$ conditions; one modality corrupted across
$13$ severities). When fusion flips from helpful to harmful, \textsc{KGA} routes correctly:
mean accuracy $0.857$ vs.\ always-fuse $0.583$ and always-single-A $0.854$ (both gaps
$P{=}1.0$), false-adapt $0/9$ commits ($\le\alpha$). This is a \emph{controlled} mechanism
confirmation---not a natural benchmark---but shows the certificate dominates both trivial
policies when detectable multimodal harm is present
(\texttt{CONTROLLED\_MULTIMODAL\_PROTOCOL\_D33\_v1}).

overpays; SAR is mostly helpful ($5\%$ harmful). Cross-seed transfer of the certificate
(Protocol~K) does not meet the headline bar; see \S\ref{sec:excluded-wins}.

deliberately excluded from this short-paper build. Their raw logs remain in the repository for
