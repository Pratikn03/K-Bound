A breakthrough or “9+” rating would exceed this evidence. Passing software checks cannot supply the missing scientific results.

**What the numerical evidence says.** The calibration comparison's loss is mean helpful benefit forgone plus five times accepted negative-benefit magnitude. Lower is better, and the following values are percentage points of this weighted loss, not percentage gains in model accuracy. On CIFAR Tent, scaled calibration gives **0.1429**, versus **0.2867** for point prediction and **0.2264** for a tune-selected margin. Against point ranking at the same exposure, the comparison narrows to **0.1429 versus 0.1610**. Simple margins do better on CIFAR EATA/SAR; point or margin policies do better in the other reported cohorts. Nominal 90% constant-cell interval inclusion falls to **50.51–60.69%** on held-out CIFAR corruptions. These are retrospective results on opened, related records, not prospective coverage guarantees. [Calibration report](calibration_value_results.md), [final numerical authority (evidence ZIP)](https://github.com/Pratikn03/K-Bound/releases/download/kbound-2026-09-22/next_phase_evidence.zip), member `output/next_phase/calibration_value_v1/FINAL_RESULTS_SUMMARY.json`.

The group-risk report corrects an important interpretation gap. For the 20% risk-selection sensitivity, held-out conditional group errors are **8/107** for EATA, **19/180** for SAR, and **0/65** for Tent, while corresponding cell rates are **1.08%, 1.59%, and 0%**. The 10% arm exposes no groups, making its conditional error undefined. The binomial population interpretation requires an appropriate common-risk IID group model, which these historical records do not establish. [Group-risk authority (evidence ZIP)](https://github.com/Pratikn03/K-Bound/releases/download/kbound-2026-09-22/next_phase_evidence.zip), member `output/next_phase/calibration_value_v1/group_risk_report_05/heldout_semantic_group_risk.json`.

The paired-transport study completed **4,860 synthetic trial configurations**. In the selected cancellation setting, joint optimization adapts in **60/60** helpful trials while subtracting separate accuracy extrema on the same polytope abstains in **60/60**. An assumption-violating world with identical observed count streams changes true benefit from **+0.16 to −0.50**, and all 60 accepted updates become harmful. This is useful evidence for the construction's conditional decision value and for the necessity of its assumptions. It is not a comparison against an executed official MaC-LP implementation or evidence of natural-shift utility. **Comparative originality.** The strongest specific theoretical claim is the exact compatible-law analysis in its declared binary residual class, including the clipped paired-benefit set and residual-audit floor. Its importance depends on whether that class and its externally justified bound are useful in practice. The distinction between identifying a decision and identifying an entire mechanism overlaps [Jhawar and Wang](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11235). Probability intervals, matrix constraints, and LP extraction overlap [Li, Han, and Ma](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.14802). Historical feature regressors for selecting adaptation overlap [MORPHEUS](https://theyoungkwon.github.io/papers/articles/danilowski_morpheus_iclr-ttu26.pdf). The paired estimand, dependence-preserving construction, explicit transport sensitivity, and integrated negative diagnostics are defensible points of differentiation; they do not establish broad conceptual priority or superiority over those methods.

The practical calibration hypothesis remains partly unsuccessful: a confidence interval should earn its complexity by improving decisions at a meaningful harm cost. The current study finds one favorable operating point and substantial transfer failures. Treating those failures as findings gives the paper more scientific value than describing all abstention as success. The local software contributes reproducibility and auditable failure handling; these are engineering strengths, not independent evidence of a new statistical principle.

**Academic level and readiness.** This is substantive graduate-level research: it combines conditional mathematical results, implementation, empirical comparisons, explicit negative controls, and source-bound evidence. A defensible manuscript can present it as a carefully scoped study of when adaptation decisions are identifiable and when empirical calibration fails. The current evidence is weaker for a paper whose central promise is broadly useful, safe adaptation in deployment. A top-tier or breakthrough claim would need a clearer advantage over closest theory, convincing native-method comparisons where feasible, and repeated independent demonstrations of useful target routing. Venue acceptance cannot be inferred from this audit.

The theory and empirical companion must remain separate. The binary frontier depends on its residual class; the multiclass LP depends on a justified transport budget and independent evidence sampling; KGA's empirical interval relies on its own calibration/transfer assumptions. Neither theorem validates the other's empirical assumptions. Numerical dual bounds apply to the supplied floating-point LP; approximate primal checks do not certify exact nonemptiness, and inverse-beta quantiles are not formally certified inverse-CDF calculations. The extension is not automatically covered by the historical Lean proof suite.

**Real-world worth now.** The demonstrated value is a research and engineering reference for recording evidence identities, enforcing a conservative fallback when specified checks fail, comparing routing policies, and making unsupported assumptions visible. It can support an instrumented local evaluation or shadow trial. It is not yet an autonomous production adaptation system with demonstrated net benefit or a validated safety guarantee.

The final lifecycle fixture run measured **1,000 requests**, with **0.1136 ms median** and **0.1842 ms p95** overhead; **1,027** journal records including warmups and fault cases passed complete chain verification. These are byte-fixture costs. The Docker API measured **100 sequential** and **100 requests at concurrency four**, with medians **2.3991 ms** and **6.0037 ms** respectively. Its 54 code/lock-file hashes matched the local source at verification. The API consumes supplied certificates; it does not execute neural candidates or automatically integrate the new lifecycle. Local production mode and a launched loopback service are concrete operational progress, but provide no external field utility, uptime, or service-level guarantee. Account explicitly for candidate selection, repeated decisions, evolving baselines, and calibration reuse; a single-invocation bound is insufficient.
5. Recover missing historical execution evidence or keep those results descriptive. Add independent executors, model families, sites, and time periods; preserve failures and uncertainty at those units rather than multiplying cell-level counts.
6. Use the completed neural cost profile to design an actual neural candidate lifecycle integrated with serving, and test operational maintenance and recovery over time. Document resource limits, total adaptation/inference cost, and benefit after costs. Physical-camera deployment remains deferred.
7. Track source-bound verification, document inspection, source seal, checksums, and reviewer-archive acceptance in the separate generated release receipts. This source-stage assessment does not certify those later checks; their completion establishes release integrity, not scientific closure of the preceding items.

No aggregate completion percentage is assigned: finishing a validator, proving a conditional theorem, and obtaining a successful independent deployment are different achievements. The remaining scientific objectives require new evidence and can legitimately fail; they cannot be completed by editing the manuscript or increasing the desired rating.
