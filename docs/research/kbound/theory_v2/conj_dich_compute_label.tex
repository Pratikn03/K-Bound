\begin{conjecture}[Frontier margin computability; closed as dichotomy in Wave~4]
\label{conj:dich-compute}
If $\Delta(\vartheta,\eta)=\int_D(1-2\eta)c_{gf}\,\mathrm dQ_\vartheta$ with
$\vartheta\mapsto Q_\vartheta$ continuous, is the frontier margin $m(O)$ always a computable
functional of $Q_X$? Wave~4 (Theorem~\ref{thm:margin-compute-dichotomy}) closes this as a
\textbf{dichotomy}: atom on $\operatorname{disc}(c_{gf})$ may make $m$ non-computable; atomless
transversal crossing makes $m$ computable.
\end{conjecture}
