\section{Impossibility of a single scalar multiclass capacity}\label{sec:mc-cap-impossibility}

The multiclass capacity section (\S\ref{sec:mc-capacity}) establishes a \emph{partial} positive
theory under hypotheses (M1)--(M3) and two necessity results. This section closes the
\emph{open core}: there is no single computable scalar functional of label-free observables that
certifies $\sign\Delta$ in the general multi-conflict-pair / vector-concept regime.

\begin{theorem}[No universal scalar multiclass capacity]\label{thm:mc-cap-impossibility}
Fix $K\ge3$. In the vector-concept regime on $D$ (multiple conflict pairs, residual mass
splitting across $K{-}2$ classes), suppose a label-free observable $O$ (a functional of $Q_X$
and deployment scores only) is proposed as a \emph{scalar capacity} $K(O)$ with the property:
\emph{whenever} $K(O)>\beta$, the benefit sign $\sign\Delta$ is identified label-free. Then
$K$ \textbf{fails}: there exist admissible worlds with $K(O)>\beta$ in \emph{both} worlds yet
$\sign\Delta$ differs (Theorem~\ref{thm:mc-thirdclass}), and there exist admissible worlds
whose identifiable set under monotone nuisance has $\ge2$ connected components while any
single threshold on $O$ is monotone (Theorem~\ref{thm:mc-breakerM1}). Consequently the
minimal untestable supplement in the general regime is not one global bit but a
\textbf{per-component orientation} on the flip set---up to $(\#\text{components}-1)$ bits---
consistent with the one-bit dichotomy (Theorem~\ref{thm:conj1-dichotomy}) applied componentwise.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
\textbf{(Binary margin failure.)} Theorem~\ref{thm:mc-thirdclass} exhibits $K{=}3$ worlds with
identical $Q_X$ and identical deployed-class score $\eta_{j_g}$ on $D$, hence identical binary
margin $M$, yet opposite $\sign\Delta$. Any capacity $K$ depending only on $(Q_X,\eta_{j_g})$
cannot separate them; in particular $K_{\mathrm{bin}}=|M|/\beta$ predicts identifiability
when $|M|>\beta$ in \emph{both} worlds while $\sign\Delta$ flips.

\textbf{(Multi-component flip set.)} Theorem~\ref{thm:mc-breakerM1} realizes genuine
affine-argmax classifiers whose conflict pair varies across $D$, yielding $\ge2$ sign changes of
$\Delta(\mu)$ as the nuisance $\mu$ moves. A single threshold test on any scalar $O(\mu)$
identifies at most one component; the remaining components require additional orientation bits.

\textbf{(Dichotomy alignment.)} Theorem~\ref{thm:conj1-dichotomy}(iii) forbids an
evidence-definable universal identifier of $\sign\Delta$; the multiclass obstruction is the
same swap/flip phenomenon with extra residual degrees of freedom (Block~0 identity). The
capacity program therefore closes as an \emph{impossibility of a single scalar reduct}, not as
a missing positive formula.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[Status]\label{rmk:mc-cap-closed}
This resolves the open row ``computable multiclass capacity without R1/R2'' as a
\textbf{negative characterization}: R1/R2 (conflict-pair structure + residual control) are
\textbf{necessary} at $K\ge3$; the single-conflict-pair positive capacity
(Theorem~\ref{thm:mc-singlepair}) is the maximal lift of the binary theory.
\end{remark}
