# Response: the conditionality of the K-Bound guarantee

Every number below is quoted from `research_lock/assumption_reports/*.json`, which are
generated by `scripts/emit_assumption_reports.py` from `NUMBERS_PACK.json` and carry the
originating entry id, method string and artifact paths in their `provenance` block.

---

We agree with the core of this criticism. The guarantee is conditional, and it does not
establish that exchangeability, risk alignment or calibration transfer holds on a new
deployment. The revision makes that conditionality a named, enforced object rather than a
caveat repeated in prose.

**Three statements, kept apart.** The paper now distinguishes (1) *theoretical coverage*,
a property of a distribution, asserted only where the assumptions are argued; (2)
*observed empirical coverage*, a hit rate with its sample size, resampling unit and an
interval computed at that unit; and (3) *deployment diagnostics*, which falsify and cannot
verify. These are Definitions 1–3 of §"Three coverage statements, kept separate".

**The contract.** Six conditions are stated immediately before the certificate theorem —
(A1) calibration exchangeability, (A2) risk alignment, (A3) the coverage premise, (A4)
protocol independence, (A5) correct sampling unit, (A6) fixed decision rule — together
with the statement that the theorem is conditional on all six and that K-Bound infers none
of them from unlabelled data. Five rename conditions the paper already carried (the rows
of the protocol-discipline table, and E1/E2). **(A5) is new**, and it is the one the
empirical sections most often left implicit.

We also make explicit where the guarantee lives: the proof uses no property of
$\widehat\Delta$ beyond its appearance in the interval endpoints, so (A3) carries the
safety and the benefit estimator buys only decision yield. A better estimator narrows the
interval; a worthless one widens it until the system abstains.

**The gate.** A deployment may issue strict adapt/freeze decisions only after passing a
predeclared seven-step gate, whose failure states map onto a four-state ladder (full
certificate / freeze-or-abstain / diagnostic only / certification disabled). It fails
closed: an unevaluated check counts as a failed check.

**Assumption reports.** Each track now emits a fixed-schema record. Running the emitter
over the audited pack gives:

| track | gate | permitted | n_units | theoretical coverage claimed |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ImageNet-C SAR | `diagnostic_only` | none | 27 | false |
| CIFAR-10-C SAR | `reject` | none | 5 | false |
| PACS | `diagnostic_only` | none | 12 | false |
| ImageNet-R | `diagnostic_only` | none | 40 | false |

`theoretical_coverage_claimed` is `false` on every track. That is not a formatting
artefact — there is no track on which we are willing to assert marginal coverage for an
unseen deployment condition.

**On the resampling unit.** The reviewer is right, and the consequence is sharper than we
had stated. On ImageNet-C SAR the beats-both conclusion **holds** when the 135 cell-seed
rows are treated as independent and **fails** when they are clustered by condition (27
units); clustering by corruption family leaves only 3 clusters, which is not a primary
interval at all. Both clusterings are admissible, so the promoted conclusion is in part a
conclusion about the clustering. The gate now marks this `diagnostic_only` rather than
letting the favourable unit stand unremarked.

**The negative results, as boundary evidence.**

- **PACS** — pooled false-adapt is 2/216 = 0.0093, Clopper–Pearson 95% [0.0011, 0.0331],
  comfortably below $\alpha$. Underneath it, `art_painting` seed 1 has false-adapt 0.111,
  *above* $\alpha$, and seed 2 abstains on all 18 cells, so the guarantee is untested
  there rather than satisfied. An acceptable pooled rate coexists with routing utility
  worse than always-adapt: coverage is not utility.
- **ImageNet-R** — KGA is worse than always-adapt on 7 of 10 backbones. Observed
  false-adapt is 1/480, but harmful cells are nearly absent on several backbones, so the
  guarantee is barely exercised. We now report it as guarantee-untested rather than as a
  safety success.
- **CIFAR-10-C SAR** — quarantined. Seed 0's harmful base rate is 5.81× the mean of seeds
  1–4, so the seeds are not exchangeable at the seed unit; the single seed that beats both
  fixed policies is seed 0. The gate returns `reject`.

K-Bound should therefore be read as **conditional insurance against detectable adaptation
harm**, not as an unconditional safety guarantee.

## One point we would put differently

We would resist the reading that the submitted version asserted the assumptions had been
proved. It already stated that the finite-sample guarantee is conditional on valid
interval coverage; already reported split-conformal and leave-one-condition-out
calibration separately, with the note that observed coverage under the latter does not
imply the same finite-sample guarantee; already described the separability screen as
one-sided; and already disclosed that episode exchangeability is not established.

What was missing was not candour but **enforcement**: the conditions were stated in
several places under three vocabularies, and nothing checked them or recorded them
alongside the numbers. That is what the contract, the gate and the reports fix. We are
happy to be corrected if a specific sentence overclaims.
