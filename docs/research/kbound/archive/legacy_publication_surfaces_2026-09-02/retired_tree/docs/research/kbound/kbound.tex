\section{Introduction}\label{sec:intro}
\begin{figure}[t]\centering
\kbgraphics[width=0.86\columnwidth]{figures/fig_decision_flow.png}
\caption{\textbf{K-Bound previews the decision.} From an unlabeled batch under shift, the certificate
estimates the adaptation benefit $\widehat\Delta$ with a finite-sample radius $\varepsilon$ and returns
\textsc{adapt} (knowably helpful), \textsc{freeze} (knowably harmful), or \textsc{abstain} (sign
unidentifiable), with false-adapt/false-freeze $\le\alpha$.}
\label{fig:teaser}

Should a model adapt to unlabeled target data, or would adapting make it worse?
Test-time adaptation (TTA) updates a model on the test stream---through entropy
minimization, sample filtering, or stability-aware updates---and in favorable
shifts recovers lost accuracy. In unfavorable shifts the same unlabeled objective
silently degrades it: the model sharpens wrong predictions, adapts to label
imbalance, or collapses under non-stationary streams. Because target labels are
unavailable at deployment, the system usually cannot tell whether adaptation is
helping or hurting.

Most work on TTA asks how to design a better adaptation mechanism. This paper asks
the prior question: should the system adapt at all? We study label-free adaptation
as a decision problem. Given a frozen baseline, an adapted candidate, and an
unlabeled target batch, the system must choose one of three actions---adapt when
adaptation is knowably helpful, freeze when knowably harmful, and abstain when the
available evidence cannot identify the sign of the benefit. Figure~\ref{fig:teaser}
previews this decision certificate.

This decision is constrained by identifiability, and the constraint has a name and a price. That
label-free deployment quantities cannot in general pin down a target risk---and hence cannot in
general decide adaptation without some assumption on the shift---is established. Garg et
al.~\cite{garg2022atc} show identifying out-of-distribution accuracy is as hard as identifying the
optimal predictor, and Kalai and Kanade~\cite{kalai2021abstain} characterize the optimal
predict-versus-abstain trade-off under shift against a shift budget. We do not re-derive this
general impossibility; we \emph{specialize} it to the adaptation-benefit sign and make its boundary
exact. The sign of the benefit is identifiable from label-free observables precisely when an
observable margin on the disagreement region exceeds a declared bound on calibration
drift---a \emph{drift budget} $\beta$---so that $|M|>\beta$. The margin is how confidently the
label-free evidence points toward ``adapt'' or ``freeze''; the budget is how far that signal can be
thrown off by calibration drift; the decision is trustworthy exactly when confidence outweighs
drift. This makes precise why common label-free heuristics work in benign regimes and fail under
drift: they live on the $\beta{=}0$ face of the frontier.

\textbf{The question that decides the method is therefore where $\beta$ comes from, and this paper
answers it: it cannot come from the deployment.} For every declared class of target laws the exact
minimax label-free budget is the fibre radius $\Gamma_z$, and it is attained by a constant audit
that reads none of its data (Theorem~\ref{thm:beta-minimax}). For the paper's own deployment class
that radius equals $\beta$ exactly (Corollary~\ref{cor:beta-is-beta}): the best possible label-free
audit of the drift budget returns the budget it was given, and the number it returns is
simultaneously the radius of the band on which a strict adapt-or-freeze commitment is unsound. Two
riders make this operational rather than philosophical. Over the unrestricted class any
$\delta$-valid audited rule commits with probability at most $\delta$---decision yield is bounded by
the error budget, at every batch size, every calibration sample size and every evidence map. And a
\emph{fully labeled} calibration sample from any other domain leaves the minimax budget unchanged
absent a separately declared coupling (Theorem~\ref{thm:anchor}). One lemma carries all of
it---that on the disagreement region the label kernel is a free parameter no label-free observable
constrains (Lemma~\ref{lem:fibre})---and the same lemma, read at three different radii, is the
matched-evidence impossibility, the abstention band, and the budget impossibility.

We then measure the prediction on our own method. Declaring $\beta$ as a high quantile of realized
drift on source-like development cells, exactly as this paper's method section prescribes, fails on
$6{,}885$ real evaluation cells (\S\ref{sec:beta-sweep-long}), and \textbf{we report that negative
result about our own parameter} rather than withdraw it. It is not a quantile-estimation failure:
across $470$ splits the conformal order statistic that replaces the plug-in quantile differs from it
by a median $0.34\%$, with a median coverage difference of exactly zero. What escapes the
impossibility is a \emph{population} of deployments rather than a deployment
(\S\ref{app:episode-beta}): under retrospective outcome logging and episode exchangeability $\beta$
is identified as an episode quantile and estimable by a conformal order statistic, with the episode
requirement bracketed to a factor of $e$. The assumption that buys it is the one a shifted
deployment violates, and we measure that too. What remains deployable is a finite-sample certificate
that trades decision yield for a false-adapt guarantee, and we measure that frontier exactly
(\S\ref{sec:decision-value}), including the track where it buys nothing.

In the test-time-adaptation setting studied here we further quantify \emph{how much} untestable
information the decision needs: the minimal supplement that no label-free-checkable assumption can
remove is exactly \emph{one bit} of orientation, which can only be supplied through structural
assumptions that are falsifiable but not verifiable. (In full generality the irreducible supplement
is problem-dependent---an interval under affine ambiguity---so we state the one-bit count for the
TTA/panel setting under our stated hypothesis, not for arbitrary functionals.)

We instantiate the framework as \textsc{KGA}, a mechanism-agnostic wrapper around
existing adaptation candidates such as Tent, EATA, and SAR. \textsc{KGA} does not
introduce a new adaptation objective. Instead, it estimates the label-free benefit
of a candidate, attaches a calibrated uncertainty radius, and returns adapt,
freeze, or abstain. This makes \textsc{KGA} a safety layer for TTA rather than a
replacement for TTA methods.

Empirically, \textsc{KGA} behaves according to the theory. \textbf{Headline (natural shifts):}
under a valid out-of-fold conformal radius \textsc{KGA} is \emph{no-harm on the four one-sided
natural tracks with locked held-out artifacts}---Camelyon17, iWildCam, Office-Home, and
RxRx1---where it matches the better fixed policy and beats the worse one at zero observed
false adaptation, with \emph{no} natural-shift beats-both. It is \textbf{not} uniformly no-harm
across the panel: on the domain-generalization and rendition tracks it is a conservative null that
\emph{loses} to always-adapt, by $2.45\times$ on PACS ($0.0431$ versus $0.0176$) and on $7$ of $10$
backbones on ImageNet-R (mean $0.0112$ under this manuscript's interpolated radius,
$0.0151$ under the exact-rank rule, against always-adapt's $0.0064$ either way), and CIFAR-10.1 fails the declared
transfer bar outright ($\mathrm{FA}_{\mathrm u}=0.167$). Those three are reported here, not
withdrawn. Office-Home (Protocol~M v2) and iWildCam
(Protocol~H v2) beat always-adapt (CIs exclude zero) but \emph{tie} always-freeze
(Tables~\ref{tab:iwildcam-H}, \ref{tab:officehome-M}); their earlier in-sample-radius
``beats-both'' was a calibration bug, corrected here to no-harm. On the one-sided WILDS shifts
Camelyon17 (Protocol~G) and RxRx1, \textsc{KGA} likewise matches the better fixed policy at
false-adapt $\le\alpha$ without beating both; the earlier Camelyon17 Protocol-G beats-both was a
separate pooling artifact (in-distribution \texttt{id\_val} cells mixed into the held-out set,
\S\ref{sec:natural-suite}). The completed three-seed PACS leave-one-domain-out study is a null
action-safety diagnostic rather than a promoted no-harm or beats-both result
(Table~\ref{tab:pacs}). \textbf{Confirmatory (identifiable synthetic/mixed):} on the pre-registered
CIFAR-10-C stress grid, where harmful adaptation is frequent and detectable, \textsc{KGA} beats both
always-adapt and always-freeze for Tent and EATA while making zero false-adapt decisions; the
unreconciled CIFAR-10-C SAR artifact is withheld. On the same mixed stream it \textbf{beats POEM and AETTA}
(Table~\ref{tab:headtohead-poem-aetta}) with Holm-corrected paired-bootstrap CIs. In online
non-stationary CIFAR-10 streams, always-adapt collapses in the harm-dominated regime and
always-freeze forgoes gains in the helpful regime, while \textsc{KGA} remains near-best across
both. At ImageNet scale, \textsc{KGA} also improves over both trivial policies on a harmful SAR
stream. ImageNet-R remains a completed weak-evidence diagnostic, not a win.

\paragraph{Contributions.}
\textbf{Submission hierarchy.} The primary claim stack is deliberately narrow: the impossibility
spine below, the finite-sample certificate, and the registered empirical panel. Multicandidate,
anytime, multiclass-capacity and regression results are companion extensions with their own stated
assumptions (Appendix~\ref{app:extensions}); none is needed to validate any empirical claim.
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{One lemma, three radii.} We isolate the construction the paper previously carried out
three times: on the disagreement region the label kernel is a free parameter that no label-free
observable constrains (Lemma~\ref{lem:fibre}). Read at radius $\delta$ it is the matched-evidence
impossibility (Theorem~\ref{lem:nonid}); at radius $\beta$ it is the abstention band of the
strict-commitment frontier (Theorem~\ref{thm:headline}); at its extreme points it is the vacuity of
label-free budget audits (Theorem~\ref{thm:aud-A}). Naming it once is what makes the rest of the
paper a single statement rather than three. The configuration it constructs is also
\emph{observed}: \S\ref{sec:necessity-observed} exhibits deployment pairs in the logged CIFAR-10-C
and ImageNet-C artifacts whose label-free evidence cannot be told apart while their benefit has
resolvably opposite sign, and measures that such pairs are $39\times$ \emph{rarer} than chance ---
so the evidence is informative and merely insufficient, which is exactly the gap the lemma
describes.
\item \textbf{The exact minimax label-free drift budget, and the identity that closes the loop.}
For every declared class $\mathcal C$ the least label-free-certifiable budget is the fibre radius
$\Gamma_z(\mathcal C)$, attained by a constant audit that reads none of its data
(Theorem~\ref{thm:beta-minimax}(a),(b)). For this paper's own deployment class,
$\Gamma_z(\mathcal C_\beta)=\beta$ exactly (Corollary~\ref{cor:beta-is-beta}): \emph{the best
possible label-free audit of the drift budget returns the budget you declared, and that number is
the radius of the band on which the frontier must abstain.} No label-free audit ever converts an
abstention into a commitment (Theorem~\ref{thm:beta-minimax}(c)), and over the unrestricted class
the audited rule's decision yield is at most the false-commitment budget $\delta$, at every batch
size, every calibration sample size and every evidence map (Theorem~\ref{thm:beta-minimax}(d)).
\item \textbf{Labels at the wrong domain buy nothing, and the three currencies a budget costs.} A
fully labeled calibration sample of any size leaves the minimax budget at $\tfrac12+|M|$ unless a
coupling to the deployment domain is separately declared (Theorem~\ref{thm:anchor}). The exact
decomposition (Proposition~\ref{prop:threeterm}) shows a budget is the sum of three terms bought in
three different currencies---labels at an anchor, a label-free transport term, and a declared
invariance defect---and that the declared term is itself a drift budget of the same type, so
declaring $\rho$ instead of $\beta$ is a renaming and not a reduction. With deployment-domain labels
the rate is closed: an $n^{-1/2}$ floor (Theorem~\ref{thm:lecam}) matching the paper's own upper
bound, and a three-way dichotomy (Theorem~\ref{thm:dichotomy}) separating no-rate, root-$n$ and
degenerate regimes.
\item \textbf{Exact benefit-sign frontier and the $\beta{=}0$ face.} The sign is identifiable over
the declared drift-budget class \emph{iff} $|M|>\beta$, with the three-way adapt/freeze/abstain rule
as the unique maximal sound certificate; ATC, DoC, GDE, COT, AETTA and agreement-on-the-line are its
$\beta{=}0$ face, sound precisely when calibration does not drift.
\item \textbf{A negative result about our own parameter, and it is the predicted one.} Declaring
$\beta$ as this paper's method section prescribes fails on $6{,}885$ real evaluation cells
(\S\ref{sec:beta-sweep-long}): on CIFAR-10-C the declared budget is $1.4\times$ to $50\times$
smaller than soundness requires, $24$--$73\%$ of deployment cells fall outside the declared class,
and committed actions are wrong at $0.4$--$16.6\%$ where the frontier promises $0\%$; on ImageNet-C
a large enough budget is derivable and returns zero commitments on all $405$ cells in five of ten
configurations. Theorem~\ref{thm:anchor} says why: the procedure supplies the first term of
Proposition~\ref{prop:threeterm} and sets the other two to zero by an undeclared exchangeability
assumption.
\item \textbf{Where a budget can come from, priced.} Under retrospective outcome logging and episode
exchangeability, $\beta$ is identified as an episode quantile and estimable by a conformal order
statistic (\S\ref{app:episode-beta}), with the episode requirement bracketed to a factor of $e$
($1/(e\alpha)-1\le K^\star\le1/\alpha-1$; between $3$ and $9$ logged deployments at $\alpha=0.10$,
first feasible on real data at exactly $K=9$). The escape is one logical move---average over the
episode law instead of taking a supremum over the fibre, paid for with historical labels---and
neither ingredient suffices alone (Proposition~\ref{epi:prop-escape}). The assumption that buys it
is measured and it fails on the axis that defines a new deployment: against a nominal
$0.900$, coverage is $0.905$ on an exchangeable control, $0.626$ when the corruption family
changes and $0.454$ when severity changes;
weighted conformal restores coverage to $1.000$ by driving decision yield to $0.000$.
\item \textbf{Split-observability (honest generality).} We isolate the structural property our
proofs actually use---a benefit functional is \emph{split-observable} if
$\sign(\Delta)=\sign(M+\gamma)$ with $M$ observable and $|\gamma|\le\beta$---and show the
impossibility, finite-$n$ floor, certificate, and minimax/necessity results hold for \emph{any}
split-observable functional, with TTA (via the disagreement-region reduction) as the demonstrated
instance. The $|M|>\beta$ criterion and the one-bit count are stated for split-observable
functionals, not for arbitrary functionals.
\item \textbf{A finite-sample certificate, with its frontier measured rather than asserted.} The
certificate controls false adaptation and false freezing under risk-aligned evidence. On the
CIFAR-10-C stress grid it commits on $66.8\%$ of $6{,}480$ cells at zero false adaptations over
$1{,}113$ and $1{,}244$ \textsc{adapt} decisions, cuts regret $5.00\times$ below the better fixed
policy and $3.1\times$ below a hindsight-tuned label-free drift heuristic held to
$\mathrm{FA}_{\mathrm u}\le0.10$, and places its commitments on cells whose true effect is
$24.5\times$ larger than the ones it declines (\S\ref{sec:decision-value}); under
leave-one-corruption-out calibration the radius inflates $4.3\times$--$6.9\times$ and regret worsens
$3.4\times$--$7.6\times$ while the false-adapt count stays at zero in all ten runs. Where the shift
is one-sided it buys nothing and we report that: on ImageNet-R it is $1.76\times$ worse than
always-adapt.
\item We validate on a multi-regime panel with bootstrap confidence intervals.
\textbf{Headline:} no-harm on the locked held-out natural shifts Office-Home, iWildCam,
Camelyon17, and RxRx1 under their declared protocols; completed PACS and ImageNet-R runs remain
null diagnostics. \textbf{Confirmatory:} beats-both on
synthetic stress grids (CIFAR-10-C, ImageNet-C SAR) and a pre-registered mixed head-to-head win over
POEM and AETTA (Table~\ref{tab:headtohead-poem-aetta}). Controlled synthetic validations and an
additional breadth dataset (PACS) are reported in Appendix~\ref{app:supp}, with CIFAR-10.1 retained
in-body as a supporting low-margin check (\S\ref{sec:external}). A separately constructed
cross-protocol aggregate uses one pooled benefit model and out-of-fold radius and is reported only
as a routing construction (\S\ref{sec:mixedstream}); it is not a universal deployment result and
does not change any per-dataset verdict.
\end{itemize}

\noindent\emph{Two bars, stated once.} Throughout, the \textbf{pre-registered headline bar} is a
point-estimate beats-both at false-adapt $\le\alpha$; the \textbf{CI-robust} criterion
(both bootstrap regret-gap CIs exclude zero) is a strictly stronger layer we report as a separate
column. Under a valid out-of-fold radius, no natural shift clears either bar: Office-Home and
iWildCam beat always-adapt with CIs excluding zero but \emph{tie} always-freeze, so both are
no-harm, not wins. The only beats-both rows are the synthetic stress grids (CIFAR-10-C, ImageNet-C
SAR).

K-Bound does not claim universal improvement, and it does not claim no-harm across the whole panel.
\textbf{The headline deployment claim is no-harm on the four one-sided natural tracks with locked
held-out artifacts} --- Camelyon17, iWildCam, Office-Home and RxRx1 --- where \textsc{KGA} matches
the better fixed policy and beats the worse one at zero observed false adaptation. On the
domain-generalization and rendition tracks it is a conservative null that \emph{loses} to
always-adapt (PACS by $2.45\times$; ImageNet-R on $7$ of $10$ backbones), and CIFAR-10.1 fails the
declared transfer bar outright; see the qualification at \S\ref{sec:intro} and
Table~\ref{tab:regime-summary}. Where the claim does hold, the mechanism is the obvious one: when
adapting is already near-oracle, a certificate can only tie or slightly trail always-adapt; when
freezing is already optimal, it should match freeze rather than force an unnecessary update. Its distinctive value---and the confirmatory wins on synthetic
grids---appear when harmful adaptation is frequent, detectable, and costly, the regime where blind
adaptation is most dangerous.

\section{Related work and positioning}
We position K-Bound as a precise delta over five adjacent lines of work. In each case the
distinction is the one the theory formalizes: between \emph{improving} an unlabeled objective
and \emph{certifying the sign} of its benefit before acting.

\subsection{Test-time adaptation methods}
Entropy-based TTA updates a frozen model on unlabeled target data. \textbf{Tent}~\cite{wang2021tent} minimizes
prediction entropy over the batch-norm affine parameters; \textbf{SHOT}~\cite{liang2020shot} combines information
maximization with pseudo-labeling for source-free adaptation; \textbf{TTT}~\cite{sun2020ttt} trains a
self-supervised auxiliary task and updates it at test time; \textbf{EATA}~\cite{niu2022eata} adds entropy
filtering of unreliable samples plus a Fisher penalty against forgetting; \textbf{SAR}~\cite{niu2023sar}
adds sharpness-aware updates with a reset that aborts incipient collapse; \textbf{CoTTA}~\cite{wang2022cotta}
stabilizes continual adaptation with teacher-student ensembling; and \textbf{MEMO}~\cite{zhang2022memo}
adapts via test-time augmentation and entropy minimization (see~\cite{liang2025ttasurvey} for a recent survey). These methods make
adaptation \emph{more robust}, and several detect or suppress instability, but all commit to
adapting: none emits a \emph{pre-adaptation} certificate separating helpful, harmful, and
unknowable evidence, and none can \emph{abstain} on the adapt/freeze decision itself. K-Bound
is mechanism-agnostic---it \emph{wraps} any such candidate (we evaluate Tent/EATA/SAR).
Tempora~\cite{tempora2026} further shows that unconstrained offline rankings can invert under
latency and compute pressure, with no fixed policy robust across temporal scenarios. K-Bound is
complementary: rather than selecting one always-on method, it certifies per-condition
\textsc{adapt}/\textsc{freeze}/\textsc{abstain} decisions.

\subsection{Protected and guarded adaptation}
A second line guards adaptation \emph{during} the update. \textbf{POEM}~\cite{bar2024poem}
protects TTA by online entropy matching---a betting-style monitor that halts or dampens a
single failure mode---and \textbf{Schirmer et al.}~\cite{schirmer2025monitoring} run anytime-valid risk monitors over the
TTA stream, raising a sequential alarm when adaptation degrades a proxy risk; both descend
from sequential harmful-shift tracking for deployed models (\textbf{Podkopaev \& Ramdas}~\cite{podkopaev2022tracking}). \textbf{PeTTA}~\cite{hoang2024petta} senses drift toward collapse and adjusts the adaptation process on the fly, and sample-reliability gates such as \textbf{DeYO}~\cite{lee2024deyo} decide \emph{which samples} are safe to adapt on.
These are the nearest neighbours of our certificate, and the delta is precise: they act
\emph{reactively, during or after} the update and control a false-\emph{alarm} rate in one
failure direction, whereas K-Bound decides \emph{before} committing, poses the
\emph{three-way} decision with an explicit \emph{unknowable} region (where abstention is
mandatory, Cor.~\ref{cor:forced-abstain}), controls the false-\emph{adapt} rate on the
benefit sign itself, and proves the matching impossibility floor
(Prop.~\ref{thm:imp-quant}). The two are complementary: a pre-adaptation certificate can
gate what a runtime monitor then tracks.

\subsection{Label-free accuracy and error estimation}
A third line predicts target \emph{performance} without labels: \textbf{ATC}~\cite{garg2022atc} thresholds
softmax confidence calibrated on source; \textbf{Agreement-on-the-Line}~\cite{baek2022aol,miller2021aol} exploits the linear
correlation between in- and out-of-distribution model \emph{agreement} to extrapolate
accuracy; \textbf{AETTA}~\cite{lee2024aetta} estimates TTA accuracy from dropout-prediction disagreement.
Crucially, Garg et al.~\cite{garg2022atc} also establish the governing limit for this entire
line: identifying OOD accuracy is \emph{as hard as} identifying the optimal predictor, so any
label-free accuracy estimate is sound only under an assumption on the shift. Our frontier
\emph{organizes} these estimators rather than competing with them: ATC, DoC, GDE, COT, AETTA,
and agreement-on-the-line are all the zero-budget ($\beta{=}0$) face of the benefit-sign frontier
(Thm.~\ref{thm:frontier}(iv))---each is sound precisely when calibration does not drift
($\gamma{=}0$), and each silently fails exactly where our budget is positive.
Closest in spirit, \textbf{Kim et al.}~\cite{kim2024taol}\ use agreement-on-the-line to decide \emph{when} TTA
helps and to select TTA hyperparameters without labels---a genuine decision use of an
accuracy estimate, but a plug-in one on that same $\beta{=}0$ face, sound only when calibration
transfers, with no finite-sample false-adapt control and no abstain region.
Estimating $R_T(f)$---or detecting that TTA has failed \emph{post hoc}---is strictly different
from certifying $\sign\big(R_T(f_0)-R_T(f_a)\big)$ \emph{before} adapting with a controlled
false-adapt rate, the object K-Bound bounds.

\subsection{Impossibility, abstention under shift, and identifiability}
The general impossibility our work specializes is \emph{owned by prior work}, and we cite it as
such. \textbf{Garg et al.}~\cite{garg2022atc} prove that identifying out-of-distribution accuracy
from unlabeled target data is as hard as identifying the optimal predictor---so point
identification provably requires an assumption on the shift---and \textbf{Kalai \&
Kanade}~\cite{kalai2021abstain} give the matching decision theory, characterizing the optimal
abstain-versus-predict rule under shift via an observable-quantity-versus-shift-budget threshold
with a minimax certificate. These are the sources of both the impossibility and the
abstention-under-budget decision we build on; K-Bound does not re-derive them but
\emph{specializes} them to the sign of the adaptation benefit and makes the boundary exact.
This hardness is also visible across unsupervised risk estimation.
\textbf{Steinhardt \& Liang}~\cite{steinhardt2016} estimate risk without labels only under a conditional-independence
structure; \textbf{Ben-David et al.}~\cite{bendavid2010theory,bendavid2010impossibility} bound target risk by source risk plus an
$\mathcal{H}$-divergence; \textbf{Lipton et al.}~\cite{lipton2018bbse} (BBSE) correct label shift under an invertible
confusion matrix; \textbf{Rosenfeld \& Garg}~\cite{rosenfeld2023dis2} bound target error under an explicit,
unverifiable assumption (their Remark~3.9 states informally the same obstruction our
Theorem~\ref{thm:conj1-dichotomy} makes exact for the benefit sign); the rank-one agreement algebra underlying our
Theorem~\ref{thm:conj1-dichotomy} is classical~\cite{parisi2014,jaffe2015,platanios2014,ibrahim2019},
its dependent-classifier diagnostics appear in~\cite{jaffe2016}, and label-free
algebraic evaluation with consistency alarms is developed in the NTQR
program~\cite{corradaemmanuel2024}. Each shows risk is identifiable only under structural assumptions and
impossible in general---the landscape our Theorem~\ref{thm:imp}(ii) specializes and makes
quantitative for the benefit sign. We weaken the target from a \emph{risk} to the \emph{sign of a difference} of
risks, which is identifiable on a strictly larger set (the observable disagreement region,
Lemma~\ref{lem:reduction}).

\subsection{Selective prediction, conformal, and anytime-valid inference}
\textbf{Selective prediction}~\cite{geifman2017selective} abstains on individual \emph{predictions};
we instead abstain on the \emph{adaptation decision}. Our finite-sample certificate reuses
split-conformal prediction (\textbf{Vovk et al.}~\cite{vovk2005}; \textbf{Angelopoulos \& Bates}~\cite{angelopoulos2023}) to build the
radius $\varepsilon$, and its streaming form (Appendix~\ref{app:anytime}) is a
testing-by-betting e-process in the safe-anytime-valid-inference tradition (\textbf{Shafer}~\cite{shafer2021};
\textbf{Ramdas et al.}~\cite{ramdas2023}); confidence-sequence and concentration tools
(\textbf{Howard et al.}~\cite{howard2021timeuniform}, \textbf{Maurer \& Pontil}~\cite{maurer2009empirical},
\textbf{Hoeffding}~\cite{hoeffding1963probability}); and Vovk's conformal test martingales~\cite{vovk2021martingale}
embody, for exchangeability, the falsify-not-verify stance our budget audit
(Thm.~\ref{thm:conj1-dichotomy}(iv)) takes for the drift budget.
Because deployment breaks exchangeability, we connect to conformal-under-shift and risk control: weighted conformal corrects coverage under known covariate shift~\cite{tibshirani2019conformal}, adaptive conformal tracks coverage online~\cite{gibbs2021adaptive}, and beyond exchangeability the coverage gap is bounded by the total-variation drift of the calibration pairs~\cite{barber2023beyond}---the population counterpart of our declared budget $\beta$. Our marginal false-adapt guarantee sits in the risk-control lineage (RCPS~\cite{bates2021rcps}; Learn-then-Test~\cite{angelopoulos2025ltt}), specialized to the adapt/freeze/abstain loss, and PAC prediction sets give set-valued analogues under covariate shift~\cite{park2022pac}.

\noindent\textbf{The surviving gap.} No prior method provides a single adapt/freeze/abstain
certificate with an \emph{explicit, proven} unknowable region and a false-adapt guarantee.
Table~\ref{tab:rel} makes the gap precise. There is a second gap, and it is the one this paper is
organized around. Every line of work above that gives a guarantee under shift---weighted and
adaptive conformal, the beyond-exchangeability coverage gap, the abstention-under-budget
rule---prices its guarantee against a declared shift or drift quantity, and none asks where that
declaration comes from at a deployment with no target labels. \S\ref{app:beta-impossible} asks it
and answers it: the minimax label-free value of that quantity is a constant equal to the
declaration itself. That is why the negative result of \S\ref{sec:beta-sweep-long} is reported here
rather than repaired, and why \S\ref{app:episode-beta} moves the question to a population of
deployments instead of trying to answer it at one.

\subsection{Benchmarks, code, and reproducibility}
TTA progress is benchmarked on standardized corruption suites---CIFAR-10/100-C and
ImageNet-C~\cite{hendrycks2019cifar10c}, plus ImageNet-R~\cite{hendrycks2021manyfaces}---and on
cross-domain shift suites including WILDS/Camelyon17/RxRx1/Office-Home~\cite{koh2021wilds,bandi2019camelyon,taylor2019rxrx1,venkateswara2017officehome}.
The field increasingly emphasizes standardized, executable evaluation
(\textbf{RobustBench}~\cite{croce2021robustbench}; \textbf{DomainBed}~\cite{gulrajani2021domainbed}) and
reproducibility checks on fresh test draws such as CIFAR-10.1~\cite{recht2019cifar10}. Most TTA
methods ship official code, but evaluations vary in batch regime, stream composition, and update
budget---\emph{precisely} the axis along which adaptation flips between helpful and harmful. We
therefore release K-Bound as a \emph{pre-registered, executable} artifact: a public repository
with pinned environments, a one-command reproduction harness, auto-downloaded standard corruption
data, fixed seeds, machine-checked theorem validators (kernel-checked generalization bounds exist in proof assistants~\cite{zhang2025leanrademacher}, though not, to our knowledge, conformal coverage), and the full stratified result manifests
behind every table and figure (\S\ref{sec:repro}). Our certificate builds on split-conformal
prediction (Vovk et al.; Angelopoulos \& Bates) and, for the streaming variant, testing-by-betting
e-processes (Shafer; Ramdas et al.). The CAN benchmark formalizes a practical ``adapt-or-skip''
decision in chronological streams~\cite{jeon2025can}; K-Bound complements that benchmark view with
a theorem-level frontier and impossibility account for when such decisions are certifiable from
label-free evidence.

\section{Problem setup}\label{sec:setup}
Source $P_S(X,Y)$ has labels at train/validation; target $P_T(X,Y)$ exposes only
$P_T(X)$ at test time. Fix a loss $\ell$, a frozen model $f_0$, and an
adapted/gated candidate $f_a$. Target risk $R_T(f)=\E_{P_T}[\ell(f(X),Y)]$.
The \emph{adaptation benefit} is
\begin{equation}
\Delta \;=\; R_T(f_0)-R_T(f_a),\qquad \Delta>0 \iff \text{adapting helps.}
\end{equation}
\emph{Observable evidence} $Z=\phi(X_{1:m},f_0,f_a)$ is any statistic computable
\emph{without} target labels (entropy, confidence, score-distribution drift,
detector disagreement, predicted-class balance, update norm, density-ratio
diagnostics). The conditional benefit is $\Delta(z)=\E[\ell(f_0(X),Y)-\ell(f_a(X),Y)\mid Z=z]$.
The central question is when $\sign\Delta(z)$ is recoverable from $Z$ alone.

\paragraph{Notation.} $f_0$ is the frozen model and $f_a$ the adapted/gated candidate;
$\Delta=R_T(f_0)-R_T(f_a)$ is the population adaptation benefit and $\Delta(z)$ its value
conditioned on evidence $Z{=}z$; $\widehat\Delta$ is a label-free estimator of $\Delta$ with
confidence radius $\varepsilon$ at miscoverage level $\alpha$; $g(Z)\in\{\textsc{adapt},
\textsc{freeze},\textsc{abstain}\}$ is the decision rule; and $D=\{x:f_0(x)\neq f_a(x)\}$ is the
observable disagreement region. \emph{Regret-to-oracle} denotes $R(g)-R(g^\star)$ against the
per-condition oracle $g^\star=\mathbf1[\Delta>0]$.

\section{Theory: one lemma, three radii, and a budget that cannot be supplied}\label{sec:theory}\label{sec:bsfrontier}\label{sec:gmeter}
\noindent\emph{What we specialize, and how general it is.} The general fact that deciding
adaptation label-free needs an assumption on the shift is due to Garg et
al.~\cite{garg2022atc} and Kalai \& Kanade~\cite{kalai2021abstain}; we do not re-derive it.
What follows \emph{specializes} that landscape to the benefit sign and makes its boundary
exact. The structural property our proofs actually use is \emph{split-observability}: a benefit
functional is split-observable if $\sign(\Delta)=\sign(M+\gamma)$ with $M$ an observable margin
and an unobservable calibration term $|\gamma|\le\beta$ bounded by the declared drift budget.
The impossibility, finite-$n$ floor, certificate, and minimax/necessity results below are
world-agnostic---they hold for \emph{any} split-observable functional---and test-time adaptation
is the canonical instance via the disagreement-region reduction (Lemma~\ref{lem:reduction}). The
sharper statements---the $|M|>\beta$ identifiability criterion and the exactly-one-bit
supplement---are established for split-observable functionals, with TTA as the demonstrated case;
we do not claim them for arbitrary functionals.

\section{Does the population frontier operationalize? A $\beta$ sweep (negative
result)}\label{sec:beta-sweep}\label{sec:beta-sweep-long}
\S\ref{app:beta-impossible} says a budget cannot be supplied by a label-free procedure. It does not
say what happens when one is supplied badly, and this section measures that on real data. The
declaration procedure tested here is the one this paper's own method section prescribes---$\beta$ as
a high quantile of realized drift on source-like development cells---and
Theorem~\ref{thm:anchor} with Proposition~\ref{prop:threeterm} says exactly what it is: it
purchases the first term of the three-term decomposition with labels at a development anchor and
sets the transport term and the invariance defect to zero by an \emph{undeclared} exchangeability
assumption. The theory therefore predicts under-coverage of unbounded size, and the population rule
$|M|\gtrless\pm\beta$ and the finite-sample rule $\widehat\Delta\pm\varepsilon$ have until now
shared vocabulary rather than evidence: $\beta$ is a declared deployment parameter and was never
numerically supplied to \textsc{KGA} on any benchmark.
\textbf{The test returns a negative result, and we report it as such.} It is also not a
quantile-estimation failure: replacing the plug-in quantile by the conformal order statistic of
Theorem~\ref{epi:thm-conformal} moves the budget by a median $0.34\%$ across $470$ splits, with a
median coverage difference of exactly zero (\S\ref{app:episode-beta}).

Because $\gamma$ is \emph{defined} as a residual, sufficiency is interval arithmetic and the
empirical content of the theorem is exactly two claims: (C1) that the label-free margin $M$ carries
usable sign information about $\Delta$, and (C2) that a budget declared from data a deployer
actually has bounds $|\gamma|$ at deployment. We measure both on $6{,}885$ real evaluation cells
(CIFAR-10-C stress grid, $432$ conditions $\times$ $3$ adapters $\times$ $5$ seeds $=6{,}480$;
ImageNet-C, $27\times3\times5=405$), with $M$ estimated \emph{out of fold} from the $11$-dimensional
label-free evidence vector alone (difference-of-confidences, a four-feature ATC-style score, and a
full-evidence gradient-boosted score), under leave-one-corruption-family-out, leave-one-seed-out and
source-like-holdout splits, and with $\beta$ declared as the $0.90$ quantile of the realized drift
on source-like development cells. An adversarial label-firewall check passes on all six
dataset~$\times$~estimator configurations: under full label permutation the out-of-fold AUC
collapses to $0.474$--$0.501$.

(C1) holds but is weaker than it looks: out-of-fold AUC against $\sign\Delta$ is $0.754$/$0.875$/
$0.974$ on CIFAR-10-C, but the base rate is $0.740$, so the ATC-style margin's raw sign agreement of
$0.808$ is only $+6.8$ points over the constant ``always adapt''---and difference-of-confidences is
\emph{anti-predictive} on ImageNet-C (AUC $0.351$/$0.264$/$0.373$ across the three splits).
(C2) fails, in both available directions. On CIFAR-10-C the declared $\widehat\beta$ is $1.4\times$
to $50\times$ smaller than the smallest budget that would make the committed actions sound;
$24$--$73\%$ of deployment cells fall outside the declared class, and commitments are wrong at
$0.4$--$16.6\%$ where the theorem promises $0\%$. On ImageNet-C a large enough budget \emph{is}
derivable and it is useless: in $5$ of $10$ configurations the declared budget produces zero
commitments on all $405$ cells. Of $20$ configurations, only $3$ achieve the promised zero
commit-error rate at nonzero yield---none of them with an ATC-style margin, none on CIFAR-10-C---and
the single most adverse row is the honest source-only calibration on ImageNet-C, which commits on
$62$ cells and gets $53.2\%$ of them wrong while formally certified. Under a genuinely held-out
margin, no $\beta>0$ matches the finite-sample rule on both decision yield and regret in $15$ of the
$18$ configurations swept; at matched yield the population rule's regret ratio is $1.1\times$ to
$8.1\times$. Sweeping $\beta\in[0,0.30]$ on CIFAR-10-C moves regret by $26\times$ ($M_{\mathrm{ATC4}}$,
leave-one-corruption-out) and $192\times$ ($M:=\widehat\Delta$), and at a single fixed
$\widehat\beta$ decision yield varies by $21$ points (CIFAR-10-C ATC4) to $52$ points (ImageNet-C
$\widehat\Delta$) across adapters and seeds: the budget behaves as a free knob, not a specification.

One positive survives, narrowly. Holding the evidence channel and calibration protocol fixed
($M:=\widehat\Delta$), $\beta$-thresholding beats the conformal interval on CIFAR-10-C at the
honestly-derived $\widehat\beta=0.0097$ (yield $0.749$ vs.\ $0.668$, regret $0.00081$ vs.\
$0.00150$, non-overlapping condition-clustered intervals at $k{=}432$)---but the intervals overlap
at $k{=}6$ corruption-family clusters, and the mechanism is a yield/soundness trade: it makes $18$
sign errors against the empirical rule's $1$. Soundness is what the theorem sells and what is given
up.

Neither proof is affected: sufficiency is arithmetic and necessity rests on the matched-evidence
construction. What is withdrawn is the \emph{operational} reading. The frontier is a statement about
a declared class whose antecedent we have now measured and found violated on most of one benchmark;
the deployable certificate is the conformal interval, and we do not present $|M|>\beta$ as a rule
K-Bound runs. Read against \S\ref{app:beta-impossible} this is not an isolated embarrassment but the
predicted instance: Corollary~\ref{cor:audA} forbids the label-free route to a budget outright, and
Theorem~\ref{thm:anchor} says that anchoring the declaration in labeled development cells changes
the minimax budget by exactly zero unless a coupling to the deployment domain is declared---which
this procedure does not declare. \S\ref{app:episode-beta} identifies the structure that would
supply the missing declaration, and measures how far these deployment populations are from it. Scripts, raw results and the full $20$-row table are in
\texttt{experiments/kbound/frontier\_sweep\_v1/}; the corresponding narrowing is recorded in
\S\ref{sec:limits}.

\section{Method: \textsc{KGA} (Knowability-Guided Adaptation)}
\textsc{KGA} is a decision \emph{wrapper}; it does not require a new adaptation
mechanism. Given a frozen $f_0$, a candidate adaptation, and an unlabeled batch:
(1) extract $Z=\phi(\cdot)$; (2) estimate benefit $\widehat\Delta(Z)$; (3) form a
radius $\varepsilon$; (4) return \textsc{adapt} if $\widehat\Delta-\varepsilon>0$,
\textsc{freeze} if $\widehat\Delta+\varepsilon<0$, else \textsc{abstain}. In our
experiments $\widehat\Delta$ is a gradient-boosted regressor and $\varepsilon$ is the $(1-\alpha)$
empirical quantile of \emph{out-of-fold} (leave-one-task-out) residuals, so the estimator and its
residual-calibration data are disjoint ($\alpha=0.1$). The coverage guarantee is split-dependent: a
clean dev/test split (the natural-shift protocols) gives \emph{exact} split-conformal coverage
$\Pr[|\widehat\Delta-\Delta|\le\varepsilon]\ge1-\alpha$ under exchangeability, while the per-cell
grids use the leave-one-out (jackknife) radius, whose calibration-set coverage is $\ge1-\alpha$ by
construction and distribution-free only asymptotically (the finite-sample jackknife$+$ of
Barber et al.\ gives $1-2\alpha$; not implemented).

\begin{algorithm}[t]
\caption{\textsc{KGA} (Knowability-Guided Adaptation)}
\label{alg:kga}
\SetKwInOut{Input}{Input}
\SetKwInOut{Guarantee}{Guarantee}
\Input{frozen $f_0$; candidate adaptation $f_a$; unlabeled target batch $X_{1:m}$; calibrated benefit model $\widehat\Delta(\cdot)$; level $\alpha$.}
Extract label-free evidence $Z=\phi(X_{1:m},f_0,f_a)$ (entropy, confidence, predicted-class balance and its drift, marginal-prediction KL, update norm, detector disagreement)\;
Estimate benefit $\widehat\Delta\leftarrow\widehat\Delta(Z)$ and radius $\varepsilon$ with out-of-fold conformal coverage $\Pr[\,|\widehat\Delta-\Delta|\le\varepsilon\,]\ge1-\alpha$ (exact under a clean split)\;
\eIf{$\widehat\Delta-\varepsilon>0$}{
  \Return \textsc{adapt} (deploy $f_a$)\;
}{
  \eIf{$\widehat\Delta+\varepsilon<0$}{
    \Return \textsc{freeze} (keep $f_0$)\;
  }{
    \Return \textsc{abstain} (keep $f_0$, flag for review)\;
  }
}
\Guarantee{the false-adapt and false-freeze rates are each $\le\alpha$ (Thm~\ref{thm:cert}).}
\end{algorithm}

\paragraph{Assumptions.} \textsc{KGA} inherits exactly the assumptions of
Theorem~\ref{thm:cert}: (i) a labeled validation/calibration sample of paired benefits that is
exchangeable with deployment, from which $\varepsilon$ is built; (ii) bounded per-sample
benefits; and (iii) an evidence map $Z$ that is \emph{risk-aligned} (it brackets $\sign\Delta$;
\S\ref{sec:def}). When (iii) fails---evidence indistinguishable across opposite-benefit
worlds---the certificate cannot fire and \textsc{KGA} \emph{abstains}, which
Corollary~\ref{cor:forced-abstain} shows is mandatory, not merely conservative. No target
labels are used at any step.

\paragraph{Cost.} \textsc{KGA} adds no training to $f_0$ and introduces no new adaptation
mechanism: per decision it costs one forward pass of $f_0$ and $f_a$ on the batch plus an
evaluation of the lightweight benefit model. It is therefore a drop-in safety wrapper around
any already-deployed TTA candidate.

\paragraph{The threshold is not tuned, and it is not the drift budget.} \textsc{KGA}'s commit
boundary is not a swept hyperparameter: $\varepsilon$ is the $(1-\alpha)$ out-of-fold residual
quantile, fixed on the calibration split and \emph{never} selected on target-test data.
$\varepsilon$ is a \emph{conformal radius on the estimation error}
$|\widehat\Delta-\Delta|$; it is \emph{not} an estimate of the drift budget $\beta$, and no
statement in this paper licenses reading it as one. The two objects answer different questions:
$\beta$ bounds an unidentified population quantity that Theorem~\ref{thm:beta-minimax} shows no
label-free procedure can supply, while $\varepsilon$ bounds a finite-sample fluctuation that
exchangeable calibration data does supply. What separates \textsc{KGA} from confidence-thresholding
(ATC) and agreement-trend selection~\cite{kim2024taol} is therefore not the value of its threshold
but the shape of its output: an abstain region, and a finite-sample false-adapt guarantee on the
commitments it does make. Those methods fit a scalar against a held-out accuracy signal and
\emph{predict accuracy}; \textsc{KGA} certifies the benefit \emph{sign} and \textsc{abstains} where
Corollary~\ref{cor:forced-abstain} proves no label-free rule can decide. ``Estimate accuracy then
threshold'' has no abstain region and no false-adapt control.

\section{Experiments}\label{sec:exp}
The empirical claim is narrow and we test it in that order. \textsc{KGA} is a safety layer: it
should beat both fixed policies only where harmful adaptation is frequent and detectable, and do no
harm elsewhere. We first establish the mechanism in a controlled label-free setting (this section
and the online streams that follow), then carry the headline to image corruption grids and held-out
natural shifts (\S\ref{sec:external}--\S\ref{sec:natural-suite}). The anomaly-detection and regression
tracks below are breadth evidence that the certificate's behavior is mechanism-agnostic, not a
competing headline.

\subsection{What the certificate buys: the decision-yield / false-adapt frontier}\label{sec:decision-value}
The certificate's guarantee is a trade, and this subsection prices it rather than asserting it. The
objection it answers is that no-harm is trivially achievable by abstaining. In this system an
\textsc{abstain} realises the frozen model, so always-abstain is numerically identical to
always-freeze and inherits its regret exactly; whether that is cheap or ruinous is a per-track
empirical fact.

\emph{Replay integrity.} Sweeping a radius multiplier $\kappa$ in the rule ``\textsc{adapt} iff
$\widehat\Delta-\kappa\varepsilon>0$, \textsc{freeze} iff $\widehat\Delta+\kappa\varepsilon<0$''
reproduces the \emph{shipped} decision on $7{,}365/7{,}365$ cells at $\kappa=1$ across all three
per-cell tracks, so the sweep is a sweep of the deployed system and not a reimplementation of it.
Every policy in the sweep is a function of $\widehat\Delta$, $\varepsilon$ and $Z$ alone; the label
arrays are registered at load time and checked by identity, buffer and value against every array a
policy consumes.

\emph{What it buys.} On CIFAR-10-C the certificate cuts regret $5.00\times$ below the better fixed
policy and $85\times$ below the worse one, at $\mathrm{FA}_{\mathrm u}=0/6480$ (Wilson $95\%$ upper
bound $0.0006$); per adapter the decision accounting is $1{,}113$ and $1{,}244$ \textsc{adapt}
decisions with zero false adaptations (Clopper--Pearson $95\%$ upper bounds $0.0027$ and $0.0024$
on the conditional rate). Always-abstain is the \emph{worst} row of the table, so the abstention
objection does not survive contact with the numbers on this track. On ImageNet-C the ratio to the
best fixed policy is $2.90\times$.

\emph{Where it commits.} Among the $4{,}330/6{,}480$ CIFAR-10-C cells where the certificate
commits, mean regret removed relative to the better fixed policy is $+0.00940$ against a
permutation null of $-0.01850$ that holds the number of \textsc{adapt} and \textsc{freeze}
commitments fixed and reassigns them at random ($p<0.0002$, $5{,}000$ permutations); the mean true
effect $|\Delta|$ on committed cells is $0.1987$ against $0.0081$ on declined cells, a ratio of
$24.5\times$ that no random subset of the same size reached in $5{,}000$ draws. The certificate
fires on the cells that matter.

\emph{Against cheap heuristics.} Held to $\mathrm{FA}_{\mathrm u}\le0.10$ and tuned in
\emph{hindsight} on the test labels, the best of four single-signal label-free rules
(\texttt{marginal\_KL}, a BN-statistics drift proxy) reaches regret $0.00472$ on CIFAR-10-C against
the certificate's $0.00150$---a $3.1\times$ gap---while making $324$ harmful adaptations out of
$6{,}480$ cells ($\mathrm{FA}_{\mathrm u}=0.050$) against the certificate's zero. Under a fair
seed split the same heuristic reaches $0.00301$ at $\mathrm{FA}_{\mathrm u}=0.032$, against the
certificate's $0.00152$ at $0.0000$ on the same held-out seeds. \textbf{The advantage over cheap
heuristics is an advantage in false-adapt rate, and it converts into a regret advantage only where
adaptation is genuinely two-sided.}

\emph{Where it buys nothing, stated as such.} On ImageNet-R the certificate is $1.76\times$
\emph{worse} than always-adapt ($0.01120$ versus $0.00636$). It buys
$\mathrm{FA}_{\mathrm u}=0.0021$ against always-adapt's $0.2021$---real safety---and pays $0.48$
accuracy points per cell for it on a track where adaptation essentially never hurts. Per backbone,
the $|\Delta|$ concentration ratio is $0.71$--$1.57$ with permutation $p$ between $0.054$ and
$1.000$: not one backbone is distinguishable from random placement, and the value per committed
decision is exactly $0.0000$ on $9$ of $10$ backbones, because when the better fixed policy is
always-adapt an \textsc{adapt} commitment reproduces it exactly. This is the clearest single
counterexample to any blanket ``uniformly no-harm on natural shifts'' reading, and it comes from an
artifact this paper promotes.

\emph{The shipped operating point is not regret-optimal.} On CIFAR-10-C it is dominated by
$\kappa=0.9$ (yield $0.684$, regret $0.00134$, $\mathrm{FA}_{\mathrm u}=0.0$ for both), about
$10\%$ of regret; and against a perfect ordering of cells by the regret a correct commitment
removes, the best achievable regret at the certificate's own yield is $0.00020$, so it sits
$7.50\times$ above the information-theoretic ceiling at equal yield ($2.79\times$ on ImageNet-C,
$2.64\times$ on ImageNet-R). $\alpha=0.10$ is defensible as a pre-registered safety choice and is
not defensible as a regret-optimal one.

\emph{Robustness of the guarantee to the calibration split.} Recalibrating $\varepsilon$
leave-one-corruption-family-out---six folds instead of $432$, so no cell is calibrated on its own
corruption family---inflates the radius by $4.3\times$--$6.9\times$ (from $0.015$--$0.022$ to
$0.092$--$0.112$), drops the commitment rate from $0.509$--$0.600$ to $0.398$--$0.417$, and worsens
regret by $3.4\times$--$7.6\times$, across Tent and EATA and five seeds. In all ten runs
$\mathrm{FA}_{\mathrm u}=0.0000$. \emph{The false-adapt guarantee survives a calibration split the
shipped protocol does not make; the decision yield and the regret advantage do not survive it
intact, and we report both halves.} (Ranges are under the exact-rank quantile; under the shipped
interpolated rank they are $4.3\times$--$7.0\times$ and $3.2\times$--$7.5\times$.) Artifact:
\texttt{frontier\_sweep\_v1/out\_cifar\_loco\_tent\_eata.json}.

\paragraph{Safety on the clean suite.} With candidate $f_a=$ rank-normalized logistic

\paragraph{Online non-stationary regime: cross-regime robustness (the decisive test).}
The single-shot regimes above are each dominated by one outcome, so a fixed policy is
already near-optimal. The real test is whether \emph{any single fixed policy is robust
across opposite regimes}. We evaluate \emph{online, continual} TTA on non-stationary
CIFAR-10 streams (ResNet-18, $0.874$ clean acc), under two schedules:
a \textbf{helpful-dominated} stream (mostly recoverable corruptions) and a
\textbf{harm-dominated} stream (long ``trap'' runs---single-class label shift, near-pure
noise, small batches---so continual Tent accumulates damage and \emph{collapses}).
\textsc{KGA} runs the certificate online: it commits a continual update only when it does
not degrade a clean labelled validation probe (K-Bound assumes labelled validation, no
\emph{target} labels), else freezes or abstains. Results over $3$ seeds
(Table~\ref{tab:online}, Fig.~\ref{fig:online}):

\emph{No fixed policy is robust.} Always-adapt is best in the helpful regime ($0.548$) but
\textbf{collapses} in the harm regime ($0.339$, $-0.10$ below freezing). Always-freeze is
best in the harm regime ($0.440$) but \textbf{forgoes gains} in the helpful regime
($0.472$). \textsc{KGA} is near-best in \emph{both} ($0.553$ and $0.426$) and never
collapses, giving the \textbf{highest mean across regimes} ($0.490$ vs always-freeze
$0.456$ vs always-adapt $0.444$). Honestly: within a single stream \textsc{KGA} ties the
better fixed policy (it edges always-adapt by $+0.005$ in the helpful regime and trails
always-freeze by $0.014$ in the harm regime); its advantage is \emph{robustness}---it is
the only policy that is near-oracle in both regimes, which is exactly the knowability
thesis (route per regime rather than commit to one policy).

\paragraph{CIFAR-10-C stress grid: the harmful regime (the decisive test).}

\texttt{contrast}, \texttt{defocus\_blur}, \texttt{fog}, \texttt{gaussian\_noise},
\texttt{jpeg\_compression}, \texttt{pixelate} --- crossing severity $\{1,5\}$ (two of five)
$\times$ batch
$\{$large-iid $200$, small $16$, tiny $8\}\times$ composition $\{$iid, class-imbalanced,
single-class/label-shift$\}\times$ update $\{$mild $10$ steps; aggressive $50$ steps,
lr $2.5{\times}10^{-3}\}$, $2$ repeats ($432$ conditions/method). The corruption and severity
subsets are the driver's \texttt{--quick} selection and every run manifest for this grid records
\texttt{"quick": true}; the nine omitted corruptions include the remainder of the noise family
(\texttt{shot\_noise}, \texttt{impulse\_noise}), the weather corruptions (\texttt{snow},
\texttt{frost}, \texttt{brightness}), and \texttt{glass\_blur}, \texttt{motion\_blur},
\texttt{zoom\_blur}, \texttt{elastic\_transform}. An earlier version of this sentence said the grid
ran ``over the official CIFAR-10-C corruptions'', which implied all fifteen and was wrong.
Note also that the two repeats are exact design-point replicates correlating at $0.948$--$0.999$,
so the effective number of independent conditions is at most $216$.
Benefit $B$ is measured on
a class-balanced held-out eval set so collapse is real accuracy loss, not a batch artifact;
\textsc{KGA} sees only label-free $Z$. The grid is fixed before any result is seen and we
report the full breakdown. The single-class/aggressive/severe cells genuinely collapse:
the retained harmful base rates ($B{<}0$) are $34\%$ (Tent) and $27\%$ (EATA).

re-analysis with a \emph{self-normalized} CEI statistic---$\tau'=\hat\tau$ divided by the

sample size, and agreement strength---revises the mechanism (validated at level and power on a

selection-margin quality, not H. Consistently, a Bonferroni per-candidate benefit-certificate

deployed adapter, and the conformal certificate calibrated on source$+$target-\emph{val} and the
held-out test evaluated \emph{once}. The $14$-candidate panel augments
Tent/EATA/SAR$\times\{$online,episodic$\}\times\{$mild,aggressive$\}$ with two \emph{inference-only}
priors --- a label-shift MLLS logit correction and a damped ``conservative'' correction (no
parameter update) --- over $2$ seeds $\times\{$iid,imbalanced,single-class$\}\times\{$tiny,small$\}$

evidence feature separates harm \emph{in-sample} (AUC $0.81$/$0.79$, $n{\approx}432$ --- no longer a
small-$n$ artifact), but the \emph{source-calibrated} certificate \emph{anti-transfers}
(transfer-AUC $0.33$ val / $0.49$ test) --- the same source$\to$OOD non-transfer that caps the other

\subsection{Natural-shift suite: do the harmful regimes occur on real data?}\label{sec:natural-suite}
The synthetic stress grids above manufacture harm by construction. The sharper question is whether
the \emph{harmful, detectable} regime---the wedge where the certificate is meant to earn its
keep---arises on \emph{natural} shift, and whether the operating point we calibrated on synthetic
data fires there. We probe this with two real shifts that bracket the detectability frontier.

\paragraph{Camelyon17 composition-stress grid: real harm that is label-free \emph{detectable}.}
\emph{Two tracks, kept distinct:} the 4-seed Camelyon17 TTA table
(Table~\ref{tab:camelyon}) is \emph{helpful-dominated}---adaptation helps on every
seed, always-adapt is the oracle, and \textsc{KGA} trails it by ${\approx}0.02$
regret---whereas the separate multi-candidate audit below shows harmful,
\emph{detectable} natural-shift cells exist, but is not yet a policy win because the
current $\tau$ threshold abstains at this operating point.
Re-running the hospital-shift candidates over a composition stress grid (iid / class-imbalanced /
single-class $\times$ \{test, val, id-val\} domains $\times$ mild/aggressive updates $\times$ $4$
seeds; $72$ conditions $\times$ $6$ TTA variants ${=}\,432$ cells, debug-scale
$n_{\mathrm{eval}}{=}256$/cell) turns the benign picture of Table~\ref{tab:camelyon} into a
\emph{mixed} one: $28.7\%$ of cells are harmful ($\bar B{=}{+}0.039$), and---crucially---the harm is
label-free \emph{detectable}: the best single evidence feature reaches harm-AUC $0.78$, and the
conformal certificate's own $-\widehat B$ score reaches $0.91$. This is the first natural-shift
confirmation that the harmful-and-detectable regime the theory targets is not a synthetic artifact.
\emph{Yet at our current operating point \textsc{KGA} abstains rather than commits.} The
multi-candidate route's gate passes on only $2.8\%$ of conditions---the real multi-candidate
statistic $\bar\tau{=}0.89$ exceeds the synthetic-calibrated threshold $\tau^\star{=}0.52$, so the
router \textsc{abstain}s on $61/72$ conditions, \textsc{freeze}s $10$, and commits a single adapt
(the six hospital-TTA variants are co-trained on one backbone---the very correlation the gate is
built to catch). The smooth-drift fallback likewise stays conservative ($21$ freeze / $51$ abstain,
bracket coverage $0.44$): its source-concept term is a conservative $f_0$ surrogate, so the
smooth-drift hypothesis is not cleanly satisfied here. Honest reading: Camelyon17 supplies the
missing \emph{harmful-and-detectable} natural-shift data point, but under the \emph{frozen,
Camelyon-free} $\tau^\star{=}0.52$ \textsc{KGA} does \emph{not} beat both---multi-candidate
regret-to-oracle $0.076$ vs always-freeze $0.071$ vs always-adapt $0.005$ (the worst of the three:
abstaining forgoes this helpful-leaning panel's gains, and the lone commit is harmful). The
$\tau^\star$ fit on synthetic grids is mis-matched to this panel's $\tau$ scale ($\bar\tau{=}0.89$);
\emph{recalibrating $\tau^\star$ on Camelyon17 itself would forfeit its role as an
external-validation set}, so we do not. The honest open direction is a \emph{scale-invariant},
source-calibrated $\tau$ statistic (future work), not per-dataset retuning. Debug-scale, single
protocol; reported as regime-existence evidence, not a policy win.

\paragraph{Diagnostic ladder (Protocols B/F; not headline).}
Earlier Camelyon runs (sparse $Z$, multicandidate $\tau^\star$, synthetic $\varepsilon$ transplant)
explain failed operating points; they motivated Protocol~G. Protocol~F route audits on the same GPU
records show multicandidate routing fails; GBR+global and PPI+Mondrian variants beat both only on
the same domain-pooled set as Protocol~G (so they inherit its \texttt{id\_val} artifact) and are
not the headline method; the held-out headline natural-shift results are Protocols~H v2 / M v2
(no-harm under a valid out-of-fold radius; Tables~\ref{tab:iwildcam-H}, \ref{tab:officehome-M}),
with Protocol~G reclassified to no-harm (Table~\ref{tab:camelyon17-G}).

\paragraph{Operating-point repair: $\varepsilon$ recalibrated, $\tau^\star$ left alone (a calibration \emph{diagnostic}, not external validation).}
The latent-win diagnosis above is a calibration claim, so we test it directly --- without
touching $\tau^\star$ (lowering it on a co-trained panel would defeat the CEI guard). \emph{Because
this step re-estimates $\varepsilon$ on a Camelyon17 split held out by seed, it touches the external
panel and is therefore a calibration diagnostic, not external validation---and, as shown next, it is
a negative in any case.} The
single-candidate radius $\varepsilon$ was fit on \emph{synthetic} grids; transplanted to
Camelyon17 it violates the Theorem~\ref{thm:cert} exchangeability premise. Re-estimating
$\varepsilon$ from a Camelyon17 calibration split held out \emph{by seed} restores that premise
(calibration and test residuals then share one law). We use all $\binom{4}{2}{=}6$ assignments of
$2$ calibration $+\,2$ test seeds; on each, fit the benefit estimator on the calibration seeds,
set $\varepsilon{=}\widehat{Q}_{1-\alpha}(|\widehat\Delta-B|)$ on \emph{their} residuals
($\alpha{=}0.10$ fixed), freeze it, and decide on the test seeds. The repair is mechanically
real: $\varepsilon$ collapses from the synthetic $\approx0.060$ to $0.030$ and is \emph{stable}
(range $0.007$, CV $0.11$), so the certificate now \emph{commits} (coverage $0.65$) instead of
abstaining. But it is not a level-$\alpha$ win: test-split mean regret is \textsc{KGA} $0.014$
$[0.007,0.021]$ versus always-freeze $0.052$ $[0.037,0.067]$ (cleanly beaten) but always-adapt
$0.013$ $[0.010,0.016]$ (CIs \emph{overlap} --- the panel is helpful-dominated), while
false-adapt is $0.185$ $[0.159,0.211]$, $\approx\!1.9{\times}\,\alpha$. Honest verdict: the harm
is label-free \emph{detectable} (AUC $0.91$) but \emph{not certifiable} at $\alpha{=}0.10$ at this
debug-scale $n_{\mathrm{eval}}{=}256$/cell --- a precise negative. Closing it needs more samples
per cell (tighter residuals) or a harm-skewed panel, not a smaller $\alpha$ or a moved $\tau^\star$.

\paragraph{Pre-registered full-scale re-run ($n_{\mathrm{eval}}{=}1024$, $5$ seeds): the gap
persists.} We pre-registered the $1/\sqrt{n}$ test \emph{before} observing any result
in Protocol~B: quadrupling the per-cell eval
pool to $n_{\mathrm{eval}}{=}1024$ should halve the conformal radius (predicted ratio
$\sqrt{256/1024}{=}0.5$) and pull false-adapt to $\le\alpha{=}0.10$. The $\alpha$-fixed, frozen-$\varepsilon$
seed-split rule was held identical. \emph{Outcome: the prediction is not confirmed.} The full-scale
runner serialized only one aggregate $(Z,B)$ point per seed (tent, eata), \emph{not} the $72{\times}6$
composition grid, so the $1/\sqrt{n}$ radius claim could be evaluated only at coarse per-seed
granularity ($5$ points, $\binom{5}{2}{=}10$ calibration splits). At that granularity the radius did
\emph{not} shrink ($\varepsilon{=}0.029$ tent / $0.038$ eata vs the debug $\varepsilon{\approx}0.030$;
ratio $\approx\!1.0$, not $0.5$) and false-adapt did \emph{not} fall below $\alpha$ ($0.33$ on the
committed seeds vs the archived $0.185$). \textsc{KGA} still cleanly beats always-freeze (regret
$0.020$ vs $0.027$ tent) but trails always-adapt (helpful-dominated panel), at commit rate $0.60$.
\textbf{Honest verdict (\textsc{Fails}, by the pre-registered criteria):} the
detectability--certifiability gap is \emph{not} closed by sample size alone---it is a deeper open
problem. A bias--variance decomposition of the radius \emph{explains why} the $1/\sqrt{n}$ prediction
failed. Splitting the eps-recal residual into estimator model error and
binomial measurement noise ($\sigma^2_{\mathrm{meas}}{=}a_0(1{-}a_0)/n{+}a_a(1{-}a_a)/n$,
$n_{\mathrm{eff}}{=}256$) on a leave-one-seed cross-fit gives an observed radius
$\varepsilon_{256}{=}0.112$ against a measurement floor $\varepsilon_{\mathrm{meas}}(256){=}0.044$
(ratio $2.55\times$), with only a fraction $\approx0.16$ of residual variance attributable to
measurement noise. The radius is thus \emph{bias-limited}: dominated by $\widehat{B}(Z)$ model error
(calibration-drift bias), which does not shrink with eval $n$. Hence the variance-limited target
$\varepsilon_{\mathrm{meas}}(1024){=}0.022$ is unreachable and a fair re-run \emph{cannot} close the
radius---the $\alpha$-fixed certifiability of this natural shift is bias-limited, not
sample-size-limited. The open problem is therefore reducing estimator bias (tying back to the
$\gamma$-frontier), not collecting more eval samples. (Cross-check: the per-seed $|B|$ spread at $1024$
is $0.71\times$ that at $256$, not the $0.5$ a variance-limited halving requires, though $n{=}10$ is
weak.)

\paragraph{The pair, read together.} Camelyon17 (detectable harm, AUC $0.78$) and ImageNet-R
(\emph{un}detectable harm, AUC $0.66$; Table~\ref{tab:imagenetr}) bracket the frontier of
Theorem~\ref{thm:frontier}: real harm exists on both ($28.7\%$ / $13.9\%$ of cells), but only on
Camelyon17 is it label-free separable. \textsc{KGA} abstains on both---\emph{correctly} on
ImageNet-R, where Corollary~\ref{cor:forced-abstain} makes abstention mandatory for any valid
certificate, and \emph{conservatively} on Camelyon17, where the harm is detectable but the
operating point is not yet tuned to it. Two honest conclusions follow: (i) the harmful regimes the
theory characterises do occur on natural shift, on both sides of the detectability frontier; and
(ii) turning the detectable side into a committed natural-shift win requires a $\tau$ statistic
whose operating point transfers \emph{without} tuning on the target---a \emph{scale-invariant,
source-calibrated} $\tau$ (future work); per-dataset recalibration on Camelyon17 would forfeit its
external-validation role and is not a legitimate path.

\subsection{Cross-protocol aggregate (out-of-fold re-run)}\label{sec:mixedstream}
Each single dataset above is \emph{one-sided}: Camelyon17 is adapt-favorable
(on its held-out OOD cells freezing leaves regret ${\approx}0.11$), whereas OfficeHome and
iWildCam are freeze-favorable (adapting leaves regret $0.047$ and $0.103$). On a one-sided
stream the better trivial policy is already near-oracle, so \textsc{KGA}'s simultaneous margin
over \emph{both} is necessarily small. To evaluate the policy benefit of per-condition
adapt/freeze/abstain routing across heterogeneous regimes, we \emph{aggregate} the held-out test
conditions of the three dev-locked datasets into a single stream ($n{=}143$); to avoid the
Protocol-G pooling artifact, \emph{Camelyon17 enters through its genuine OOD \texttt{test}/\texttt{val}
cells only} ($36$ cells; in-distribution \texttt{id\_val} excluded). This is a \emph{cross-protocol
aggregate}, \emph{not} one universal gate calibrated once and transferred across datasets: each
dataset keeps its own locked dev-calibrated gate, and we make no cross-dataset transfer claim.
Because this aggregate genuinely mixes helpful conditions (Camelyon) with harmful ones (Office-Home,
iWildCam), it is the one setting in our suite where per-condition routing could in principle beat
both fixed policies.

\paragraph{Out-of-fold re-run (\texttt{mixed\_protocol\_oof\_v2}).}
The earlier figures for this aggregate (regret $0.0026$; ${\sim}24\times$ below always-adapt,
${\sim}13\times$ below always-freeze) used an \emph{in-sample} conformal radius---the calibration bug
corrected in \S\ref{sec:natural-suite}---and are \textbf{withdrawn}. Under leave-one-out conformal
calibration per dataset (\texttt{scripts/mixed\_stream\_kbound.py}), the pooled stream yields mean
regret $\mathrm{regret}_{\textsc{kga}}{=}0.0059$ vs.\ $0.0632$ (always-adapt) and $0.0342$
(always-freeze), with pooled false-adapt $0$. Bootstrap regret gaps: vs.\ always-freeze
$+0.0283$ $[0.019,\,0.038]$; vs.\ always-adapt $+0.0573$ $[0.043,\,0.072]$; both $95\%$ CIs exclude
zero (\texttt{research\_lock/KBOUND\_MIXED\_STREAM\_v2.json}). This is a \textbf{beats-both} on a
\emph{constructed} heterogeneous aggregate---not a natural-shift headline. Magnitudes are smaller
than the withdrawn in-sample-radius multipliers; we do not report those ratios as findings.

\paragraph{Universal single gate (pre-registered, 2026-07-03).} The per-dataset-gate caveat above
is closed by two pre-registered arms (\texttt{research\_lock/WIN\_HUNT\_v2\_PROTOCOL.yaml},
\texttt{WIN\_HUNT\_v3\_PROTOCOL.yaml}; bars frozen before scoring, each scored once). \emph{One}
GBR benefit model and \emph{one} pooled leave-one-out conformal radius, fit on the pooled dev
records over the shared base-11 evidence and applied unchanged to the pooled held-out conditions:
on the three-source stream ($n{=}143$), regret $0.0182$ vs.\ always-adapt $0.0632$ and
always-freeze $0.0342$, both $95\%$ CIs excluding zero, $\mathrm{FA}_{\mathrm u}{=}0.000$; on the
seven-source extension adding all four PACS leave-one-domain splits ($n{=}359$,
$\varepsilon_{\mathrm{universal}}{=}0.096$), regret $0.0183$ vs.\ $0.0404$ / $0.0282$, both CIs
excluding zero, $\mathrm{FA}_{\mathrm u}{=}0.000$. Per-source regrets show the mechanism: the gate
rides adaptation on Camelyon17, freezing on iWildCam and Office-Home, and no-harm ties across
PACS. Honest scope: the stream composition is researcher-pooled (datasets and splits locked
before these arms were registered); this is a certified heterogeneous-deployment win under a
universal calibration, not a single-dataset natural-shift win. Verdicts:
\texttt{research\_lock/WIN\_HUNT\_v2\_ARM\_A\_result.json},
\texttt{WIN\_HUNT\_v3\_ARM\_E\_result.json}.

\paragraph{Report-all-arms: the rich-evidence Camelyon re-run FAILs (pre-registered, scored once).}
The companion GPU arm (\texttt{WIN\_HUNT\_v2} Arm~B) asked whether \emph{richer evidence} unlocks
the single-dataset Camelyon17 win that v1 could not produce: a fresh $4$-seed run
(Tent/EATA/SAR, $n{=}432$ records) logging the full $30$-dimensional evidence (base panel $+$
$13$-dim \texttt{ev2}: MaNo~\cite{xie2024mano}, nuclear norm~\cite{deng2023confidence},

($\mathrm{FA}_{\mathrm u}{=}0.044\le\alpha{=}0.10$) and \textsc{KGA} beats always-freeze
(gap $-0.0223$, CI $[-0.0264,-0.0183]$), but the $\tau'$ gate rejects risk-alignment on $77\%$
of gated records, driving abstention to $59.5\%$; on this helpful-dominated dataset the
abstention premium costs real accuracy, and \textsc{KGA}'s regret ($0.0292$) is \emph{worse}
than always-adapt ($0.0129$; gap CI $[0.0095,0.0231]$ excludes zero). Richer evidence therefore
does not move Camelyon17 across the frontier---the v1 mechanism (evidence-limited, not
gate-limited) stands, and off the identifiable regime the certificate behaves exactly as the
theory prescribes: it stays valid and pays abstention cost rather than committing uncertifiable
adaptations. The headline Camelyon17 claim is unchanged (Protocol~G no-harm under the canonical
dev-locked gate); this arm is reported because its bar was frozen before scoring
(\texttt{experiments/kbound/results/natural\_win\_v2\_camelyon/result\_a4929009.json}).

(0) \textbf{The population frontier does not operationalize, and $\beta$ is not estimable from
development data.} This is the paper's own negative result (\S\ref{sec:beta-sweep-long}). Declaring
$\beta$ the way this paper recommends---a high quantile of the realized drift on source-like
development cells---gives a budget $1.4\times$ to $50\times$ smaller than soundness requires on
CIFAR-10-C, where $24$--$73\%$ of deployment cells violate the declared class and committed actions
are wrong at $0.4$--$16.6\%$ against a promised $0\%$; on ImageNet-C a sufficiently large budget is
derivable and returns zero commitments on all $405$ cells in half the configurations. Consequently
we withdraw the operational reading of Theorem~\ref{thm:frontier} while keeping its impossibility
content, whose proof is untouched, and we do not offer $\beta$ selection as a solved deployment
step. Limitations of that test itself: two benchmarks with $6$ and $3$ corruption families, so
family-clustered intervals are reported but are not primary; the benefit rather than the
disagreement scale, since $\mu_T(D)$ is not recorded in these artifacts; the minimum sound budget is
an oracle diagnostic no deployer could compute; and regret resolves \textsc{abstain} to
\textsc{freeze}, which penalizes abstention heavily---the soundness conclusions do not depend on
that convention, the regret comparisons do.

(1) Scope of the empirical win: on the CIFAR-10-C \emph{stress} grid \textsc{KGA} beats both
trivial policies for Tent and EATA at $0\%$ false-adapt (Table~\ref{tab:decisive}). The
CIFAR-10-C SAR artifact is withheld because its archived
aggregate does not reproduce from the current seed-0 replay. On ImageNet-C
(Table~\ref{tab:imagenetc}) the pattern \emph{flips}---
Tent and EATA are robust so \textsc{KGA} ties always-adapt, while SAR collapses and \textsc{KGA}
beats both---a win we confirmed survives a \emph{mechanism-faithful} SAR re-implementation
(Table~\ref{tab:imagenetc-faithful}; SAR harmful on $44\%$ of cells, regret-to-oracle $0.023$ vs
$0.112$/$0.027$), so it is not an implementation artifact, modulo SAR's official gentler-LR,
\texttt{layer4}-frozen schedule, which we have not yet run. On \emph{per-corruption} CIFAR-10-C and within a single online stream (both
helpful-dominated) it again \emph{ties} the better fixed policy. The common rule across all of
these: \textsc{KGA} approximately matches the better trivial policy, paying at most a small
abstention cost when it declines a helpful but uncertifiable update. It \emph{strictly} wins only
where catastrophic, detectable harm is actually present---and which method collapses depends on the
benchmark. A tighter online (streaming) certificate is future work.
(2) \textbf{Real-shift scope: no single-dataset natural beats-both.} Under a valid out-of-fold
conformal radius, no natural shift is a beats-both; the four one-sided locked tracks are
\emph{no-harm} and the remaining three (PACS, ImageNet-R, CIFAR-10.1) are nulls or declared
failures that lose to always-adapt --- ``no-harm'' does not extend to them. Two fully instrumented
pre-registered attempts (\texttt{NATURAL\_WIN\_PROTOCOL\_v1}) fail their frozen bars with the
false-adapt guarantee intact, so single-dataset beats-both wins remain confined to the synthetic
stress grids (CIFAR-10-C, ImageNet-C SAR). On \emph{pooled} heterogeneous natural deployments,
however, the pre-registered universal-gate arms are CI-robust beats-both wins
(\S\ref{sec:mixedstream}). Office-Home (Protocol~M v2) and
iWildCam (Protocol~H v2) beat always-adapt with CIs excluding zero but \emph{tie} always-freeze
(Tables~\ref{tab:iwildcam-H}, \ref{tab:officehome-M}); their earlier in-sample-radius beats-both was
a calibration bug, corrected here. Camelyon17 Protocol~G is likewise \emph{not} a win: its pooled
beats-both included in-distribution \texttt{id\_val} cells, and on genuine OOD test \textsc{KGA}
ties always-adapt (Table~\ref{tab:camelyon17-G}, no-harm).
Earlier Camelyon diagnostics (\S\ref{sec:natural-suite}) and multicandidate routes are not
competing headline claims. \textbf{ImageNet-C} harmful SAR win is synthetic corruption.
\textbf{CIFAR-10.1} is a within-seed low-margin probe; cross-seed transfer fails
(\S\ref{sec:excluded-wins}). \textbf{ImageNet-R} is a completed weak-evidence diagnostic: its
estimated margin does not clear the empirical radius, so no candidate supports a promoted
confidence-robust beats-both claim. This null does not establish structural non-identifiability;
weak benefit estimation or calibration transfer could produce the same observation. The deployed
\textbf{Office-Home} adapter audit (Table~\ref{tab:officehome}) is an invalid-transfer regime,
distinct from Protocol~M v2.
\textbf{PACS} is a completed three-seed domain-generalization breadth check and remains a null
diagnostic; no leave-one-domain-out target supports a promoted multiseed win (Table~\ref{tab:pacs}).
We caution that the conformal radius can still fail to transfer under \emph{severe} geographic or
sensor shift, where in-distribution calibration need not hold out-of-distribution; characterizing
that failure mode is future work.
(3) The conformal radius assumes task exchangeability.
(4) The sign-of-difference characterization is proved for binary, multiclass, and
regression (Lemma~\ref{lem:reduction}); the unconditional label-free \emph{bracketing}
problem is resolved \emph{negatively} by Theorem~\ref{thm:conj1-dichotomy}: no
evidence-definable assumption class can identify the benefit sign. On the margin-monotone
relative-calibration class the one-bit supplement certifies the sign \emph{unconditionally}, and
that class is \emph{a} weakest such falsifiable class under a general-position assumption
(Theorem~\ref{thm:cmono-weakest}); the \emph{unconditional} weakest classes are characterized as an
explicit finite family of dominance polytopes (Theorem~\ref{thm:uncond-weakest}), with no unique
weakest class and General Position recovered as the collapsing face.
(5) \textsc{KGA}'s value scales with how often catastrophic, detectable harm
occurs---precisely the quantity the theory characterizes. On helpful-dominated shifts
\textsc{KGA} \emph{ties} always-adapt; this is the certificate declining to commit a harmful update,
not a failure---it approximately matches the better policy (at most a small abstention cost), so a
near-tie where always-adapt is already oracle-optimal is the intended outcome, and the value proposition is
conditional (insurance against detectable harm).
(6) Abstention lowers \emph{decision} coverage by construction but never withholds a
prediction: an \textsc{abstain} routes to the safe default (keep $f_0$, the source model), so
every input still receives an output, and the coverage/false-adapt trade-off is explicit and
tunable via the $\alpha$ safety/coverage sweep reported above.
(7) \textbf{These limitations are the frontier, not flaws.} Points (1) and (4)--(6) are not
independent weaknesses but one phenomenon---the benefit-sign frontier (Theorem~\ref{thm:frontier})
seen from the operating side. Tying always-adapt on a helpful-dominated shift is the no-harm
guarantee \emph{holding} (one cannot beat an oracle that is already always-adapt); ImageNet-R in the
\emph{unknowable} regime is the impossibility direction firing where the observable margin falls
below the drift budget and \emph{no} label-free rule can decide the sign
(Theorem~\ref{thm:conj1-dichotomy}); and the coverage reduction is the \emph{mandatory} abstention of
Corollary~\ref{cor:forced-abstain}, not a tuning loss---committing there would be a false-adapt. None
can be removed without violating the impossibility result: a method that ``won everywhere'' would be
claiming to decide the sign of an unidentifiable quantity. The single axis that improves all three is
a \emph{richer label-free evidence map}---raising the observable margin $|M|$ so more shifts clear the
budget (more knowable shifts, fewer abstentions, higher coverage)---together with a \emph{tighter
radius} (more calibration data or a separately validated finite-sample method) that reclaims knowable cells abstained only out of
conservatism. Both are bounded by the frontier they cannot cross.

(8) \textbf{Replication and maturity hierarchy.} All promoted empirical artifacts were produced
internally; the repository freezes an external procedure in
\texttt{INDEPENDENT\_REPLICATION\_PROTOCOL.md}, but this release does not claim independent
replication. The primary contribution is the binary split-observable frontier, its finite-sample
certificate, and the registered empirical panel. Multicandidate, anytime, multiclass-capacity,
regression, and auditable-budget results are companion extensions with explicit conditions and open
formal clauses, not additional headline claims. PACS and ImageNet-R remain completed null/weak-evidence
diagnostics. CIFAR-10-C SAR is quarantined and supplies no evidence unless the reinstatement gates in
\texttt{CIFAR10C\_SAR\_QUARANTINE.md} are met.

(9) \textbf{The escape of \S\ref{app:episode-beta} is marginal, not conditional.} The episode budget
controls a rate \emph{across} deployments drawn from the episode law; it does not protect the
deployment in front of you. Proposition~\ref{epi:prop-escape}(a) is explicit: conditional on the
episode being one of the adversarial ones in its own fibre, the frontier commits and is wrong, and
Lemma~\ref{lem:fibre} says no label-free observable distinguishes that episode from a benign one. A
deployer told ``the budget was estimated'' has bought a long-run frequency, and
Remark~\ref{epi:rem-marginal} states the same distinction that governs the certificate's own
false-adapt guarantee. We state this in three places by design.

(10) \textbf{The budget route is worth taking only because it amortises, and that rationale is
conditional on E2.} Proposition~\ref{epi:prop-probe}(b): at a \emph{single} deployment a labeled
probe of the same size dominates the episode budget, because the probe measures $\gamma$ where it is
needed and the budget measures it elsewhere. The episode route wins only across many deployments, so
when E2 fails it loses its statistical guarantee and its economic rationale at the same time---and
E2 is measured to fail exactly on the axis that defines a new deployment
(\S\ref{epi:sec-numerics}).

(11) \textbf{E2 is not verifiable, only falsifiable, and its observable part is a one-sided screen.}
Corollary~\ref{epi:cor-not-free} shows no label-free statistic certifies the conditional component
of E2. The marginal component is testable: a cross-fitted history-versus-deployment separability
AUC on the evidence alone reads $0.497\pm0.033$ on the exchangeable control, as it must, and
correlates with realised coverage at Pearson $r=-0.35$ ($p=1.2\times10^{-14}$, $n=470$ splits).
Used as a screen at $\Delta_{\mathrm{sep}}<0.70$ it gives mean coverage $0.905$ against a nominal
$0.900$, with $7.7\%$ of splits below $0.85$; above the threshold, mean coverage $0.782$ with $43.3\%$ below $0.85$. A low
reading is informative; a high reading is a warning and nothing more. We report it alongside any
episode budget and do not treat it as a certificate.

\subsection{When to deploy K-Bound}\label{sec:discussion}
\begin{sloppypar}
The theory and experiments converge on a clear deployment rule: K-Bound's value is governed by how
often \emph{catastrophic, detectable} harm occurs---safety insurance in benign regimes, real wins in
collapse-prone synthetic grids (Table~\ref{tab:decisive}).
The trichotomy reads off accordingly. \textbf{(i)~Benign regimes} where adaptation
almost always helps (per-corruption CIFAR-10-C, clean anomaly suites): always-adapt is already
near-oracle, and K-Bound is \emph{safety insurance}---it matches always-adapt at a controlled
false-adapt rate, costing only coverage. \textbf{(ii)~Collapse-prone regimes}---small or
class-imbalanced batches, single-class/label-shift streams, aggressive updates, long
non-stationary runs: adaptation becomes a per-condition coin flip, and K-Bound delivers a real
win, cutting regret-to-oracle $\sim$$2.5$--$5.4\times$ versus always-adapt at $0\%$ false-adapt
on the CIFAR-10-C stress grid (Table~\ref{tab:decisive}). \textbf{(iii)~Self-protecting
candidates} (e.g.\ SAR's reset): the headroom shrinks, and K-Bound \emph{ties} rather than
beats---approximately matching the better policy, at most a small abstention cost. The practical takeaway: deploy K-Bound as a default guard around
any TTA candidate; its overhead is one forward pass, and its benefit scales with exactly the
risk it is designed to detect.
\end{sloppypar}

A label-free adapt/freeze decision is possible only relative to a declared bound on calibration
drift, so the question that decides the method is where that bound comes from. It cannot come from
the deployment, and that is a theorem with an exact value rather than a limitation we regret. For
every declared class of target laws the least label-free-certifiable budget is the fibre radius
$\Gamma_z$, attained by a constant audit that reads none of its data
(Theorem~\ref{thm:beta-minimax}); on the paper's own deployment class that radius is $\beta$ exactly
(Corollary~\ref{cor:beta-is-beta}), so the best possible label-free audit of the budget returns the
budget it was given, and the number it returns is simultaneously the radius of the band on which the
frontier must abstain. Over the unrestricted class the audited rule's decision yield is at most the
false-commitment budget $\delta$, at every batch size and for every evidence map, and a fully
labeled calibration sample from any other domain moves the minimax budget by exactly zero
(Theorem~\ref{thm:anchor}). One lemma---that the label kernel on the disagreement region is
unconstrained by every label-free observable (Lemma~\ref{lem:fibre})---carries the matched-evidence
impossibility, the abstention band and the budget impossibility as three readings of the same
one-parameter family. Our own $\beta$-sweep is the predicted instance: on $6{,}885$ real evaluation
cells, declaring $\beta$ the way this paper's method section prescribes under-covers by the size the
theorem permits. Nor is the impossibility's configuration merely constructed: in logged deployments
there are pairs whose label-free evidence cannot be told apart while their benefit has resolvably
opposite sign, and about $30\%$ of evaluable CIFAR-10-C cells sit in fibres whose sign genuinely
goes both ways --- though such pairs are $39\times$ \emph{rarer} than chance, so the evidence is
informative and merely insufficient (\S\ref{sec:necessity-observed}).

A budget can come from a \emph{population} of deployments, and we price it: between $3$ and $9$
logged deployments at $\alpha=0.10$ under retrospective outcome logging and episode
exchangeability, first feasible on real data at exactly $K=9$. The escape is localised to one
logical move, averaging over the episode law instead of taking a supremum over the fibre, paid for
with historical labels, and neither ingredient works alone
(Proposition~\ref{epi:prop-escape}). The assumption that buys it is the one a shifted deployment
violates: nominal $0.900$ coverage holds under an exchangeable control ($0.905$) and along nuisance
axes and collapses to $0.626$ when the corruption family changes and $0.454$ when severity changes,
and the weighted-conformal relaxation restores coverage to $1.000$ only by driving decision yield to
$0.000$ --- the vacuity of Theorem~\ref{thm:aud-A} reappearing inside the method meant to escape it.

What remains achievable is a finite-sample certificate that trades decision yield for a false-adapt
guarantee, and we measure that frontier rather than assert it. On the CIFAR-10-C stress grid it
commits on $66.8\%$ of $6{,}480$ cells at zero false adaptations, cuts regret $5.00\times$ below the
better fixed policy, places its commitments on cells whose true effect is $24.5\times$ larger than
the ones it declines, and still returns zero false adaptations in all ten leave-one-corruption-out
runs. The binding resource is calibration structure, not sample size: on Camelyon17 real-shift
certifiability is \emph{bias-limited}, and more unlabeled data cannot shrink a radius whose error is
calibration drift. On natural shifts the outcome is scoped, not uniform: \textsc{KGA} is no-harm on
the four one-sided tracks with locked held-out artifacts (Camelyon17, iWildCam, Office-Home,
RxRx1), and on PACS, ImageNet-R and CIFAR-10.1 it is a conservative null or a declared transfer
failure and \emph{loses} to always-adapt --- $1.76\times$ worse on ImageNet-R --- which we report
rather than exclude. Protocols~H v2 and~M v2 beat always-adapt with CIs excluding zero but
\emph{tie} always-freeze, so neither is a beats-both (Tables~\ref{tab:iwildcam-H},
\ref{tab:officehome-M}); their earlier in-sample-radius wins were a calibration bug, corrected here,
and a re-audit withdrew the earlier Camelyon17 Protocol-G beats-both as a domain-pooling artifact
(Table~\ref{tab:camelyon17-G}). The only beats-both results are the synthetic stress grids
(CIFAR-10-C, ImageNet-C SAR). K-Bound is a safety, validity and abstention layer for test-time
adaptation; the drift budget its own theory requires is not obtainable at a deployment, and the
certificate is what remains when that is taken seriously.

\section{Deferred results: the companion theorem stack}\label{app:extensions}\label{app:anytime}\label{app:theory-ext}\label{app:anytime-seq}\label{app:multicand}\label{app:stack}
Two verified extensions of the batch certificate (Theorem~\ref{thm:cert}) are referred to elsewhere
in this paper. We state them here; their proofs, their machine validators
(\texttt{theory\_v2/val\_sequential\_anytime.py}, \texttt{val\_multicandidate.py}) and the remaining
extension stack---multiclass and regression reductions, router and knowability capacity, the
$\gamma$-meter route, the minimax and tight-constant closures, and the Lean formalisation
inventory---are in the companion technical report, whose sources are the \texttt{theory\_v2/}
directory of the released tree. Nothing in the impossibility spine
(\S\ref{sec:theory}--\S\ref{app:episode-beta}) and no empirical claim in this paper depends on any
of them; they are carried as extensions with their own stated assumptions.

\begin{theorem}[Anytime-valid sequential adaptation certificate]\label{thm:anytime}
Let windows $t=1,2,\dots$ on a filtered probability space produce bounded, label-free-computable
benefit summaries $X_t\in[a,b]$ with $a<0<b$, and write $\Delta_t=\E[X_t\mid\mathcal F_{t-1}]$ for
the conditional benefit of window $t$; no independence, identical distribution or stationarity
across windows is assumed. Fix $\alpha\in(0,1)$, a constant $c\in(0,1)$, and predictable bets
$\lambda_t\in[0,c/|a|]$, $\lambda_t^{-}\in[0,c/b]$, and set
$E_t^{+}=\prod_{i\le t}(1+\lambda_iX_i)$ and $E_t^{-}=\prod_{i\le t}(1-\lambda_i^{-}X_i)$.
Return \textsc{adapt} at the first $t$ with $E_t^{+}\ge1/\alpha$, \textsc{freeze} at the first $t$
with $E_t^{-}\ge1/\alpha$, and \textsc{abstain} otherwise, committing absorbingly at the first
crossing. Then \textnormal{(i)} under $H_0:\Delta_t\le0$ for all $t$,
$\Pr(\exists t\ge1:\textsc{decision}_t=\textsc{adapt})\le\alpha$; \textnormal{(ii)} symmetrically,
under $H_0':\Delta_t\ge0$ for all $t$, $\Pr(\exists t\ge1:\textsc{decision}_t=\textsc{freeze})\le\alpha$.
Both bounds are time-uniform, so they hold at an arbitrary, data-dependent stopping time $\tau$.
Run on a single window with the bet matching the conformal radius, the rule specialises to the
level-$\alpha$ decision of Theorem~\ref{thm:cert}. The guarantee is about the sign of
$\Delta_t=\E[X_t\mid\mathcal F_{t-1}]$, the population object the stream targets; identifiability of
the \emph{true} benefit sign from $X_t$ remains governed by the frontier of
Theorem~\ref{thm:headline}, and time-uniformity does not remove the drift budget.
\emph{Proof: companion report (one-sided betting supermartingale and Ville's inequality).}
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[Multicandidate certified routing: family-wise false-adapt control]\label{thm:multicand}
Fix $f_0$ and candidate adapters $f_a^{(1)},\dots,f_a^{(K)}$ with benefits
$\Delta_k=R_T(f_0)-R_T(f_a^{(k)})$, and suppose each admits a label-free lower-confidence bound
$L_k(\delta)=\widehat\Delta_k-\varepsilon_k(\cdot\,;\delta)$ with $\Pr[\Delta_k<L_k(\delta)]\le\delta$
for every $\delta\in(0,1)$---the one-sided half of the split-conformal coverage of
Theorem~\ref{thm:cert}, instantiated per candidate on a shared exchangeable calibration sample. Set
the corrected level $\delta_K=\alpha/K$, form the certified-helpful set $S=\{k:L_k(\delta_K)>0\}$,
commit an arbitrary $\sigma\in S$ when $S\neq\varnothing$ and abstain otherwise. Then the
family-wise false-adapt event $\{\sigma\in S\ \wedge\ \Delta_\sigma\le0\}$ has probability at most
$\alpha$, under arbitrary dependence among the $K$ coverage events and for \emph{every} selector
$\sigma$, including data-dependent and adversarial ones. The \v{S}id\'ak level
$\delta_K=1-(1-\alpha)^{1/K}$ requires mutual independence of the $K$ under-coverage events, which
generally fails under a shared calibration sample and under a common deployment input; Bonferroni is
the assumption-light default. \emph{Proof: companion report.}
\end{theorem}

\paragraph{Real-data anytime demonstration (pre-registered).} The streaming certificate has a
first real-data application (\texttt{research\_lock/WIN\_HUNT\_v3\_PROTOCOL.yaml}, arm~D; bar
frozen before scoring). On a native-order iWildCam test stream ($35{,}370$ images, $45$ windows
of $50$ batches), continual Tent \emph{collapses}: $75.6\%$ of windows harmful, mean window
benefit $-0.370$, always-adapt value $0.329$ versus always-freeze $0.700$. Two one-sided
truncated-aGRAPA betting e-processes on the per-window benefit (the construction of
Theorem~\ref{thm:anytime}) run at $\alpha{=}0.10$: the \textsc{freeze} process crosses
$1/\alpha$ at \textbf{window 6}, the gate locks the safe action, anytime false-adapt is
\textbf{0}, and the realized stream value $0.6995$ ties the oracle policy to four decimals. Per
the frozen bar this is a \emph{demonstration}, not a beats-both (freezing is the oracle on this
stream, so no policy can beat it): the time-uniform guarantee holding while a $37$-point
collapse is caught within six windows on real data. Verdict:
\texttt{research\_lock/WIN\_HUNT\_v3\_ARM\_D\_result.json}.

\paragraph{Fourth natural-shift track: RxRx1 --- the harmful-and-\emph{detectable} corner at $1{,}139$-class scale.}
RxRx1 (WILDS; fluorescent cellular-microscopy images, $1{,}139$ siRNA classes, an OOD
\emph{experimental-batch} shift; the official WILDS ERM ResNet-50, in-distribution accuracy
${\approx}0.36$, frozen accuracy $0.283$ on the OOD-test eval, mean over the three seeds) is the
sharpest harmful-regime real shift in our suite. Over the full $9{+}$ protocol
(composition$\times$batch$\times$aggressiveness over $10$ condition seeds and all $3$ official WILDS
base-model seeds $=360$ conditions, $2{,}160$ records, a commodity GPU ($16$\,GB), complete
$360/360$), adaptation is \emph{harmful-dominated and label-free detectable}: harmful base rate
$81.5\%$ ($B<0$), mean benefit $\bar B={-}0.063$, and---unlike ImageNet-R---the harm \emph{is}
detectable (best evidence AUC $0.78$--$0.84$ across the three seeds, the BN-affine update-norm the top
signal). Always-adapt is actively destructive: even the best candidate (Tent-episodic, $0.276$) trails
the frozen model ($0.283$), and SAR-online \emph{collapses} to $0.024$ ($\approx$ chance over
$1{,}139$ classes) on the single-class/tiny/aggressive cells. Here the certificate's value is purely
defensive, and it delivers: the single-candidate \textsc{KGA} freezes or abstains on \emph{all} $360$
conditions with \emph{zero} false adapts, matching the frozen optimum (regret vs the per-condition
oracle $0.003$--$0.007$) and avoiding the collapse outright---on SAR-online it pays regret $0.000$
where always-adapt pays $0.259$. It does \emph{not} ``beat both'' because freezing already \emph{is}
the oracle policy (no helpful candidate exists to capture)---the honest does-no-harm outcome on the
harmful side, the mirror image of Camelyon17's benign-side tie (Table~\ref{tab:camelyon}). As on
ImageNet-R, the multi-candidate agreement route abstains on all $360$ conditions
($\hat\tau\in[1.06,2.71]$, all $\gg\tau^\star{=}0.52$): the six candidates share one backbone, the
conditional-error-independence violation the CEI diagnostic exists to catch. The harmful-dominated
verdict, the SAR-online collapse, the detectable harm, and the $100\%$ abstention \emph{replicate
across all three independent WILDS base-model seeds} (per-seed harmful base rate $74$--$88\%$, best
AUC $0.78$--$0.84$). RxRx1 thus closes the real-data map of the harmful corner---\emph{detectable}
harm at $1{,}139$-class scale where ``adapt'' is catastrophic and the zero-false-adapt guarantee
carries the load (Table~\ref{tab:rxrx1}). These results are from the three archived
official-seed manifests.

\section{Proof and experiment status}\label{app:status}
Theorem~\ref{thm:imp} is proved with a constructed witness and strengthened to a quantitative Le
Cam minimax floor (part (ii)); Lemma~\ref{thm:gate} is a plug-in regret
decomposition (exact identity) with a minimax floor; Theorem~\ref{thm:cert} is proved
\emph{conditional} on a valid label-free interval estimator and complemented by an anytime-valid
e-process (manuscript, App.~B); Corollary~\ref{thm:pos} proves the covariate case and
Lemma~\ref{lem:reduction} the binary, multiclass, and regression
sign-of-difference results. The multiclass/regression label-free \emph{bracketing} is closed under
a bounded calibration-transfer assumption (manuscript, App.~C); the \emph{unconditional}
bracketing (Conjecture~\ref{conj:gen}) is resolved as a testability dichotomy
(Theorem~\ref{thm:conj1-dichotomy}: no evidence-definable assumption identifies the
sign; the minimal untestable supplement is one bit, which suffices under falsifiable
structure). Every theorem ships a
numerical sanity-check in the release package; all were re-run
from a clean environment and pass: the Le Cam floor tracks the closed form, the regret identity
holds to ${\sim}10^{-17}$, the multiclass identity to $10^{-16}$ with $100\%$ sign agreement over
$4000$ trials, calibration-transfer sign-recovery is $100\%$ when the confidence gap exceeds
$2\eta$, the router generalization gap decays as ${\sim}n^{-0.66}$, and the drift-corrected anytime
false-adapt rate stays $\le\alpha$. Where multi-seed runs are available, experiments report means
$\pm$ std with paired $t$-tests; single-seed/scoping tracks are labeled explicitly in captions and
discussed as regime-classification evidence. We also report evidence/$\alpha$/batch ablations, a
regression covariate-shift track, and a clean
non-identifiability witness.
