## Natural-runner integrity closure

The maintained natural runners now fail closed on the previously exploitable resume and provenance
paths.

- Generic partial-state sealing/loading requires a runner-specific semantic validator. Internal
  inventory hashes alone are explicitly rejected as an authenticity check.
- Camelyon17, ImageNet-R, GeoShift, and RxRx1 recompute scientific cell identities, deterministic
  sample receipts and labels, evidence dimensions, update-norm equality, exact TTA protocol,
  score/benefit arithmetic, candidate inventories, and condition summaries.
- Office-Home and iWildCam reconstruct their exact stream and evaluation positions, live sample
  identifiers, evaluation labels, and population facts from the current dataset/index. Office-Home
  also reconstructs source-reference identities.
- Selected stream/evaluation samples are unique and disjoint; unreadable requested samples fail the
  cell instead of being silently substituted.
- Checkpoint file hashes and tensor-state hashes are separate, model-seed identities are explicit,
  and byte-different checkpoint containers with identical tensor states cannot masquerade as
  independent models.
- Multiclass Route B and the retired scored Route C fail closed as unsupported. ImageNet-R and
  RxRx1 use explicit balanced-accuracy contracts.
- Online and episodic candidate TTA are archived as transductive protocols, including the exact
  update/evaluation order and evaluation-batch BatchNorm use.
- RxRx1 completion receipts are bound to the exact run name, run directory, result path, result
  bytes, ordered population, checkpoint tensors, implementation hashes, and scientific
  configuration. A copied receipt or a stale same-path receipt cannot stop a changed run.

These controls protect against accidental contamination and self-consistent JSON resealing under
the declared runtime. Protection against a privileged actor who can rewrite code, data, artifacts,
and their seal together requires an externally stored or signed release digest.
