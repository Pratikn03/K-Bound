\subsection{Two new rows, one reach: where v0.5 leaves
Conjecture~\ref{conj:gen}}\label{sec:reach-unification}

The ambiguity reach $\rho(P)=\tfrac12(\sup I(P)-\inf I(P))$ of
Definition~\ref{def:reach} is the single quantity that decides knowability
(Proposition~\ref{thm:unify}): commit $\sign c(P)$ iff $|c(P)|>\rho(P)$. The two
v0.5 results are exactly two more evaluations of this $\rho$, and they share the
common feature that the reach is now \emph{computed from an observable},
not merely assumed.

\begin{proposition}[Reach-table extension]\label{prop:reach-table-v05}
Adjoin to the table of Proposition~\ref{prop:reach-table} the rows
\begin{center}
%% fix-queue item 32: \resizebox dropped; set at \small instead of rescaled.
\small
{%
\begin{tabular}{llll}
\hline
family & center $c$ & reach $\rho$ & source \\
\hline
multi-view, \emph{approx.}\ indep. & $\widehat\Delta(\widetilde b)$ &
  $O(\tau)$ on the normal cone $\;+\;$ tangential blind spot &
  Thm.~\ref{thm:multicand-residual} \\
smooth drift, radius $L d$ & $U-2T_S$ & $2\,L\,d\,W$ (observable) &
  Prop.~\ref{thm:smooth-drift} \\
\hline
\end{tabular}}
\end{center}
where $\tau$ is the observable rank-one residual \textup{(1A)} and $d$ the
observable covariate discrepancy \textup{(1B)}. In both, the previously
\emph{assumed} structural quantity (exact independence; a known drift radius $B$)
is replaced by a \emph{measured} one ($\tau$; $d$), at the cost of a single
residual modulus---a tangential-control budget \textup{(1A)} or a Lipschitz
constant $L$ \textup{(1B)}. Direction (i) of Proposition~\ref{thm:unify} then
certifies $\sign\Delta$ in each family whenever $|c|>\rho$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Each row is an evaluation of $\rho$ in its family. \emph{Smooth drift:} the
admissible benefit interval is $\{U-2T_S-2\E_T[(f_0{-}f_a)b]:\|b\|_{\infty,D}\le
Ld\}$, whose half-width is $LdW$ by the tight H\"older bound of
Proposition~\ref{thm:smooth-drift}; so $\rho=2LdW$ and $c=U-2T_S$. \emph{Multi-view:}
the benefit for candidate $i$ is $\Delta^{(i)}=P_T(D_i)(2a_i-1)$
(Theorem~\ref{thm:disagree}), and the equivalence class of advantage vectors
producing the observed agreements to tolerance $\tau$ has, by
Theorem~\ref{thm:multicand-residual}(b,c), half-width $O(\tau)$ in the directions
\emph{normal} to the rank-one variety; the $M$-dimensional \emph{tangential} cone
is unconstrained by $\tau$ (Theorem~\ref{thm:multicand-residual}(d)), where the
reach equals the full two-point gap of Theorem~\ref{thm:imp}. Plugging each
$(c,\rho)$ into Proposition~\ref{thm:unify}(i) gives the certificate. \qedhere
\end{proof}

\noindent\emph{Numerically validated} (\texttt{val\_reach\_unification.py};
seed $20260609$, CPU only). \textbf{Drift row:} brute-forcing the benefit
interval over admissible drifts reproduces the closed-form reach $2LdW$ with ratio
$\mathbf{1.000}$ (range $[1.000,1.000]$ over $60$ random worlds); the unified
$|c|>\rho$ certificate makes \textbf{zero} false certifications, and below the
boundary both signs are realized ($100\%$), i.e.\ the converse is real.
\textbf{Multi-view row:} under exact independence the reach is $\approx0$
($0.020$ sampling floor), reproducing the Steinhardt--Liang row; under generic
approximate independence the recovery half-spread tracks the observable $\tau$
monotonically (Spearman $\mathbf{1.00}$); and a \emph{realizable} tangential
error-correlation yields $\tau=2\times10^{-9}\!\approx\!0$ with true
misspecification $\eta=0.53$ and a \emph{wrong} recovered sign---confirming that
$\tau$ bounds only the normal reach and the tangent is the irreducible
Theorem~\ref{thm:imp} blind spot.

\paragraph{Conjecture~\ref{conj:gen}, sharpened.} The open problem was the
unconditional label-free bracketing of $\sign\Delta$ for $K\ge3$ and for
regression. v0.5 closes two structured slices of it with \emph{checkable}
prices: (1A) when $M\ge4$ candidates are available and the rank-one residual
$\tau$ is small \emph{and} the tangential misspecification is controlled; and
(1B) when the concept drift is Lipschitz-coupled to an observable covariate
discrepancy. In reach-table terms, both move a row from ``reach assumed'' to
``reach measured up to one modulus''. What remains open is now stated precisely:

\begin{quote}\itshape
Compute, or two-sidedly bound, the ambiguity reach $\rho(P)$ for a
\emph{nonparametric} family (unknown conditionals, unbounded drift) using
\emph{only} observable side-information and \emph{no} residual structural
modulus---equivalently, exhibit a label-free statistic that upper-bounds
$\rho(P)$ including its tangential component, with a tight matching converse.
\end{quote}

\noindent The 1A blind spot shows why this is hard and not a mere gap in our
analysis: an agreement-only statistic is provably blind to a tangential family
that moves $\Delta$ across zero (Theorem~\ref{thm:imp}), so \emph{some} extra
observable (a third-order tensor, an instrument, a probe) or \emph{some} residual
modulus is necessary. The contribution of v0.5 is to convert ``unknowable in
general'' into ``knowable up to one named, checkable modulus per family'', and to
name exactly the modulus each family still pays.

\paragraph{Honest scope.} Direction (i) of Proposition~\ref{thm:unify} is
near-definitional once the reaches are computed; the value here is organizational
(two literatures---unsupervised accuracy estimation and bounded-shift transfer---
become two rows of one table) plus the precise restatement of the open problem.
The multi-view reach is observable \emph{only} on the normal cone; we do not claim
to bound the tangential component, and the sharpened conjecture says so. All
numbers are synthetic consistency checks of the reach identities, not evidence
about real workloads.
