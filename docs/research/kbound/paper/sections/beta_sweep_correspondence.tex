\begin{remark}[What the theorems predict about this experiment, and what they do
not]\label{rem:empirical}
The $\beta$-sweep is \emph{not} a label-free audit: its budget
$\widehat\beta=q_{0.90}(|\gamma|)$ is computed on labeled source-like development cells.
Corollary~\ref{cor:audA} therefore does not apply to it, and we do not claim it as a confirmation of
Corollary~\ref{cor:audA}. What applies is Theorem~\ref{thm:anchor} with
Proposition~\ref{prop:threeterm}: the procedure supplies the first term and \emph{sets the other two
to zero by an undeclared exchangeability assumption between development and deployment cells}. The
theory then predicts under-coverage of unbounded size, and that is what is measured.
\begin{itemize}
\item \emph{Coverage.} $\widehat\beta$ is a $0.90$ quantile on development cells, so
$\Pr(|\gamma|\le\widehat\beta)$ has a by-construction null of $0.900$ on the deployment cells
whenever the two pools are exchangeable ($0.900$ for CIFAR-10-C and $0.8963$ for ImageNet-C,
recorded as \texttt{coverage\_on\_dev\_cells\_BY\_CONSTRUCTION}). CIFAR-10-C measures
$0.274$--$0.756$, far below that null: the exchangeability declaration is falsified, so
$\rho+\Delta_{\mathcal F}>0$ was set to $0$, and the committed error rate is correspondingly
$0.4\%$--$16.6\%$ where Theorem~\ref{thm:headline} promises $0\%$. ImageNet-C measures
$0.904$--$0.985$ in five of the six configurations recorded in
\texttt{adversarial\_ablations\_results.json}, at or above the null, which \emph{carries no
information}; and its three \texttt{loco} rows additionally have
\texttt{dev\_and\_target\_disjoint}$=$\texttt{false}. The single ImageNet-C configuration whose
coverage is a genuine measurement is \texttt{srclike}$|$\texttt{M\_gbm}, at $0.470$ in that re-run
and $0.511$ in Table~\ref{tab:beta-sweep} (which uses the primary $\widehat\beta=0.0302$); it is
also the configuration with commit-error $0.532$ on $62$ committed cells (sign match $0.468$, $95\%$
CI $[0.340,0.599]$).
\item \emph{Sup versus quantile.} Theorem~\ref{thm:beta-minimax} bounds any valid budget by a
\emph{supremum} over the fibre, not a development quantile. Measured on CIFAR-10-C: the maximal
realised $|\gamma|$ on deployment cells exceeds the maximal $|\gamma|$ on development cells by
factors of $2.4$--$28.4$ across the ten configurations (\texttt{gamma\_absmax\_target} versus
\texttt{beta\_hat\_max} in \texttt{beta\_sweep\_results.json}).
\item \emph{Anchor destruction, quantitatively.} Theorem~\ref{thm:anchor} says an uninformative
anchor leaves only a class-scale constant. Under the global label permutation of
\texttt{adversarial\_ablations.py}, any estimator fit on the permuted benefit column converges to
the constant $\E[\Delta]$, so the declaration procedure must return approximately
$q_{0.90}(|\Delta-\E\Delta|)$ --- a number computable from the raw artifacts with no model fitting.
On CIFAR-10-C that prediction is $0.2376$; the six measured shuffled budgets are
$0.2468$--$0.2606$, ratios $1.04$--$1.10$, against real budgets of $0.0178$--$0.0482$ (inflation
$5.2\times$--$14.1\times$), with zero commitments in $5/5$ replicates for the \texttt{M\_doc} and
\texttt{M\_atc4} channels. On ImageNet-C the prediction is $0.0689$ against measured
$0.0742$--$0.1171$ (ratios $1.08$--$1.70$; $n=405$). Script and output:
\path{frontier_sweep_v1/beta_impossible/check_anchor_collapse.py} and
\path{frontier_sweep_v1/beta_impossible/anchor_collapse_check.json}.
\item \emph{A prediction that fails, stated as such.} On ImageNet-C the anchored budget for the
\texttt{M\_doc} and \texttt{M\_atc4} channels is \emph{larger} than the anchor-free constant
($0.199$--$0.239$ real versus $0.074$--$0.090$ shuffled), which is the reverse of the pattern the
mechanism above describes. Nothing in \S\ref{sec:impossible} forbids it:
Theorem~\ref{thm:beta-minimax} lower-bounds \emph{valid} audits and says nothing about how far an
invalid heuristic may overshoot. The mechanism is visible in the same artifact ---
difference-of-confidences is anti-predictive on ImageNet-C (AUC $0.351$), so its residuals exceed
the marginal spread of the benefit. The operational reading is that on that benchmark the anchored
declaration is beaten by the trivial constant, and those are exactly the rows with decision yield
$0.000$. We report this as a failed prediction rather than omit it.
\end{itemize}
\end{remark}
