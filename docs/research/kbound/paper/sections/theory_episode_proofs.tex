\subsection{Proofs}

\begin{proof}[Proof of Theorem~\ref{epi:thm-ident}]
(a) $\beta^\star(\alpha)$ is by construction the $(1-\alpha)$-quantile of $|\gamma|$ under $Q$, hence
a functional of the $(W,\gamma)$-law; and $\{|\gamma|\le b\}=\{P\in\mathcal C_b\}$ by the definition
of $\mathcal C_b$, so the quantile characterisation is immediate. For the decision consequence, fix
the history and condition on $\{|\gamma_{K+1}|\le b\}$. On that event
Theorem~\ref{thm:headline}(ii) applies, so a strict commitment made at margin $|M_{K+1}|>b$ has the
correct sign; a false strict commitment therefore requires $\{|\gamma_{K+1}|>b\}$, an event of
probability at most $\alpha$. (This is Theorem~\ref{thm:short-audDE} with the audit event supplied by
$Q$ instead of by a concentration bound.)

(b) Fix the $W$-marginal $Q_W$. By Lemma~\ref{lem:fibre}, for any episode with observables
$(\mu,f_0,f_a,s)$ --- hence any value of $W$ --- and any $\bar a\in[0,1]$ there is a label kernel on
$D$ realising $\bar a$ while leaving $(Z,M)$ unchanged. The achievable drift set at matched evidence
is $I_M=[-(M+\tfrac12),\,\tfrac12-M]$, an interval of length $1$ containing $0$ whose endpoints have
absolute values $\tfrac12+M$ and $\tfrac12-M$, the larger being $\tfrac12+|M|\ge\tfrac12$. Hence for
every $b\in[0,\tfrac12]$ at least one of $+b,-b$ lies in $I_M$; concretely, set
$\theta(W)=+1$ if $b\le\tfrac12-M$ and $\theta(W)=-1$ otherwise, which is admissible because
$b>\tfrac12-M$ forces $M+\tfrac12>1-b\ge b$ for $b\le\tfrac12$. Define $Q_b$ by drawing
$W\sim Q_W$ and realising $\gamma=\theta(W)\,b$ by the corresponding kernel. Then $Q_b$ has
$W$-marginal $Q_W$, and $|\gamma|=b$ $Q_b$-almost surely, so
$Q_b(|\gamma|\le b')=0<1-\alpha$ for every $b'<b$ while $Q_b(|\gamma|\le b)=1$; hence
$\beta^\star_{Q_b}(\alpha)=b$ for every $\alpha\in(0,1)$. Since $b$ was arbitrary in
$[0,\tfrac12]$, the $W$-marginal carries no information about $\beta^\star$.
\end{proof}

\begin{proof}[Proof of Theorem~\ref{epi:thm-conformal}]
Define the counterfactual score $S_{K+1}=|\widehat\gamma_{K+1}|$ that a size-$n$ label sample from
the current episode would produce. Under E1--E2 the vector $(S_1,\dots,S_{K+1})$ is exchangeable:
$V_{1:K+1}$ is exchangeable, the label samples are conditionally i.i.d.\ of common size $n$ given the
episodes, and $S_e$ is the same measurable functional of $(V_e,\text{labels}_e)$ for every $e$. Split
conformal on an exchangeable sequence gives
$\Pr\bigl(S_{K+1}\le S_{(j)}\bigr)\ge j/(K+1)\ge1-\alpha+\delta$. Independently, Hoeffding on the
$n$ i.i.d.\ variables $u_i\in[-1,1]$ gives
$\Pr\bigl(|\widehat\gamma_{K+1}-\gamma_{K+1}|>t(n,\delta)\bigr)\le\delta$. On the intersection,
$|\gamma_{K+1}|\le S_{K+1}+t\le S_{(j)}+t$. A union bound gives failure probability at most
$(\alpha-\delta)+\delta=\alpha$. Feasibility is $j\le K$, i.e.\ $\alpha\ge\tfrac1{K+1}+\delta$.
\end{proof}

\begin{proof}[Proof of Theorem~\ref{epi:thm-floor}]
For $p\in(0,1)$ let $Q_p$ be the i.i.d.\ episode law with $|\gamma_e|=b$ with probability $p$ and
$\gamma_e=0$ otherwise (realisable at fixed observables by Lemma~\ref{lem:fibre}, as in
Theorem~\ref{epi:thm-ident}(b)). Suppose $h(0,\dots,0)<b$. The event
$A=\{\gamma_1=\dots=\gamma_K=0\}\cap\{|\gamma_{K+1}|=b\}$ has $Q_p$-probability $p(1-p)^K$ and on $A$
the budget misses. Hence $\alpha\ge\sup_{p}p(1-p)^K$. Setting $p=1/(K+1)$ gives
$\alpha\ge\frac1{K+1}(1-\frac1{K+1})^K$. Finally
$K\ln(1-\tfrac1{K+1})=-K\ln(1+\tfrac1K)\ge-1$ since $\ln(1+x)\le x$, so
$(1-\tfrac1{K+1})^K\ge e^{-1}$.
\end{proof}

\begin{proof}[Proof of Proposition~\ref{epi:prop-labels}]
The Bernstein statement is standard for $u\in[-1,1]$ (range $2$); the sufficient $n$ follows by
bounding each term by $\kappa\beta/2$. For the identity, calibration gives $\E[u]=0$ and
$\E[u^2]=\E\bigl[\E[(C-s)^2\mid X]\bigr]=\E[\Var(C\mid X)]=\E[s(1-s)]$, so $\sigma^2=\E[s(1-s)]$.
Also $\Var(C)=\E[C]-\E[C]^2=\E[s]-\E[s]^2=\E[s(1-s)]+\bigl(\E[s^2]-\E[s]^2\bigr)
=\E[s(1-s)]+\Var(s)$.
\end{proof}

\begin{proof}[Proof of Proposition~\ref{epi:prop-probe}]
(a) Consider the two worlds $\bar a=\tfrac12\pm\varepsilon$ realised at matched observables by
Lemma~\ref{lem:fibre}; by Lemma~\ref{lem:reduction} they have
$\Delta>0$ and $\Delta<0$ respectively. Each probe is Bernoulli($\bar a$) for the event
$\{f_a(X)=Y\}$. In the $+$ world, $\Pr_+[\textsc{freeze}]\le\alpha$ and
$\Pr_+[\textsc{abstain}]\le\eta$, so $\Pr_+[\textsc{adapt}]\ge1-\alpha-\eta$; in the $-$ world
$\Pr_-[\textsc{adapt}]\le\alpha$. Hence
$\TV\bigl(\mathrm{Ber}(\tfrac12+\varepsilon)^{\otimes k},
\mathrm{Ber}(\tfrac12-\varepsilon)^{\otimes k}\bigr)\ge1-2\alpha-\eta$. Pinsker and tensorisation
give $\TV\le\sqrt{k\,\KL/2}$ with
$\KL=2\varepsilon\ln\frac{1/2+\varepsilon}{1/2-\varepsilon}
=2\varepsilon\cdot2\operatorname{artanh}(2\varepsilon)\le
2\varepsilon\cdot\frac{4\varepsilon}{1-4\varepsilon^2}\le\frac{32\varepsilon^2}{3}$
for $\varepsilon\le\tfrac14$. Thus $1-2\alpha-\eta\le4\varepsilon\sqrt{k/3}$, which rearranges to the
claim. (b) is arithmetic on the two label counts, using
$n\asymp\sigma^2\log(1/\delta)/\beta^2$ and $k\asymp\log(1/\alpha)/\varepsilon^2$.
\end{proof}

\begin{proof}[Proof of Theorem~\ref{epi:thm-shift}]
(a) Write $\mathcal H$ for the history. By Theorem~\ref{epi:thm-conformal},
$\E_{\mathcal H}\bigl[Q(|\gamma|>\widehat\beta(\mathcal H))\bigr]\le\alpha$. Since
$Q'(A)\le\rho\,Q(A)$ for every measurable $A$,
$\Pr_{Q'}(\text{miss})=\E_{\mathcal H}\bigl[Q'(|\gamma|>\widehat\beta)\bigr]
\le\rho\,\E_{\mathcal H}\bigl[Q(|\gamma|>\widehat\beta)\bigr]\le\rho\alpha$. The feasibility
condition at level $\alpha/\rho$ is $\alpha/\rho\ge1/(K+1)$.
(b) Under the stated factorisation the scores $S_1,\dots,S_{K+1}$ are weighted exchangeable in the
sense of \cite{tibshirani2019conformal} with weight function $w$; their Theorem~2 gives exact
$(1-\alpha)$ coverage for the weighted quantile
$\inf\{s:\sum_k p_k\mathbf 1\{S_k\le s\}\ge1-\alpha\}$,
$p_k=w(W_k)/\bigl(\sum_i w(W_i)+w(W_{K+1})\bigr)$. Estimability of $w$ from unlabeled data is the
standard two-sample density-ratio reduction: $w\propto\frac{\pi(W)}{1-\pi(W)}$ for
$\pi(W)=\Pr(\text{episode is current}\mid W)$, and $W$ contains no labels.
\end{proof}

\begin{proof}[Proof of Theorem~\ref{thm:lecam}]
Take $s\equiv\tfrac12$ on $D$, so $M=0$, and for $t\in[0,\tfrac14]$ let $P_t$ be the law of
Lemma~\ref{lem:fibre} with $\eta\equiv\tfrac12+t$; then $u_t\equiv t$ is the constant $t$,
$P_t\in\mathcal C_{\mathcal U}$ whenever $t\le\tau(\mathcal U)$, and $\gamma(P_t)=t$, while
$P_0$ is the null law with $\gamma=0$. The audit's data are $n$ i.i.d.\
$\mathrm{Bernoulli}(\tfrac12+t)$ variables together with label-free quantities whose law does
not depend on $t$ (Lemma~\ref{lem:fibre}); write $Q_t$ for the induced law of the audit's
input and $A:=\{\widehat\beta\ge t\}$.

Suppose, for contradiction, that $\kappa<t$ for some $t\le\min\{\tau(\mathcal U),\tfrac14\}$
with $\TV(Q_0,Q_t)<\tfrac12$. Validity at $P_t$ gives $Q_t(A)\ge1-\delta\ge\tfrac56$;
usefulness at $P_0$ gives $Q_0(A)\le Q_0(\widehat\beta>\kappa)\le\tfrac13$. Hence
$\TV(Q_0,Q_t)\ge Q_t(A)-Q_0(A)\ge\tfrac56-\tfrac13=\tfrac12$, a contradiction. Therefore
$\kappa\ge t$ for every such $t$.

It remains to bound $\TV$. The $t$-dependent part of $Q_t$ is a product of $n$ Bernoulli laws,
so by the tensorisation of $\KL$, then $\KL\le\chi^2$, then Pinsker,
\[
\TV(Q_0,Q_t)\le\sqrt{\tfrac12\KL(Q_t\Vert Q_0)}
=\sqrt{\tfrac{n}{2}\,\KL\bigl(\mathrm{Ber}(\tfrac12{+}t)\,\Vert\,\mathrm{Ber}(\tfrac12)\bigr)}
\le\sqrt{\tfrac{n}{2}\cdot\tfrac{t^2}{\tfrac12(1-\tfrac12)}}=t\sqrt{2n},
\]
using $\chi^2(\mathrm{Ber}(p)\Vert\mathrm{Ber}(q))=(p-q)^2/(q(1-q))$ with $q=\tfrac12$. Thus
$\TV<\tfrac12$ whenever $t<(2\sqrt{2n})^{-1}$. Taking the supremum of admissible $t$ over
$t<\min\{\tau(\mathcal U),\tfrac14,(2\sqrt{2n})^{-1}\}$ gives the claim.
(The conditions of Pinsker's and the $\KL\le\chi^2$ inequalities hold: both laws are
mutually absolutely continuous for $t<\tfrac12$.)
\end{proof}

deployments, not the sample size within one.}

\subsection{Two implementation bugs in our own first version}

Both were found by us, both are fixed in the released code, and both are recorded here because
anyone reusing these artifacts will meet the second one.

\begin{enumerate}
\item \emph{A nested cross-fit manufactured coverage of $1.000$.} The first implementation fitted the
benefit model with an inner cross-fit that made history residuals systematically larger than
deployment residuals, so the conformal quantile was inflated and the exchangeable control returned
perfect coverage. The three-pool design of \S\ref{sec:episode-empirics} --- fit pool, history,
deployment, disjoint, with $M$ fit once and applied identically --- fixes it, and the control then
reads $0.905$, as it must.
\item \emph{An unshuffled \texttt{cross\_val\_predict} produced a separability AUC of $0.24$.} The
episode-weight fit called \texttt{cross\_val\_predict(..., cv=5)} without shuffling.
\texttt{StratifiedKFold} then took contiguous blocks, and the deployment rows arrive in the
artifact's native (method, seed, condition) order, so each fold was a coherent block of conditions;
the separability AUC came out at $0.24$ on splits whose true separability is $0.5$. With
\texttt{shuffle=True} the control reads $0.497\pm0.033$. \textbf{Anyone reusing
\texttt{cross\_val\_predict} on these artifacts should shuffle.} The superseded run is retained in
the tree as \texttt{*\_UNSHUFFLED\_SUPERSEDED.*} rather than deleted.
\end{enumerate}

A third, smaller correction: seeding used \texttt{hash(name)}, which Python salts per process. It was
replaced by \texttt{zlib.crc32}, and the script is now bit-reproducible.
