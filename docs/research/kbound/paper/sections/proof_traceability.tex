\label{sec:proof-traceability}

7 September 2026 integration receipt records a successful Lean 4.29.1
strict-core build and transitive-axiom audit for 303 registered declarations:
65 retained core declarations and 238 additional results, including supporting
lemmas. The theorem map contains 309 distinct \texttt{\#check} entries; six are
outside that registry. Eleven public-root example files contain 67 examples.
These are different inventories, not a percentage of the paper proved.

A fresh byte-level check on 8 September matched all 77 formal-source,
configuration and example files bound by the integration receipt, and verified
the linked strict-audit and independent-replay receipts. The recorded build was
incremental with cached dependencies, not a cold dependency rebuild; no new
kernel build is asserted by this source check. Path-redacted companions

\path{formal/actual_fibre_radius_followon_20260907.portable.json} preserve each
original receipt's SHA-256 and non-path fields; they are not fresh proof receipts.

\paragraph{Population reduction and frontier.}
\path{JointKernelScore.joint_score_benefit} derives the score-residual identity
from the conditional kernel of an actual binary joint law.
\path{ActualWorldFrontier.actual_identified_interval} identifies the clipped
benefit interval over the full fixed-input-marginal residual class.
\path{ActualWorldEvidence.actual_augmented_fibre_strict_direction_iff}
connects this class to a fixed marginal-based observation experiment.
The restricted-class results in \path{ActualWorldSubclass} require the legal
interior opposite-pair and boundary zero constructions; individual directional
uniqueness additionally requires a nonempty class. These results do not establish
those membership conditions for an arbitrary deployment subclass or justify
conditioning on a model pair adapted on the same observed batch.

\paragraph{Audit floor.}
\path{measurable_audit_floor} proves the bound at the possibly unattained
supremum of a nonempty bounded residual fibre under a common observation law.
\path{ActualFibreRadius.actual_augmented_fibre_radius} supplies the actual
full-class radius equality under positive disagreement mass,
$0\le\beta\le\tfrac12$, and the fixed observation experiment. This is not an
estimator of a valid deployment $\beta$ from unlabeled data.

\paragraph{Coverage and decision.}
\path{ExtendedRadiusCertificate.directional_error_probability} proves that
coverage for the named scalar target bounds the union of false strict
directions, allowing measurable finite or infinite radii.
\path{ExactConformal.literal_residual_certificate} constructs the literal
ceiling-rank absolute-residual interval, including ties and the infinite-radius
case, under joint score exchangeability. Neither result establishes benchmark
exchangeability, calibration transfer, or operational label custody. For a
population-target application, valid coverage of population benefit is the
required statistical premise; exact fibre-wide identification is a separate
question. The \path{PopulationTransfer} module combines cell and sampling
coverage by a union bound, but does not supply the reported experiments with
a population sampling-error radius.

\paragraph{Supporting statements.}
\path{WeightedHelpful} assumes per-cell helpfulness and common nonnegative
weights, with positive total weight for means. \path{FeatureRank} proves a
nine-dimensional real-linear schema and row rank at most nine under the exact
coordinate relations; it does not establish observed numerical rank or
probabilistic independence. \path{RiskAlignment} proves that alignment is
weaker than strict identification, including an actual zero/positive-law witness.

The current prose states the fixed observation, model-pair, seed-coupling and
nonempty-subclass qualifications. Their empirical applicability remains
unproved. Manuscript revisions require their own final source binding and
semantic review; unchanged Lean sources do not make every revised sentence
kernel-checked. The historical full one-bit/H/ratio-rate extension remains
excluded. Formal verification, empirical provenance, implementation tests and
publication rendering therefore retain separate scopes.
