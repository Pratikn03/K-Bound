\section{How to Read the Empirical Record}
\label{fr:sec-evidence-hierarchy}

The empirical record answers a narrower question than the population theory.  The theory asks
which benefit signs are uniform over a declared class of target laws.  The experiments instead
record what a fixed gate did on named evaluation cells after a particular candidate, evidence map,
benefit predictor, and calibration rule had been frozen.  Keeping those two questions separate is
not a stylistic precaution.  It determines which denominator is meaningful, which sources may
support a sentence, and what an observed error can falsify.

This atlas therefore uses an evidence-role taxonomy.  The roles are not cumulative stages, and a
record does not ``move upward'' merely by accumulating artifacts.  A strong checksum cannot
compensate for a weak study design, and a prospectively governed design cannot compensate for an
invalid metric.  Conversely, an unfavorable or stopped study remains informative when its status
and stopping rule are stated accurately.  The objective is an auditable account of directional
action, error exposure, and utility consequences, not a tournament table of adaptation methods.

\subsection{The object that receives an action}
\label{fr:sec-action-object}

An evaluation cell is the smallest unit to which the recorded gate assigns one action.  A cell can
be a corruption condition, a checkpoint--location pair, or another protocol-defined collection of
examples.  For a higher-is-better score $S$, the measured outcome for cell $i$ is
\begin{equation}
 B_i=\Delta_i^{\mathrm{cell}}
   =S(f_a;\mathcal E_i)-S(f_0;\mathcal E_i).
\label{fr:eq-observed-cell-benefit}
\end{equation}
This is an observed score difference after labels are joined offline.  It is not automatically an
estimate of the population benefit $\Delta$, and it need not be an average of per-example losses.
For example, a difference in cell-level macro-F1 is a nonlinear functional of a confusion table.
No sample-size argument converts such a difference into the population quantity merely because
the action itself did not read the labels.

The prediction $\widehat\Delta_i$ and its empirical radius $\varepsilon_i$ are separate objects.
The gate commits to ADAPT when $\widehat\Delta_i-\varepsilon_i>0$, commits to FREEZE when
$\widehat\Delta_i+\varepsilon_i<0$, and otherwise records ABSTAIN while serving the frozen model.
FREEZE and ABSTAIN therefore have the same served predictor but different meanings.  FREEZE is a
directional rejection of the candidate.  ABSTAIN records unresolved or unavailable evidence.  A
system that silently converts every missing assessment into FREEZE would manufacture apparent
negative-direction confidence that the evidence never supplied.

\subsection{Four denominators that must not be conflated}
\label{fr:sec-denominator-discipline}

Let $n$ be the number of equally weighted recorded cells, $A$ the number of ADAPT actions, $F$ the
number of FREEZE actions, and $U=n-A-F$ the number of abstentions.  Let $A^{-}$ count ADAPT actions
on cells with $B_i\le 0$, and let $F^{+}$ count FREEZE actions on cells with $B_i\ge 0$.  The basic
descriptive quantities are
\begin{align}
  \mathrm{exposure}_{A}&=A/n,&
  \mathrm{exposure}_{F}&=F/n,&
  \mathrm{commitment}&=(A+F)/n,\label{fr:eq-action-exposure}\\
  \widehat{\mathrm{FA}}_{\mathrm u}&=A^{-}/n,&
  \widehat{\mathrm{FA}}_{\mathrm c}&=A^{-}/A,&
  \widehat{\mathrm{FF}}_{\mathrm u}&=F^{+}/n,&
  \widehat{\mathrm{FF}}_{\mathrm c}&=F^{+}/F.
\label{fr:eq-directional-errors}
\end{align}
The conditional ratios are undefined when the corresponding action count is zero.  The all-cell
and action-conditional denominators answer different operational questions.  The first asks how
often a wrong directional commitment appears in the stream of all decisions.  The second asks how
often a particular commitment is wrong among the times that branch is exercised.  Neither is the
population event involving the unobserved population benefit, and neither is an interval-coverage
rate.

A zero numerator is uninterpretable without exposure.  Zero false adaptations among many ADAPT
actions describes a different operating record from zero false adaptations produced by a gate that
never adapts.  Likewise, an all-frozen policy may preserve utility when the candidate is harmful,
but it has supplied no evidence about its ability to recognize a helpful candidate.  For that
reason every result discussion below begins with $A/F/U$ and the relevant error numerator before
turning to accuracy or regret.

Regret is a secondary utility measure.  On each cell the observed oracle serves the model with the
higher recorded score.  The policy regret is its shortfall from that oracle, averaged using the
protocol's declared weights.  Always-adapt and always-freeze are useful reference policies because
they expose the cost of the two unconditional choices.  A lower point regret for the gate is a
candidate- and panel-specific utility fact.  It is not a definition of safety and cannot establish
a uniform advantage over future candidates or target laws.

\subsection{Why a generated table is not a new experiment}
\label{fr:sec-generated-surfaces}

The presentation tables under \path{paper/generated/} are consumers of the authorities above.
They round values and arrange columns for LaTeX.  They do not create observations, change action
rules, or promote a diagnostic.  In this report, a literal number not expanded through a macro is
transcribed only from one of those current generated surfaces or from exact maintained text whose
artifact is named.  The presentation layer must be regenerated when an authority changes; it must
not be edited to force agreement with prose.

This distinction is especially important for later studies.  CCT-20 and the So2Sat development
screen occurred after the canonical 106-source panel was assembled.  Their separate, receipt-linked
authorities are not merged into that JSON.  Treating absence from the canonical panel as absence of
the study would be wrong.  Silently copying their rows into the canonical panel would also be wrong,
because it would erase the independent protocol and release boundary.  The claim ledger links each
study to the authority that actually governs it.

\subsection{A reproducible claim is a tuple, not a sentence}
\label{fr:sec-claim-tuple}

For audit purposes, an empirical claim should be read as the tuple
\begin{equation}
  \mathcal Q=(\text{candidate},\text{checkpoint},\text{cell unit},\text{metric},
  \text{gate},\text{calibration},\text{weights},\text{status},\text{authority}).
\label{fr:eq-claim-tuple}
\end{equation}
Changing any component creates a different empirical question.  A new adapter learning rate
changes the candidate.  Replacing accuracy by macro-F1 changes the outcome.  Pooling cells instead
of weighting families equally changes the estimand.  Reusing the same radius after changing the
checkpoint changes the calibration identity.  Calling an opened grid prospective changes the
status without changing the data, which is precisely why it is not allowed.

The tuple also clarifies what a checksum can establish.  A digest can show that the bytes named by
the authority have not changed.  It cannot show that the metric was appropriate, that candidate
construction was independent of the test inputs, that residual scores are exchangeable, or that a
corruption family represents a future deployment environment.  Those are semantic or statistical
premises and require their own evidence.

\section{Controlled CIFAR-10-C Dossier}
\label{fr:sec-cifar-dossier}

The CIFAR-10-C study is the largest current record of both directional branches.  Its value comes
from dense controlled variation and explicit outcome-disjoint reconstruction.  Its limitation is
equally important: the grid is fully opened, its cells are related constructions from one corpus,
and all candidates reuse one source checkpoint.  The correct description is therefore an
\emph{opened, dependent, constructed six-family diagnostic}.  It is neither prospective
confirmation nor a sample of independent deployments.

\subsection{Factorial construction and weighting}
\label{fr:sec-cifar-grid}

For each candidate and RNG seed, the grid crosses six corruption families---Gaussian noise,
defocus blur, fog, contrast, pixelate, and JPEG compression---with severities 1 and 5.  Each of
those 12 corruption--severity states is crossed with three batch sizes (200, 16, and 8), three
compositions (IID, imbalanced, and single-class), two update schedules (10 steps at learning rate
$10^{-3}$ and 50 steps at $2.5\times10^{-3}$), and two repeats.  The product is
\begin{equation}
  6\times2\times3\times3\times2\times2=432
  \quad\text{cells per RNG seed}.
\label{fr:eq-cifar-grid-size}
\end{equation}
Seeds 0 through 4 give 2,160 cells for each candidate.  These are five stream/run seeds conditional
on one archived ResNet-18 checkpoint.  They repeat stream construction and adapter randomness;
they are not five independently trained models.

Primary point summaries assign equal weight to all 2,160 cells.  The family sensitivity has a
different, explicitly hierarchical aggregation: it first averages seeds and condition cells within
each corruption family, then assigns equal weight to the six family means.  The two summaries
answer different questions.  A cell-weighted average emphasizes every factorial condition equally.
A family-weighted analysis treats the six named corruption families as its six resampling units.
Neither weighting scheme makes the families independent, and neither turns the opened grid into a
future-domain sample.

\subsection{Local ports and the composite operating point}
\label{fr:sec-cifar-operating-point}

Tent, EATA, and SAR in this grid are local protocol-matched ports, not untouched reproductions of
each paper's default implementation.  Candidate preview runs in training mode.  Target-batch
statistics replace stored BatchNorm running statistics, while gradient updates modify the selected
normalization affine parameters, including LayerNorm parameters where present.  Scoring retains
the train-mode BatchNorm behavior in evaluation chunks of 512 images.  Consequently a recorded
cell benefit combines at least two mechanisms: replacement of running statistics by
evaluation-batch statistics, and parameter updates from the adapter objective.

This composite is the candidate that was actually measured.  It would be inaccurate to attribute
the full cell delta to gradients alone, and it would be equally inaccurate to call the delta a
BatchNorm-only effect.  A BatchNorm-statistics-only control exists in code but was not executed on
this grid.  Candidate names identify the local ports; they do not erase the operating-point
details.  Any comparison to a published method must therefore carry the local-port qualifier.

The ImageNet-C SAR port makes the consequence concrete.  Its learning rate is
$4\times10^{-3}$, compared with the published $2.5\times10^{-4}$ setting, a factor of 16.  The port
also adapts the recorded BatchNorm affine parameters in an upper ResNet block excluded by the
published default, and it combines target-batch statistics with gradient updates.  No matched
control was run at the published SAR operating point.  The ImageNet-C record therefore describes
this aggressive local candidate, not SAR as published.

\subsection{Three-way cross-fitting as an information-flow contract}
\label{fr:sec-three-way-crossfit}

The current reconstruction does not reuse the scored outcome in either of the two artifacts that
determine its action.  Stable sample identifiers are sorted by a SHA-256-derived order and assigned
round-robin to five score folds.  For a given score fold $S_k$, the complement is reordered with a
fold-specific salt.  Approximately 30 percent of that complement, subject to the exact-rank minimum,
is assigned to residual calibration; the remainder is assigned to estimator fitting.  Writing
$T_k$ for the fit indices and $C_k$ for the calibration indices gives
\begin{equation}
  S_k\cap T_k=S_k\cap C_k=T_k\cap C_k=\varnothing.
\label{fr:eq-three-way-disjoint}
\end{equation}
The benefit regressor is fitted on $(Z_j,B_j)$ for $j\in T_k$.  Absolute residuals on $C_k$ produce
the exact-rank radius.  Only then are prediction, radius, and action computed for $i\in S_k$.

This design repairs a subtle leak in the older two-stage replay.  Excluding $B_i$ from the model
that predicts cell $i$ is not sufficient if $B_i$ can alter another cell's fitted prediction,
enter that cell's residual, and then flow back into the radius used for cell $i$.  The separate
calibration role blocks that indirect path.  The maintained implementation records hashes of the
score, fit, and calibration IDs for each fold together with hashes of normalized inputs and live
code dependencies.

The contract is tested at the whole-pipeline level.  The test changes one scored $B_i$, reruns all
fits and calibrations, and requires that the same cell's $\widehat\Delta_i$,
$\varepsilon_i$, and action are unchanged.  The test lives at
\path{tests/test_controlled_grid_crossfit.py}; it also demonstrates that the retired radius path
changes under this perturbation.  This is stronger than inspecting a function signature because
it follows the complete implemented dependency path.

The invariant proves only what it tests: scored-cell outcome disjointness.  It does not prove that
different cells are independent or exchangeable.  Score folds reuse overlapping complements.
Cells share the source checkpoint, corpus, corruption generator, and factorial design.  Exact-rank
coverage for a future unit would need a defensible calibration/deployment relation beyond this
software invariant.  The current inclusion rates are consequently descriptive audits of opened
cells, not verified coverage promises for a new corruption family.

\subsection{Six-family sensitivity and the multiplicity boundary}
\label{fr:sec-cifar-family-inference}

For the family analysis, define a paired gap as fixed-policy regret minus KGA regret, so positive
values favor KGA.  Tent's gap against always-adapt is $0.00606$ with ordinary, unadjusted nominal
95 percent family-bootstrap interval $[0.00209,0.00954]$.  Its gap against always-freeze is
$0.12202$ with interval $[0.06282,0.17757]$.  EATA's adapt-side interval is
$[-0.00135,0.00438]$ and therefore crosses zero, although its freeze-side interval is positive.
SAR's adapt-side gap is negative.  This is why Tent and EATA must not be described with the same
interval sentence: Tent has two favorable nominal intervals, whereas EATA does not.

The ordinary intervals and the retrospective reference tests are different inferential objects.
The retrospective family contains all six candidate-by-baseline contrasts; the exact-rank replay,
corruption-family bootstrap, sign-flip calculation, and Holm analysis were chosen after the grid
was open.  Under Holm adjustment over all six sign-flip values, Tent's adjusted values are
$0.140625$ against always-adapt and $0.09375$ against always-freeze.  EATA's are $0.3125$ and
$0.09375$.  No candidate clears both comparisons at family-wise level $0.05$.

A within-Tent two-comparison adjustment gives smaller post-hoc values, but it is not the declared
six-comparison family and cannot replace it.  Likewise, a positive unadjusted interval endpoint is
not a multiplicity-adjusted confidence statement.  The family count is small, the clusters share a
checkpoint, and coordinatewise sign-flip invariance is an unverified reference assumption.  The
proper conclusion is a favorable nominal Tent pattern accompanied by a failed retrospective Holm
gate, not confirmatory or cluster-robust evidence.

\section{Natural and Architecture-Panel Dossiers}
\label{fr:sec-natural-dossiers}

The natural and semantic-shift records test different failure modes from the constructed CIFAR
grid.  They do not form one pooled benchmark.  Some predominantly exercise retention, some expose
a candidate-specific adverse pattern, and one stops before the target stage.  Their scientific
value lies in showing where the gate's action branches were and were not exercised.

\subsection{CCT-20: a governed all-frozen outcome}
\label{fr:sec-cct-dossier}

CCT-20 crosses \CCTCheckpointCount{} independently trained source checkpoints with
\CCTLocationCount{} target camera locations, giving \CCTCellCount{} checkpoint--location cells.
The release macros record \CCTProbeImageCount{} probe images and
\CCTEvaluationImageCount{} evaluation images, \CCTTargetImageCount{} in total.  Its primary metric
is set-membership top-1 accuracy: a prediction is correct if it matches any distinct archived
category for the image.  It is not the official single-label leaderboard metric.

The Tent candidate uses the official implementation with one non-episodic Adam update per incoming
batch at learning rate $10^{-3}$.  Each batch is scored using pre-update logits, adaptation state
continues within one checkpoint--location cell, and state resets between cells.  This was a
cell-outcome-unopened, internally sealed execution: individual cell outcomes remained unopened until
one-shot scoring.  Aggregate target-label metadata had previously been inspected, so the study was
not globally label-unopened or publicly preregistered.

Two endpoints were locked.  The limited safe-utility endpoint uses nominal pointwise 95 percent
paired product-bootstrap lower endpoints.  With $L_a$ denoting the gap against always-adapt and
$L_f$ the gap against always-freeze, it requires
\begin{equation}
  L_a>0,\qquad L_f>\CCTSafeUtilityMargin.
\label{fr:eq-cct-safe-utility}
\end{equation}
The generated margin \CCTSafeUtilityMargin{} is on the accuracy-proportion scale.  The second
inequality is noninferiority to the frozen policy and can be satisfied by a controller that always
serves $f_0$.  The endpoint name does not turn this check into a population-safety theorem.

The strong-success rule is more demanding and is the appropriate test of bidirectional selective
routing.  It requires all 45 cells; nominal 97.5 percent Bonferroni intervals strictly above zero
against both fixed policies; both exact one-sided tests over nine location means to survive Holm
adjustment at $0.05$; ADAPT rate at least $0.10$; FREEZE rate at least $0.10$; and total commitment
at least $0.30$.  Failure of any component blocks that claim.  These thresholds were not added
after seeing the result.

The nominal pointwise 95 percent intervals in the CCT safe-utility table belong to the locked
safe-utility endpoint.  The nominal 97.5 percent Bonferroni intervals in the strong-success audit
belong to a separate, stricter two-comparison criterion; they are not alternate versions of the same
interval.

The realized action record was \CCTAdaptCount{} ADAPT, \CCTFreezeCount{} FREEZE, and
\CCTAbstainCount{} ABSTAIN.
Because both FREEZE and ABSTAIN serve $f_0$, every evaluated prediction came from the frozen model.
Tent helped in \CCTHelpfulCount{} cell, tied in \CCTZeroCount{}, and harmed in
\CCTHarmfulCount{} cells.  The gate issued FREEZE on the one helpful cell and on 43 harmful cells;
on the remaining harmful cell, the ABSTAIN action served the frozen model.  The observed
false-FREEZE frequency is
$\CCTFalseFreezeCount{}/\CCTFalseFreezeOverallDenominator{}$ over all cells and
$\CCTFalseFreezeCount{}/\CCTFalseFreezeConditionalDenominator{}$ conditional on a FREEZE action.  The
false-ADAPT frequency is $0/45$, while the conditional false-ADAPT rate is undefined because the
ADAPT count is zero.  The zero false-ADAPT numerator is therefore mechanical for that branch.

Against always-adapt, the baseline-minus-KGA point contrast is \CCTVsAdaptPoint{} with nominal
97.5 percent interval $[\CCTVsAdaptCILower,\CCTVsAdaptCIUpper]$, exact location sign-flip value
\CCTVsAdaptExactP{}, and Holm value \CCTVsAdaptHolmP{}.  Against always-freeze, the contrast and
interval endpoints are \CCTVsFreezePoint{}, $[\CCTVsFreezeCILower,\CCTVsFreezeCIUpper]$, with
Holm value \CCTVsFreezeHolmP{}.  The stored decision therefore matches always-freeze and avoids the
measured loss from always-adapt.  It passes the limited safe-utility endpoint but fails strict
improvement over freeze and fails the ADAPT-exposure threshold.  Its release verdict remains
\CCTVerdict: all-frozen fail-closed behavior, not selective-routing success.

The inferential assumptions remain visible.  The two-way bootstrap resamples checkpoint rows and
location columns; the exact test enumerates sign flips of nine location means.  Enumeration removes
Monte Carlo error, but not the need for coordinatewise sign-flip invariance.  The ridge estimator
was fitted using 10 rows from only two camera locations and calibrated on 45 rows aggregated to
nine cis-test locations.  The target locations are trans-test.  All 55 development cells were
harmful for Tent, so the estimator saw no helpful development example.  These facts explain why
the result is evidence of conservative retention rather than evidence that a helpful/harmful
boundary was learned across natural locations.

\subsection{ImageNet-R: architecture variation is not environment replication}
\label{fr:sec-imagenetr-dossier}

The current ImageNet-R authority contains four registered run seeds, ten backbone-specific
candidates, and 12 conditions per backbone and seed, for 480 cells.  The architecture-panel
aggregate is descriptive because each backbone is a separately calibrated candidate.  It is not
one router applied to ten independent target domains.

Before utility is considered, the aggregate action record is $A/F/U=179/34/267$: 179 ADAPT,
34 FREEZE, and 267 ABSTAIN actions.
There are six false ADAPT actions, giving $6/480$ on the all-cell denominator and $6/179$
conditional on ADAPT.  These are descriptive sums across separately calibrated backbone
candidates, not error estimates for one deployable policy.

The canonical aggregate gives KGA regret $0.0137$, always-adapt regret $0.0064$, and
always-freeze regret $0.0325$.  KGA has higher regret than always-adapt on eight of the ten
backbones.  Four of those eight have a 0 percent harmful-update base rate, leaving no candidate harm
for a router to avoid.  The two remaining backbone patterns are conservative ties.  On
\texttt{efficientnet\_b0}, KGA has lower regret than always-adapt but exactly matches
always-freeze at zero regret.  On \texttt{swin\_t}, it again has lower regret than always-adapt but
matches always-freeze at $0.00473958$.  Both rows make no ADAPT decisions.  Thus KGA has lower
regret than each fixed comparator on zero of ten backbones.

This breakdown is adverse but useful.  It shows that an aggregate gap can hide two structurally
different limits: when adaptation is uniformly helpful, abstention or freezing forgoes utility;
when adaptation is harmful, a conservative controller may merely recover the frozen policy.  The
panel does not support a universal ranking, but it directly constrains any claim that the current
gate transfers across architectures.

\subsection{ImageNet-C: candidate identity and an aggressive SAR port}
\label{fr:sec-imagenetc-dossier}

The current ImageNet-C authority has 27 conditions per run seed and five run seeds, or 135 cells
per candidate.  The candidate rows differ sharply.  The SAR row has 28/9/98 ADAPT/FREEZE/ABSTAIN
actions and records $0/135$ marginal false-adapt events, or $0/28$ conditional on ADAPT.  Its point regrets are $\ICkga$ for KGA,
$\ICadapt$ for always-adapt, and $\ICfreeze$ for always-freeze.  Descriptive run-seed intervals have
positive lower endpoints against the two fixed policies, but the run seeds do not record
independent checkpoint identities.  Those intervals cannot support an independent-checkpoint or
confidence-robust promotion.

The operating-point disclosure is inseparable from the numerical row.  As noted above, SAR uses
the $4\times10^{-3}$ local learning rate rather than the published $2.5\times10^{-4}$ setting,
includes a broader set of BatchNorm affine parameters, and combines evaluation-batch statistics
with updates.  No published-setting control exists.  The point record is therefore a diagnosis of
that candidate configuration only.  It is not evidence for a statement about SAR under its
published default.

\subsection{So2Sat-LCZ42: stopping is the result}
\label{fr:sec-so2sat-dossier}

The locked So2Sat-LCZ42 v1 study evaluated two candidates, Tent and SAR, at a development-only
feasibility stage.  Its current generated surface records nine development cities, five independent
source checkpoints, and 45 development cells per candidate, hence 90 opened development outcomes.
Tent showed both helpful and harmful cities, but neither candidate passed all nine predeclared
feasibility checks.  The locked decision was to stop before gate calibration.

The access boundary is more important than a target score because no target score exists.
Gate-calibration rows read before selection were zero.  Target-input count, target-pixel reads, and
target-label reads were all zero.  The target runner was not executed.  The v1 protocol also named
one action per city while the dormant runner would have produced city--checkpoint actions.  That
mismatch did not affect an action because the study stopped before any target action was created.

The development outcomes are now open.  Retuning on them cannot make the same panel fresh.  A v2
design resolves the action unit, but it remains a no-go without its own pre-calibration seal and
target authorization.  The defensible conclusion from v1 is a negative development-stage
eligibility result with no target access.  It is not a natural-shift routing result, and the absence
of a target table must not be filled with exploratory handoff notes.

\section{Negative Results That Define the Present Boundary}
\label{fr:sec-negative-results}

Negative results are not ancillary in a decision paper.  They identify which tempting shortcuts do
not support the desired action claim.  Three records are particularly important: the failure of a
development proxy for the population budget, the mismatch between exploratory search counts and a
frozen multiplicity family, and the historical status of local comparator ports.

\subsection{The development surrogate for \texorpdfstring{$\beta$}{beta} failed}
\label{fr:sec-beta-failure}

The population theorem assumes an external bound $|\gamma|\le\beta$ on a hidden
disagreement-conditional calibration residual.  The empirical radius $\varepsilon$ instead
describes prediction error for a measured cell target under a calibration protocol.  The two
quantities live in different layers.  Replacing $\beta$ by a fitted development residual would
need a justified bridge from source-like development cells to the target disagreement fibre.

That route was tested and failed.  The development-based estimate was between $1.4$ and
$50\times$ too small on real evaluation cells.  Across the reported configurations, 24--73 percent
of cells fell outside the declared class induced by that estimate, and commit error ranged from
0.4 to 16.6 percent.  In five of ten configurations the route returned no commitments on all 405
ImageNet-C cells.  The estimation route is therefore withdrawn; $\beta$ must be declared from
domain knowledge or another explicit structural assumption instead.

This negative result has a provenance limit of its own.  The maintained manuscript records the
exact result text, but the named raw beta-sweep artifact is not present in the canonical release
bundle.  The result is historically documented rather than currently regenerable from the
canonical panel.  That limitation must accompany the numbers.  It would be incorrect either to
hide the failed test or to present its historical record as a newly sealed experiment.  KGA does
not estimate $\beta$ and does not apply the population inequality numerically.

\subsection{Search history is not a multiplicity family}
\label{fr:sec-search-history}

One superseded repository census reported 1,387 determinations and 326 positives, or 23.5 percent.
A broader archived scan reported 1,427 determinations and 345 positives, or 24.2 percent.  These
counts overlap, and neither froze a reproducible family definition, exact file list, and repository
state before the outcomes were examined.  They are historical search summaries, not two
independent experiments and not a basis for calculating a release-wide adjusted $p$-value.

The current six-contrast CIFAR family is narrower and separately defined.  CCT-20 has its own
two-comparison inference family.  Archived comparator-port values have another historical scope.
Pooling these families after the fact would obscure rather than solve selection.  A future
confirmatory inventory must enumerate candidate--track arms, incomplete runs, exclusions,
calibration choices, and both fixed-policy contrasts before target outcomes are opened.

\section{From an Artifact to a Defensible Sentence}
\label{fr:sec-interpretation-workflow}

The same numerical row can support several sentences of very different strength.  The following
workflow makes the choice explicit.  It is deliberately conservative because the cost of an
ambiguous claim is larger than the cost of one additional qualifier.

\subsection{First identify the direction and target}
\label{fr:sec-interpret-target}

Write down the sign convention before comparing values.  In this report, measured benefit is
adapted score minus frozen score.  Regret is a nonnegative shortfall from the cell oracle.  Paired
policy gaps are fixed-policy regret minus KGA regret, so a positive gap favors KGA.  Changing the
sign convention between a table and its interval reverses the interpretation even when every
number is copied correctly.

Then name the target.  A cell-benefit outcome, a mean family gap, a location-cluster contrast, and
population risk reduction are not synonyms.  The interval must cover the same target that appears
in the action proposition.  If the target is $B_i=\Delta_i^{\mathrm{cell}}$, the resulting statement
is about the cell.  Moving to $\Delta$ needs an additional sampling and metric bridge.

\subsection{Then expose the branch that generated the utility}
\label{fr:sec-interpret-exposure}

Report $A/F/U$ before describing regret.  A policy can match always-freeze because it correctly
rejects many harmful candidates, because every interval is unresolved, or because its action path
is unavailable.  Those mechanisms have different deployment meaning even when the served
predictions are identical.  Similarly, low regret on an all-helpful panel can come from adapting on
every cell without demonstrating harm detection.

For each wrong-direction numerator, print the all-cell denominator and the action denominator.
When a denominator is zero, use ``undefined,'' not zero.  Preserve ties in the error definition:
strict directional semantics count ADAPT with $B_i\le0$ and FREEZE with $B_i\ge0$ as unsupported
directions, even though a tie has zero utility regret for either served model.

\subsection{Finally state design, dependence, and multiplicity}
\label{fr:sec-interpret-design}

Label access is not a binary adjective for an entire project.  A study can use labeled development
cells, keep target cell outcomes unopened until scoring, and still have inspected aggregate target
metadata during dataset selection.  State each boundary.  Likewise, distinguish one checkpoint
with five RNG seeds from five independent checkpoints, and distinguish ten candidate backbones
from ten environments for one router.

Every interval needs its resampling or calibration unit and its status.  A retrospective family
bootstrap over six corruptions is not an independently validated confidence interval.  A sign-flip
enumeration is exact relative to its reference distribution but does not verify the sign-symmetry
assumption.  Holm adjustment acts on the named collection of $p$-values; it neither adjusts ordinary
confidence intervals nor repairs an invalid marginal test.

\section{Interpretation Checklist}
\label{fr:sec-interpretation-checklist}

Before accepting an empirical sentence from this report, verify every item below.  A failed item
does not always invalidate the underlying artifact, but it narrows the admissible wording.

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Authority.} Is the number expanded from a current macro or copied from the exact
  generated surface linked to the canonical, CCT-20, or So2Sat authority?  Is its digest recorded?

  \item \textbf{Status.} Is the study current diagnostic, governed target execution, development
  stop, withheld row, invalid route, or historical provenance?  Does the sentence use that status
  rather than a more favorable one?

  \item \textbf{Candidate identity.} Are checkpoint, adapter port, learning rate, update schedule,
  adapted parameters, BatchNorm mode, and state-reset semantics the ones actually evaluated?

  \item \textbf{Evaluation unit.} Is one action attached to a condition cell,
  checkpoint--location pair, city--checkpoint pair, or another declared unit?  Are images within a
  cell being mistaken for independent gate decisions?

  \item \textbf{Metric.} Is the score accuracy, set-membership accuracy, balanced accuracy, or
  macro-F1?  Does the metric match the authority and the population claim, if any?

  \item \textbf{Action exposure.} Are ADAPT, FREEZE, and ABSTAIN counts visible?  Does a reported
  zero error come with nonzero exposure, or is it mechanical because the branch never ran?

  \item \textbf{Error denominator.} Is a false-action numerator divided by all cells or only by the
  corresponding action count?  Is an absent conditional denominator marked undefined?

  \item \textbf{Outcome flow.} Could the scored $B_i$ change its own prediction, radius, or action?
  For the controlled grid, does the whole-pipeline perturbation test still pass under the current
  implementation?

  \item \textbf{Dependence.} Do folds share a checkpoint, corpus, environment, or overlapping
  complement?  Are RNG seeds being presented accurately rather than relabeled as independent model
  replications?

  \item \textbf{Interval premise.} What target, level, rank rule, and resampling unit does the
  interval use?  Is exchangeability, sign symmetry, or transfer assumed rather than verified?

  \item \textbf{Multiplicity.} Which contrasts form the family, when was that family frozen, and
  what exactly was adjusted?  Are historical search counts or separate studies being inappropriately
  pooled?

  \item \textbf{Utility scope.} Does a regret or accuracy contrast describe only the recorded
  candidate and panel?  Is it kept secondary to directional correctness, exposure, and the protocol
  that made the action possible?

  \item \textbf{Population boundary.} Has a measured-cell statement been promoted to population
  benefit without a sampling-error bridge?  Has the empirical radius $\varepsilon$ been confused
  with the externally declared population residual budget $\beta$?

  \item \textbf{Negative evidence.} Are stopped studies, adverse architecture patterns, failed
  estimators, withheld metrics, and absent official comparisons retained rather than omitted from
  the claim family?

  \item \textbf{Reproduction boundary.} Do the available compact sources suffice to recompute the
  displayed result?  If raw data or checkpoints are external or historical identity is incomplete,
  does the sentence say so?
\end{enumerate}

Passing this sentence-level checklist does not certify a deployment.  It shows only that the claim
is traceable, action exposure is explicit, and remaining assumptions are visible.  That is the
reporting standard for directional action under uncertainty; accuracy and regret remain scoped
consequences.
