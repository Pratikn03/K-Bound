# Appendix (standalone): KGA's false-adapt certificate generalizes to a novel collapse-prone adapter

_Standalone robustness note. NOT part of the main K-Bound paper or PDF — kept here so it can be folded into a K-Bound appendix later if Pratik chooses. Primary evidence: `stage2_manifest.json` in this directory (protocol `elara_opt_stage2_protocol_v1`, CIFAR-10-C, dev/eval corruptions disjoint). Full ELARA-Opt run archived at `archive/elara_opt_archived_20260621.tar.gz`._

ELARA-Opt — a brand-new test-time-adaptation *optimizer* candidate built and stress-tested in a separate exploration, and **not** part of the K-Bound contribution — is itself a null result as an optimizer: on the held-out CIFAR-10-C stress suite it does not beat the strongest baseline (EATA), with a paired accuracy delta of −0.0083 (95% CI [−0.0121, −0.0047], excludes zero), so it is not proposed as a method. Its one transferable finding concerns K-Bound's safety machinery. KGA's false-adapt certificate was fit on a dev split whose corruptions are disjoint from evaluation and was never exposed to this candidate during its design; applied after the fact to ELARA-Opt on the stress suite — where always-adapting with this candidate is accuracy-reducing (false-adapt, benefit B < 0) on 78% of cells for the headline `elara_uniform` configuration and on roughly 70–97% of cells across its stress-arm family — the certificate correctly froze it, yielding a realized false-adapt rate of 0.000 against a budget of α = 0.10 while holding regret-to-oracle to 0.0073 and beating always-adapt's 0.0141. In short, the certificate gated a previously-unseen, collapse-prone adapter to *zero* harmful adaptations and stayed within budget — evidence that K-Bound's false-adapt guarantee generalizes to a novel dangerous adapter it was not designed around, not merely to the adapters used to derive it.

Source fields in `stage2_manifest.json`: `claim_audit.safety_KGA_ELARA_false_adapt_le_alpha` ("CONFIRMED (FA=0.000 vs alpha=0.1)"); `safety_frontier_EVAL."KGA(ELARA-Opt)"` (false_adapt 0.0, regret_KGA 0.0073, regret_always_adapt 0.0141, within_budget true); `candidate_quality_EVAL.*.always_adapt_false_adapt_B<0` (0.78 for `elara_uniform`, up to ~0.97 across the stress arms). The optimizer null result is recorded in `claim_audit.optimizer_value_beats_strongest_baseline` and `paired_bootstrap_EVAL`.
