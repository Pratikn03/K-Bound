On four one-sided natural tracks (Camelyon17,
iWildCam, RxRx1, Office-Home) the certificate ties the better fixed policy at zero observed false
adaptation, but \input{kbound_abstract_disclosures}. On PACS, ImageNet-R and CIFAR-10.1 the
