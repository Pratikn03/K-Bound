Test-time adaptation (TTA) can silently degrade a deployed model on unlabeled target data, and with no target labels the system cannot tell a helpful shift from a harmful one. We recast TTA as a safety decision made \emph{before} the update---whether to \emph{adapt}, \emph{freeze}, or \emph{abstain}---and give a label-free certificate for it. Defining adaptation benefit as $\Delta=R_T(f_0)-R_T(f_a)$, we record that over a declared calibration-drift class a strict adapt-or-freeze commitment is uniformly sound if and only if the label-free evidence margin exceeds the declared drift budget; inside the closed band, matched evidence or zero-benefit ambiguity leaves abstention as the only action that certifies a \emph{strict} benefit direction. The substantive half of that statement is a construction: two target laws with \emph{identical} label-free evidence laws and opposite nonzero benefits, from which we also obtain that any label-free audit of the drift budget itself is vacuous over the unrestricted class. Knowability-Guided Adaptation (\textsc{KGA}) is the separate finite-sample rule: it wraps any adapter (Tent, EATA, SAR), estimates $\Delta$ from label-free evidence, and commits only when a calibrated interval $\widehat\Delta\pm\varepsilon$ excludes zero, controlling the marginal false-adapt rate at a chosen level under interval coverage. The finite-sample rule does not numerically test the population frontier, and $\varepsilon$ is not an estimate of the budget. Our primary evidence is a safety guarantee, not an accuracy gain. On the four one-sided natural tracks with locked held-out artifacts---hospital (Camelyon17), wildlife-camera (iWildCam), laboratory-batch (RxRx1), and domain (Office-Home) shifts---KGA ties the better fixed policy at zero observed false adaptation; on PACS, ImageNet-R, and CIFAR-10.1 it is a conservative null or fails the declared transfer bar, and we report those outcomes rather than withdraw the tracks. The guarantee is exercised with real statistical power on exactly one track: the CIFAR-10-C stress grid, where $1{,}113$ \textsc{adapt} decisions produce zero false adaptations (Clopper--Pearson $95\%$ upper bound $0.0027$ on the conditional rate). K-Bound is a safety, validity, and abstention layer for TTA; it does not compete on accuracy and does not claim universal improvement.
