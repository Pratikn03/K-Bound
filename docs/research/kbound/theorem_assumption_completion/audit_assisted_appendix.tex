\section{Audit-Assisted Bounds for a Fixed Finite Frame}
\label{app:finite-frame-audit}

Additional labels permit a different, design-based question. Fix a finite frame
of $N$ observations, its frozen and adapted predictions, and its scoring rule.
Draw audit indices independently and uniformly with replacement after fixing
these objects. Conditional on the frame and its labels, any fixed correctness
indicator evaluated at the sampled indices is iid Bernoulli with its
finite-frame frequency as success probability. Dependence among the original
images does not invalidate this randomization argument; it does prevent
interpreting the frame as independent future environments. Correct labels,
faithful identities and the specified randomization remain premises.

\paragraph{Paired accuracy and the binary residual.}
Let $D$ be the known prediction-disagreement stratum and $d=|D|/N$.
When $d>0$, audit draws are independently uniform within $D$, with replacement.
Outside $D$ the paired correctness difference is zero. Within $D$, let
$p_+$ and $p_-$ denote helpful and harmful outcome frequencies. Two outward
binomial intervals with error budgets $\alpha/2$ give
\[
 \Delta_N\in d[L_+-U_-,\ U_+-L_-]
\]
with probability at least $1-\alpha$ over the audit draws. No independence
between the two intervals is required. This construction also permits
set-valued labels: both differing predictions can be correct, or both wrong.
It gives no macro-F1 guarantee.

For a single-label binary task, exactly one prediction is correct on $D$.
A single interval $[L,U]$ for adapted correctness gives
$\Delta_N\in2d[L-1/2,U-1/2]$. If $\bar s$ is the known fixed score mean on $D$,
then $\gamma_N\in[L-\bar s,U-\bar s]$, yielding
\[
 \widehat\beta=\max\{|L-\bar s|,|U-\bar s|\}.
\]
This is a labeled-audit confidence bound for the fixed frame, not a label-free
estimate of a uniform residual-class radius. When $D$ is empty,
$\Delta_N=0$ and the conditional residual is undefined.

\paragraph{An audit-assisted transport budget.}
For a fixed single-label class partition and bin map, independently uniform
sampling within each source and target frame gives binomial class-bin counts
conditional on their class counts. Allocate $\alpha/(2RK+K)$ to each source
and target class-bin interval and target-prior interval. The union bound gives
simultaneous containment. Given source bounds $[a^-_{ry},a^+_{ry}]$ and target
bounds $[b^-_{ry},b^+_{ry}]$, define
\[
 v_y=\min\left\{1,\frac12\sum_r
 \max\{|b^-_{ry}-a^+_{ry}|,|b^+_{ry}-a^-_{ry}|\}\right\}.
\]
Maximizing $\sum_y\pi_yv_y$ over the simplex intersected with the target-prior
boxes gives an upper confidence bound for the aggregate conditional-TV
budget. The implementation uses exact rational allocation in descending
$v_y$ order. Empty prior intersections return the vacuous bound one. An absent
source class has no uniquely defined conditional law; its bounds cover any
declared normalized extension. Multilabel memberships are not this partition.
The random budget's failure probability must be added to the subsequent
paired-transport certificate's budget. These derivations remain written
arguments; the Python audit implementation is not claimed as fully mechanized.

\paragraph{Retrospective CCT-20 check.}
We applied the paired-accuracy procedure to all 45 previously evaluated
checkpoint-by-location cells, preserving the original set-membership
correctness rule and excluding only the original adaptation-probe role.
Each cell used 512 independent draws with replacement from its disagreement
stratum and error budget $1/900$, giving family budget $0.05$. All draw
inventories were stored before the new scorer joined the already opened
historical labels. The 23,040 draws gave 16,428 distinct label uses when
summed across cells and 8,954 distinct image IDs overall. These are simulated
label lookups, not measured annotation labor. Shared images across checkpoints
are not independent deployments. Every full-frame benefit reconstructed the original scoring
authority and lay inside its audit interval. The audit issued 35 FREEZE and
10 ABSTAIN decisions, with no ADAPT decision among 44 harmful and one helpful
frame. Thus it did not demonstrate useful adaptation selection. This is a
retrospective label-budget simulation on fixed recorded predictions, not a
new prospective natural-shift study or validation of KGA's calibration transfer.
