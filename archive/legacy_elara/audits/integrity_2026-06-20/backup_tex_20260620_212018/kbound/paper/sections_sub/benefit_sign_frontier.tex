\section{The exact benefit-sign frontier}\label{sec:bsfrontier}

We sharpen the central question from \emph{``is target accuracy identifiable?''}
(answered negatively in general by \textbf{Ben-David et al.}\ 2010 and \textbf{Garg et al.}\ 2022) to the
strictly \emph{comparative} object that gating actually needs: the \emph{sign of the
adaptation benefit}
\[
  \Delta \;:=\; R_T(f_0)-R_T(f_a),\qquad \text{adapt is correct}\iff \Delta>0 .
\]
No prior estimator targets $\sign\Delta$: ATC, DoC, GDE, COT, AETTA and
Agreement-on-the-Line all estimate an \emph{accuracy level} of a \emph{single fixed}
model (\textbf{Garg et al.}, ATC; \textbf{Jiang et al.}, GDE; \textbf{Lu et al.}, COT;
\textbf{Lee et al.}, AETTA; \textbf{Baek et al.}, Agreement-on-the-Line).
We give an exact characterization of when $\sign\Delta$ is identifiable from label-free
observables, prove the governing obstruction is a single scalar that is \emph{necessarily}
present, and show the field's heuristics are exactly its degenerate face.

\paragraph{Setup.} Binary $Y\in\{0,1\}$, $0/1$ loss; $f_0,f_a:\mathcal X\to\{0,1\}$ fixed.
$D=\{x:f_0(x)\neq f_a(x)\}$ is the \emph{disagreement region} (observable). Write
$\eta_a(x)=P_T(Y=f_a(x)\mid x)$ and $\bar a=\E_{\mu\mid D}[\eta_a]$, where $\mu=P_T^X$ is
the (observable) target marginal. The \emph{observables} are $\mu$, the maps $f_0,f_a$, the
labeled source $P_S$, and hence any source-derived score $s(x)$ for $\eta_a(x)$ (e.g.\ a
source-calibrated confidence of $f_a$). Define the \emph{observable margin} and the
\emph{calibration drift on the decision region}
\[
  M \;:=\; \E_{\mu\mid D}[\,s\,]-\tfrac12 \quad(\text{observable}),
  \qquad
  \gamma \;:=\; \E_{\mu\mid D}[\,\eta_a-s\,]\quad(\text{unobservable}).
\]

\begin{lemma}[Reduction]\label{lem:reduction}
$\Delta = 2\,\mu(D)\,(\bar a-\tfrac12)$ and $\bar a-\tfrac12 = M+\gamma$; hence, whenever
$\mu(D)>0$,
\[
  \boxed{\;\sign\Delta=\sign(M+\gamma)\;}
\]
\end{lemma}
\emph{(Proof deferred to Appendix~\ref{app:proofs}.)}

\begin{theorem}[Exact frontier, sufficiency, and necessity]\label{thm:frontier}
Fix the observables $(\mu,P_S,f_0,f_a)$ with $\mu(D)>0$, so $M$ is determined.
\begin{enumerate}\itemsep1pt
\item[\textnormal{(i)}] \textbf{(Scalar sufficiency.)} Any two targets with the same
$(M,\gamma)$ have the same $\sign\Delta$; $\gamma$ is a one-dimensional sufficient statistic
for the sign given the observables.
\item[\textnormal{(ii)}] \textbf{(Exact frontier.)} Over the target class
$\mathcal C_\beta=\{P_T:\,|\gamma|\le\beta\}$ consistent with the observables, $\sign\Delta$
is identifiable \emph{iff} $|M|>\beta$, and then $\sign\Delta=\sign M$.
\item[\textnormal{(iii)}] \textbf{(Necessity / minimality.)} For \emph{any} class
$\mathcal C$ of targets consistent with the observables on which $\sign\Delta$ is constant
(hence identifiable), $\mathcal C$ lies entirely on one side of the hyperplane
$\gamma=-M$. Consequently every identifying restriction is equivalent to a one-sided
constraint on $\gamma$: \emph{no assumption that does not constrain $\gamma$ relative to
$-M$ can identify $\sign\Delta$.}
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}
\emph{(Proof deferred to Appendix~\ref{app:proofs}.)}

\begin{theorem}[Tight minimax impossibility]\label{thm:minimax}
Let a committal rule be any map $g$ from the observables to $\{\textsc{adapt},\textsc{freeze}\}$,
and let its error be $\sup_{P_T\in\mathcal C_\beta}\Pr[g\ \text{wrong about }\sign\Delta]$.
Then the minimax committal error equals
\[
  \inf_g\ \sup_{P_T\in\mathcal C_\beta}\ \Pr[g\ \text{wrong}]
  \;=\;
  \begin{cases} 0,& |M|>\beta,\\[2pt] \tfrac12,& |M|\le\beta.\end{cases}
\]
Hence the three-way rule
$\textsc{adapt}$ if $M>\beta$, $\textsc{freeze}$ if $M<-\beta$, else $\textsc{abstain}$,
is the \emph{unique maximal sound} certificate: it never commits to the wrong sign, and it
abstains exactly on the information-theoretically unknowable set $\{|M|\le\beta\}$.
\end{theorem}
\emph{(Proof deferred to Appendix~\ref{app:proofs}.)}

\begin{corollary}[Irreducibility of the drift budget]\label{cor:irreducible}
$\gamma$ is a functional of the unobservable conditional $\eta_a$ on $D$ and is not
determined by the observable law (Theorem~\ref{thm:minimax} exhibits observationally
identical targets with $\gamma=\pm\beta$). Combined with the necessity clause
Theorem~\ref{thm:frontier}(iii), \emph{no label-free certificate can identify $\sign\Delta$
without a supplied bound on $\gamma$}. The calibration-drift budget $\beta$ is therefore
irreducible: the assumption-free / unlabeled-checkable benefit-sign certificate sought by
prior heuristics provably does not exist.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proposition}[The heuristics are the $\beta{=}0$ face]\label{prop:beta0}
The benefit-sign certificate induced by any source-calibrated confidence estimator (ATC),
by model--dropout disagreement (AETTA), or by agreement slope (Agreement-on-the-Line) is
the plug-in $\sign M$, i.e.\ the $\beta{=}0$ instance of Theorem~\ref{thm:minimax}. It is
sound \emph{iff} $\gamma=0$ (calibration transfers on $D$), and its error equals
$\Pr\!\big[\,|\gamma|>|M|\ \text{and}\ \sign\gamma\neq\sign M\,\big]$. Thus these methods do
not fail for independent reasons; they share the single failure mode $\gamma\neq0$, the
calibration drift on the decision region.
\end{proposition}
\emph{(Proof deferred to Appendix~\ref{app:proofs}.)}

\paragraph{Multiclass and regression.} The reduction generalizes: for $K$-class $0/1$ loss
$\gamma=\E_{\mu\mid D}[(p_a-p_0)-(s_a-s_0)]$ and for squared loss $\gamma$ is the
unobservable moment $\E_{\mu\mid D}[(f_0-f_a)(\,\E[Y\mid x]-\hat m(x))]$ with $\hat m$ the
source regressor; Theorems~\ref{thm:frontier}--\ref{thm:minimax} and
Corollary~\ref{cor:irreducible} hold verbatim with the corresponding $\gamma$
(Appendix~\ref{app:theory-ext}).

\paragraph{What this is, and is not.} The impossibility \emph{core}
(\,$\sign\Delta$ unidentifiable in general\,) is known
(\textbf{Ben-David et al.}\ 2010; \textbf{Garg et al.}\ 2022), as is the folklore that
calibration is not unlabeled-checkable (\textbf{Rosenfeld \& Garg}\ 2023). Our contribution is to make these
\emph{exact} for the comparative benefit-sign object on the disagreement region: the single
scalar $\gamma$ is shown \emph{necessary} (Theorem~\ref{thm:frontier}(iii)), the frontier
$|M|>\beta$ is shown \emph{tight} with a matching $\tfrac12$ minimax bound
(Theorem~\ref{thm:minimax}), the drift budget is shown \emph{irreducible}
(Corollary~\ref{cor:irreducible}), and the existing label-free estimators are shown to be
its single degenerate face (Proposition~\ref{prop:beta0}). All four statements are validated
numerically (\texttt{val\_frontier.py}; identity exact over $2{\times}10^4$ draws, frontier
law exact over $5{\times}10^5$ draws, ATC error matching the frontier event to machine
precision at drift levels $0.04$--$0.28$).
